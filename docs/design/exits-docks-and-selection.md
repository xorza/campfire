# Exits, docks and selection

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish selects units as in the release game, by a drag that takes no building, by shift to add or remove, and by a double click that takes every unit of a type on the screen; sees each vehicle drive out of its War Factory's door and each soldier out of its barracks; sees supply trucks dock at a supply dock's and a center's own points; puts soldiers into an Overlord's and a Helix's battle bunker and has them leave a building it sells; hears every weapon fire, the Inferno Cannon's among them, which fires at nothing within its minimum range; and plays with no crash of an order to enter or use. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Exits, docks and selection; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `02-engine-core.md` for the selection, `04-capabilities/production.md` for exits and docks, `04-capabilities/interaction.md` for bunkers, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's exits, docks, bunkers and selection](../research/zero-hour-exits-and-selection.md).

## Decisions

- **A drag selects no building.** A drag selects only the player's units that walk, as `SelectionTranslator` refuses a structure to a drag so that one command panel serves the selection, and a unit that does not walk is the engine's building; a click still selects a building. Refused: today's drag of every unit, buildings among them; an engine tag of a structure, which a game's data would have to give each building again.
- **Shift adds, a double click takes a kind.** With the add key held, shift by default, a click or a drag adds to the selection, and a click on units all selected removes them; a double click on a unit selects every unit of its type the screen shows, and every one on the map with the map key, alt by default, keeping the selection with the add key held, as `SelectionTranslator::onMouseLeftDoubleClick` does. The keys are bindings of the input map. Refused: a selection of a kind by type and its variations apart, which the release game does not make.
- **A trained unit drives out of its producer.** Production makes a unit at its producer's exit point, inside the producer's body when the point lies there, and moves it out by a forced move of exit to the producer's natural rally point, which crosses its producer's body alone, then routes it to the player's rally point, as `DefaultProductionExitUpdate` and `aiFollowExitProductionPath`, which ignores the factory, send it. Refused: today's nearest open cell, which puts it beside the body on any side; a dash, which every static body stops; a route that ignores one body, which the pathing grid does not know.
- **A dock has its point.** A node and a drop-off may name `dock`, a point in their own frame a gatherer docks at, within its gather's reach, for its gather or its delivery, as `DockUpdate` docks a gatherer at `DockAction`; the import reads the bone, which America's supply center and the supply warehouse give, and the others dock at their body, as Zero Hour's dock of no bone does at its own place. Refused: today's dock 10 m from any side of the box (09-zero-hour.md, A gatherer docks as a dozer builds), which stays for a dock of no point; the drive from `DockStart` to `DockEnd` and the waiting at `DockWaiting<n>`, which would make the loop hold a path and a queue of places, a loss for now.
- **A bunker is an entry a tag opens.** A container's `enter` may name `open`, the tags its unit holds while it takes riders; the Overlord's entry is its battle bunker's, of 5 infantry that fire, which the import finds as the transport its upgrade's creation list makes inside it (`ContainInsideSourceObject`), opened by the tag that upgrade gives, its first trigger's name suffixed `created`; the Helix's, of 5 infantry and vehicles that do not fire, is always open, as its `HelixContain` is a transport of its own. Refused: Zero Hour's portable structure, a container riding the container that passes it the queries for riders (`OverlordContain`), which would make a rider a container and every rider query a redirect; the Helix's riders firing from its bunker, which would make a rider's fire follow its container's tags after it entered, a loss with the Gattling cannon's and the propaganda tower's riders.
- **A look follows its converted action.** The import keys each weapon's fire effect and fire sound by each action it converts the weapon to, its `_starting` and `_upgraded` ones among them. Refused: today's key by the weapon's own name, under which 39 gated weapons play nothing.
- **A transport may assault.** A container's `enter` may name `assault`, a share of a life: an attack or an attack-move ordered to it lets out each rider above that share of its life to fight, as `AssaultTransportAIUpdate` deploys the Troop Crawler's troops, so a bot's crawler fights by its Red Guards as a player's does; the import reads `MembersGetHealedAtLifeRatio`. Refused: a bot that evacuates its containers, which a player's crawler would not do; a base start of empty containers, which the release game makes full; the riders kept inside, a loss. The crawler's call of its hurt riders back to heal, and its call of all back on an idle order, stay losses.
- **A sold container lets its riders out.** A container whose sale starts orders its riders out, and one that leaves the match by a despawn takes its riders with it, as `OpenContain::onSelling` and `onDelete` do; the sale's start reaches interaction through a contract below both. Refused: riders left out of the world, today's.
- **A base start's room connects.** A mode's room near a point is a cell clear of every body that connects, room by room along the search's grid, to the edge of its search, so no body the call placed encloses a unit it places; with none that connects, any room serves. Refused: a room of any clear cell, today's, under which America's first Ranger on Tournament Desert stands enclosed; the walkable region of the pathing grid, which holds none of the bodies the call spawned.
- **What the slice leaves.** A dock's boxes drawn going, a portable structure's own life and model, the Overlord's Gattling cannon and propaganda tower as riders, and the exit paths of a container's riders: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The client's tests select by a drag that skips a building, add and remove by the add key, and select a type on the screen and on the map by a double click.
- The production tests make a unit inside its producer's body and move it out to its natural rally point, then its rally point; dock a gatherer at a node's and a drop-off's points and hold a second at a waiting point.
- The interaction tests refuse a rider to a closed entry and take it once the tag opens it, let a rider fire only with the fire tag, let a sold container's riders out, and despawn a despawned container's riders with it.
- The mode's tests place a base start's units only in cells that connect to the map's ground.
- The import's tests give every gated weapon its look, the War Factory and the barracks their exit points, the supply dock and the center their dock points, and the Overlord and the Helix their entries.
- The Zero Hour check pins the exits, the docks and the bunkers, plays a soldier entering an Overlord's bunker and firing from it, and its bots' match still ends.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert as China against the America builder bot selects as the decisions say, sees vehicles leave the War Factory's door, sees trucks dock at the docks' points, puts soldiers in a bunker, hears the Inferno Cannon, and plays to the end with no crash.
