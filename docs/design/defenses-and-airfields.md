# Defenses and airfields

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish attacks an enemy Fire Base, Stinger Site and Battle Bus, sees their health fall and kills them, the bus first stopped as the bunker of its second life; trains no more jets at an airfield than it has places; sees a factory's and an airfield's doors open as each unit comes out, an American jet rolling out of its hangar; and sees each jet gather speed down its runway, lift off as it nears the runway's end and climb out past it, and come back by the airfield's approach. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Defenses and airfields; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/combat.md` for a second life, `04-capabilities/production.md` for places and doors, `04-capabilities/airfields.md` for the takeoff and the approach, `02-engine-core.md` for the client's doors, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's bodies](../research/zero-hour-bodies.md) and [Zero Hour's jets](../research/zero-hour-jets.md).

## Decisions

- **A hive is a structure.** The import gives an object of a `HiveStructureBody` its life as it gives a `StructureBody`'s, as the hive is a structure body that also passes some damage to its spawns; the passing stays a loss (09-zero-hour.md, Spawns). Refused: a hive of no life, today's, which no weapon targets and nothing kills.
- **A type may live twice.** A unit type's `second_life = { life, modifier }`, combat's: the first damage that would kill its unit, of a kind other than one no armor reduces, leaves it the least life, then its life maximum becomes `life`, its life whole, and it holds `modifier`, by which a mode gives it its second armor and stops its walk, as `UndeadBody::startSecondLife` sets `SecondLifeMaxHealth`, heals fully and sets `ARMORSET_SECOND_LIFE`; its second life is state; the raise of its maximum comes with the tick's stats, so a second damage in the tick its second life starts takes the first life's maximum, and the rest of the tick's damage past it kills it, where Zero Hour's has its second maximum at once. Refused: a death and the spawn of a bunker's type, which loses the unit's id, its riders and its experience; a hook of each damage in Rhai, which every hit of a battle would call.
- **A train at a place reserves it as it queues.** A train `at_place` joins its producer's queue only while the producer's places outnumber the units that hold them and the trains at a place it has queued, and its button is dimmed while none is free, as `reserveDoorForExit` reserves a place as the train queues. This changes `04-capabilities/production.md`'s A jet holds a place, which refused the reservation as the queue's due time reads no book: the start's check reads the producer's places, and the queue's due time stays. Refused: the wait at the queue's end, today's, under which an airfield of 4 places queues a fifth jet.
- **A producer's door opens before its unit leaves.** A producer's `doors = { count, opening_ms, open_ms, closing_ms }`, production's: a train whose time ended makes its unit once its door has opened, holds the door open its open time from the last unit through it, then closes it; a train at a place takes the door of its place, which stays open while its place holds a unit, as `setHoldDoorOpen` holds it, and opens for a jet of a gone airfield that takes the place. Each door's phase and the tick it began are the producer's state, which every client that sees the producer receives, so its look draws `door_<n>_opening`, `door_<n>_waiting_open` and `door_<n>_closing`, as `ProductionUpdate::update` holds each door's frames. Refused: doors the client draws alone from an event of each train's end, which a client that joins later misses, and which leaves units leaving a door's opening time sooner than the release game's; today's closed doors, a loss (09-zero-hour.md, Doors).
- **A hangar is a jet's start.** A producer's place may name its `exit`, production's, the point its unit is made at, from which an airfield taxis it to its place, as `exitObjectViaDoor` makes each jet in its place's hangar and sends it along its exit path to its parking space; the import gives America's places their hangars, and China's, which park in their hangars, none. Refused: a hangar of the airfield's own data, which production, which makes the unit, does not read.
- **A jet takes off as a jet.** The sortie rolls its jet from a stop, gaining its `acceleration` a tick to its speed, toward the airfield's `approach = { height, past }`, `past` a share of the runway's length past its end, and lifts it toward the approach's height by its climb times `(1 − d / L)²`, `d` its distance to the runway's end and `L` the runway's length, through a flight goal of a height and a climb that the sortie sets each tick, as `JetTakeoffOrLandingState` sets its lift; it lets the runway go once past the end, flies from the approach, and lands from it, gliding at its least speed from the height it holds there to the runway's end. Refused: a winged mover in navigation, as Zero Hour's `moveTowardsPositionWings` is its general mover of forces, mass and damping, which a jet needs only for this path; today's roll at its full speed and climb past the runway's end.
- **What the slice leaves.** A hive's damage passed to its spawns, the Battle Bus's slow death between its lives, a highlander's, an immortal's and an inactive body, and a carrier's deck: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The import's tests give a Fire Base and a Stinger Site their life, the Battle Bus its second life and its modifier, the American airfield its hangars, and both airfields and the War Factory their doors.
- The combat tests kill a unit of a second life once into it, at the least life and then its second maximum, refuse the second life to a kind no armor reduces, and keep it over a save and a restore; the production tests refuse a fifth jet at an airfield of 4 places and take it once a jet dies, open a door before its unit leaves and close it after its open time, and keep a door's phase over a save and a restore; the airfields tests roll a jet from a stop, lift it by the ratio, and fly it to the exit.
- The Zero Hour check pins the bodies, the doors and the hangars, plays a Fire Base and a Battle Bus killed, a fifth jet refused, and a jet's takeoff, and its bots' match still ends.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert kills an enemy Fire Base and a Battle Bus, sees an airfield refuse a fifth jet, sees the doors open as units come out, and sees jets take off and land as the release game's.
