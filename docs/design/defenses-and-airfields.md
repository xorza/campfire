# Defenses and airfields

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish attacks an enemy Fire Base, Stinger Site and Battle Bus, sees their health fall and kills them, the bus first stopped as the bunker of its second life; trains no more jets at an airfield than it has places; sees a factory's and an airfield's doors open as each unit comes out, a Chinese jet rolling out of its hangar; and sees each jet gather speed down its runway, lift off as it nears the runway's end and climb out past it, and come back by the airfield's approach. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Defenses and airfields; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/combat.md` for a second life, `04-capabilities/production.md` for places and doors, `04-capabilities/airfields.md` for the takeoff and the approach, `02-engine-core.md` for the client's doors, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's bodies](../research/zero-hour-bodies.md) and [Zero Hour's jets](../research/zero-hour-jets.md).

## Decisions

- **A hive is a structure.** The import gives an object of a `HiveStructureBody` its life as it gives a `StructureBody`'s, as the hive is a structure body that also passes some damage to its spawns; the passing stays a loss (09-zero-hour.md, Spawns). Refused: a hive of no life, today's, which no weapon targets and nothing kills.
- **A type may live twice.** A unit type's `second_life = { life, modifier }`, combat's: the first damage that would kill its unit, of a kind other than one no armor reduces, leaves it the least life, then its life maximum becomes `life`, its life whole, and it holds `modifier`, by which a mode gives it its second armor and stops its walk, as `UndeadBody::startSecondLife` sets `SecondLifeMaxHealth`, heals fully and sets `ARMORSET_SECOND_LIFE`; its second life is state. Refused: a death and the spawn of a bunker's type, which loses the unit's id, its riders and its experience; a hook of each damage in Rhai, which every hit of a battle would call.
- **A train at a place reserves it as it queues.** A train `at_place` joins its producer's queue only while the producer's places outnumber the units that hold them and the trains at a place it has queued, and its button is dimmed while none is free, as `reserveDoorForExit` reserves a place as the train queues. This changes `04-capabilities/production.md`'s A jet holds a place, which refused the reservation as the queue's due time reads no book: the start's check reads the producer's places, and the queue's due time stays. Refused: the wait at the queue's end, today's, under which an airfield of 4 places queues a fifth jet.
- **A producer's door opens before its unit leaves.** A producer's `doors = { count, opening_ms, open_ms, closing_ms }`, production's: a train whose time ended makes its unit once its door has opened, holds the door open its open time from the last unit through it, then closes it; a train at a place takes the door of its place. Each door's phase and the tick it began are the producer's state, which every client that sees the producer receives, so its look draws `door_<n>_opening`, `door_<n>_waiting_open` and `door_<n>_closing`, as `ProductionUpdate::update` holds each door's frames. Refused: doors the client draws alone from an event of each train's end, which a client that joins later misses, and which leaves units leaving a door's opening time sooner than the release game's; today's closed doors, a loss (09-zero-hour.md, Doors).
- **A hangar is a jet's start.** An airfield of `hangars` gives each place a hangar point, where its jet is made, and from which it taxis to its place, as `ParkInHangars` makes China's jets in their hangars.
- **A jet takes off as a jet.** A jet rolls from its runway's start gathering speed by its acceleration, lifts by its climb times `(1 − d / L)²`, `d` its distance to the runway's end and `L` the runway's length, and flies to the runway's end raised to the airfield's approach height and on to its exit, three quarters of the runway's length past the end, as `JetTakeoffOrLandingState` does; it lands from the approach, at the same height and distance, at its least speed. The investigation of this step reads `Locomotor::moveTowardsPositionWings` and compares the client's takeoff with the release game's before the design of the mover is proposed. Refused: today's roll at its full speed and climb past the runway's end.
- **What the slice leaves.** A hive's damage passed to its spawns, the Battle Bus's slow death between its lives, a highlander's, an immortal's and an inactive body, a door held open by a unit that waits in it, and a carrier's deck: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The import's tests give a Fire Base and a Stinger Site their life, the Battle Bus its second life and its modifier, the Chinese airfield its hangars, and both airfields and the War Factory their doors.
- The combat tests kill a unit of a second life once into it, at the least life and then its second maximum, refuse the second life to a kind no armor reduces, and keep it over a save and a restore; the production tests refuse a fifth jet at an airfield of 4 places and take it once a jet dies, open a door before its unit leaves and close it after its open time, and keep a door's phase over a save and a restore; the airfields tests roll a jet from a stop, lift it by the ratio, and fly it to the exit.
- The Zero Hour check pins the bodies, the doors and the hangars, plays a Fire Base and a Battle Bus killed, a fifth jet refused, and a jet's takeoff, and its bots' match still ends.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert kills an enemy Fire Base and a Battle Bus, sees an airfield refuse a fifth jet, sees the doors open as units come out, and sees jets take off and land as the release game's.
