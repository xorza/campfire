# Jets

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish on Tournament Desert trains a Raptor or a MiG at an airfield, which waits at its parking place, taxis to its runway, takes off down it, strikes a target in passes, comes back to land and reload, and falls when shot down. Its plan is `PLAN.md`'s Jets; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/navigation.md` for wings, `04-capabilities/combat.md` for the aim and the clip, a new `04-capabilities/airfields.md` for the airfield, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's jets](../research/zero-hour-jets.md).

## Decisions

- **A jet flies on wings.** A unit type's `flight` may give `wings`, its least speed and its circling radius: a winged flier in the air never stands, moving along its facing each tick at its speed and turning toward its goal at its turn rate as it moves, and with no goal it circles the place it holds at its circling radius, as Zero Hour's `WINGS` locomotor keeps its `MinSpeed` and circles its held place (`maintainCurrentPositionWings`). Refused: Zero Hour's acceleration, lift and braking, a physics no other flier has, as Helicopters refused it (helicopters.md, A flier holds a height); a jet that stops in the air as a helicopter does.
- **A jet aims by flying.** A winged attacker never turns in place: with its target in its reach it keeps the target as its goal and starts its attack within `aim_deg` of the target's bearing, passes it and comes round for the next, as `AIAttackAimAtTargetState` keeps a unit of non-zero `MinSpeed` flying at its victim and OpenRA's fixed-wing aircraft attacks in runs. Refused: a turn in place, which no wing makes.
- **A clip may reload at home.** A clip's `reload` of `home` refills only while its unit stands at its place at its airfield, over its reload time times the share of the clip it lacks, its shots back as the time goes, as `JetOrHeliReloadAmmoState` refills a clip. Refused: a reload in the air by time, which keeps a jet out for ever.
- **An airfield is a capability.** The new capability `airfields` holds an airfield's places and runways, a jet's place, its taxi, takeoff, landing, reload, heal and return, as Zero Hour's `ParkingPlaceBehavior` and `JetAIUpdate` hold them apart from production and the locomotor. Refused: a part of production, which makes units and does not land them; a part of navigation, which walks units and does not refill their clips.
- **A jet holds a place.** An airfield trains a unit of its `sortie` only while one of its places is free, holds one for it as the train is queued, makes it at that place, or at the place's hangar for an airfield that parks in hangars, and keeps the place its own while it lives, as `ParkingPlaceBehavior::hasAvailableSpaceFor` and `reserveDoorForExit` do. Refused: a train that waits for nothing and a jet with no place to land.
- **A jet takes off and lands on its runway.** A jet waits for its column's runway, taxis to its prep point, waits its takeoff pause, rolls from the runway's start to its end and climbs; to land it waits for the runway, flies to an approach point beyond the runway's end at the airfield's approach height, lands from the end to the start and taxis to its place; one jet holds a runway at a time, as Zero Hour's jets do (decided 2026-10-27). Refused: OpenRA's vertical takeoff and landing on the place, under which a jet lifts off as a helicopter.
- **A jet goes home.** A jet whose every clip that reloads at home is empty, or that stands idle in the air for its sortie's idle time, returns to its airfield, lands, reloads and heals at its airfield's heal a second, as `JetAIUpdate::update` returns it; one whose airfield is gone takes the nearest airfield of its player with a free place, and with none circles where its airfield stood, losing its sortie's share of its life a second, as `JetOrHeliCirclingDeadAirfieldState` does. Refused: OpenRA's flight idle with no damage, under which a jet with no airfield circles for ever.
- **The import flies a winged locomotor.** An object whose normal locomotor flies on `AIR` with the `WINGS` appearance and whose AI is `JetAIUpdate` becomes a flier of wings on the rules package's `air` layer, of its locomotor's speed, turn rate, `MinSpeed`, `CirclingRadius`, `PreferredHeight` and `AirborneTargetingHeight`, its climb from its `Lift` as a hover's, and a `sortie` of its `TakeoffPause`, `ReturnToBaseIdleTime` and `OutOfAmmoDamagePerSecond`; its taxi locomotor's speed is its speed on the ground; an airfield's `ParkingPlaceBehavior` becomes its `airfield`, of its places' and runways' bones, its `ApproachHeight` and its `HealAmountPerSecond`; a weapon of `AutoReloadsClip = RETURN_TO_BASE` reloads at home.
- **What the slice leaves.** The afterburners; countermeasures; the lock-on cursor; the attack's sneaky offset; the pilot's ejection; a carrier's deck; the Aurora's bomb and the Stealth Fighter's stealth, as their weapons and stealth do not convert; the Comanche's landing at its airfield; a jet's pitch and roll; each counted as a loss.

## Checks

- Navigation's tests fly a winged flier past its goal and back round it at its speed and turn rate, never standing in the air, and circle the place it holds with no goal.
- Combat's tests start a winged attacker's attack only within its aim of its target's bearing, with no turn in place, and refill a clip that reloads at home only at its place, in proportion to its time.
- The airfields' tests train a jet only into a free place, take it off and land it along its runway's points one jet a runway, send it home empty or idle, reload and heal it there, and move it to another airfield or circle it as its own dies.
- The import's tests convert a winged locomotor, a jet's sortie, an airfield's places and runways, and a clip that returns to base, and count each loss.
- The Zero Hour check's play trains a Raptor, takes it off, strikes a target with it, lands and reloads it, its ticks pinned.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert sees a jet taxi, take off down its runway, strike in passes, land and reload, and fall when shot down.
