# Combat

## Mechanism

- **Life, weapons and one damage pass.** `combat` gives units a life pool and weapons, which are actions of the `attack` kind ([Actions](actions.md)), and runs damage, heals, deaths and kill credit; who may hit whom comes from the teams' relations.
- **A type can die exactly when it has life.** A unit type has `combat` exactly when it has the life pool, so every unit that can reach zero life can die.
- **What a death does is the type's.** A death reads `on_death` through its unit's type, as a hit reads its armor, and a unit dies when its life reaches zero, so no part of a unit repeats either. Refused: `on_death` copied onto each unit as state, which a snapshot and the hash carried for every unit, and a marker of the units that can die that `Life` requires, which says only that the unit has life, as the death's query reads it already.

## Weapons

- **The first weapon that selects the target.** An attack names its target by stable id and uses the first weapon whose filter selects it; an order no weapon selects is ignored.
- **A weapon may give its own damage and period.** An attack takes `amount` in place of its attacker's damage stat, and `period_ms` in place of its rate stat, one of each pair, as StarCraft II holds a weapon's damage and period on the weapon; a MOBA keeps its stats, which items and levels raise.
- **Clips.** A weapon may fire in clips: after the shot that empties its clip it is ready its reload after that shot, in place of its period, as Zero Hour's `Weapon::reloadWithBonus` sets the next shot; a stop, a move or a new target keeps the count.
- **Range counts only at the start.** A delivery happens unless its target died, despawned or became no target, so an attack that starts in range lands.
- **One attack hits one unit,** of a weapon of no splash: its roll, leech and on-hit effects count once.
- **An attack may splash.** An `attack` of a projectile takes `splash = { radius, affects, spares_source }`: where its projectile ends it deals its damage to each living unit of the relations `affects` names whose volume lies within `radius`, its source too unless it spares it, and its hit deals nothing more, as OpenRA's weapon holds a `SpreadDamageWarhead` beside its projectile and 0 A.D.'s attack a `Splash`; data of the weapon, no script, and the damage its source's, as Zero Hour's `dealDamageInternal` counts it, where an area its `on_hit` launches would need a script for every splash.
- **A splash may have an outer ring.** A splash's `outer = { radius, amount }` deals its amount to each unit its `affects` selects whose volume lies within its radius and beyond the splash's own, as Zero Hour's `SecondaryDamage` within `SecondaryDamageRadius` (`dealDamageInternal`). Refused: a falloff with the distance, as Spring's `edgeEffectiveness`, which no imported game holds; a second splash of its own, which would strike the inner ring twice.
- **One bonus scales both rings.** An attack of its own amount takes `damage_bonus = "<stat>"`, of base 0, as it takes `rate_bonus`, and one multiplier of that stat scales its amount and its splash's `outer` amount, rounded once, as Zero Hour's weapon bonus scales its primary and its secondary damage alike (`WeaponTemplate::getSecondaryDamage`); the load refuses `damage_bonus` or `outer` beside a `damage` stat. Refused: the outer ring's fixed amount, which a stat that raises the weapon's damage leaves as it is.
- **A pre-attack.** An attack's `pre_attack = { ms, each }`, `each` one of `shot`, `target` and `clip`, is a phase apart from its windup: it starts as the unit aims, runs through the reload, starts again for each shot, target or clip, a clip's as the clip empties, starts again in the next tick when it ends before its weapon is ready, and ends on a loss of range, a death or a new order, as Zero Hour's `PreAttackDelay` and `PreAttackType`, which the import reads, run, its fire state falling back to its aim, which pre-fires again (`AIAttackFireWeaponState::update`), and its clip loaded as its reload starts (`Weapon::reloadWithBonus`). Refused: the windup, which starts only on a ready slot and sits inside the period; a pre-attack that, done, waits for its weapon, which fires a shot of a pre-attack shorter than the reload sooner than Zero Hour does.
- **An attack may scatter.** An `attack` of a projectile that flies to a point, a curve or a steered flight, takes `scatter = { radius, tags }`: a shot at a target of a tag the table names adds the largest of their radii, and above 0 aims at a point drawn on its own stream within it from the target's ground point, as Zero Hour's `fireWeaponTemplate` scatters at infantry, and a steered shot at that point tracks no unit, as Zero Hour launches a scattered missile at its point and not at its victim; the engine's random sequence is not the original's.
- **An attack may bound its pitch.** An `attack` takes `pitch_deg = [low, high]`: a target whose pitches from its attacker's center to its volume's bottom and top all miss the range is out of reach, as `Weapon::isWithinTargetPitch` meets `MinTargetPitch` and `MaxTargetPitch`; the bound lies within a quarter turn, so the test by the sine of a pitch past it tells each pitch apart, and the chase walks by range alone.
- **A unit type may have a turret.** A type's `turret = { turn_rate, tolerance_deg, slot, places, pivot }` turns its attacks of the slot kind, at the `places` among its unit's slots of that kind or at every one, toward the attack's target at its rate, about the vertical through `pivot`, a point of its unit's frame, and back toward 0 at half of it when idle, as `TurretAI` turns; an attack of the slot starts its windup only within the tolerance of its bearing, as `TurretAIAimTurretState` fires within 0.035 radians and OpenRA's `AttackTurreted.CanAttack` waits for its turret. It aims only while the weapon its unit attacks the target with, the one its attack starts with, is of a slot it carries, else turns back toward 0; an attack of a slot it does not carry fires at its body's bearing, as Zero Hour's turret aims only for the weapon slots its `ControlledWeaponSlots` names (`AIUpdateInterface::getWhichTurretForWeaponSlot`, `getWhichTurretForCurrentWeapon`). The aim is a part of the unit its owner's client predicts, and the client turns the node the look names. Refused: a turret of each slot kind alone, which aims a weapon of a slot the original's turret does not control.
- **An attacker aims its body.** An `attack` of `aim_deg` that no turret carries turns its attacker, once its target lies in its reach, toward the target at the attacker's turn step, and starts only within `aim_deg` of the target's bearing, as `AIAttackAimAtTargetState::update` turns a unit in place and fires within its weapon's `AcceptableAimDelta`, at least 0.035 radians; a weapon of none fires at any bearing, as a hero of a MOBA does. The facing is the unit's own part, which the walk turns toward its leg and the attack's start toward its target in reach, each in its own system of the tick's fixed order. Refused: the weapon's launch toward its target whatever its unit faces, which flew a Tank Hunter's missile out of its soldier's side once it left along its barrel.
- **A turret may pitch.** A turret's `pitch = { turn_rate, fire_deg, least_deg, rest_deg, pivot }` pitches its weapons at its rate about the level axis across its unit through `pivot`: to `fire_deg` when given, else toward the target's place from its unit's top, no lower than `least_deg`, and back toward `rest_deg` at half its rate when idle, as `TurretAIAimTurretState::update` pitches a turret of `AllowsPitch` to its `FirePitch` or toward its target above `MinPhysicalPitch`, and the recenter state back to `NaturalTurretPitch`; an attack of its slot starts only within the turret's tolerance of its pitch too, where the game waits for the pitch to reach its aim exactly, so a target that moves after the aim in a tick does not hold the start. The angle and the pitch are one part, `TurretAim`, as one turret holds both, and the client turns the pitch's node in its own frame, as `W3DModelDraw` controls a turret's bones. Refused: a lob that grows with the distance to a ground target, Zero Hour's `GroundUnitPitch`, which the import leaves (09-zero-hour.md, A turret's art angle and lob).
- **A shot leaves its weapon's barrel.** A type's `barrels` give each of its weapons that has them the barrels its shots leave from in turn, each a point of its unit's frame and the way its projectile leaves along, as Zero Hour launches a projectile at its weapon slot's `WeaponLaunchBone`, the next barrel with each shot (`Weapon::fireWeapon`), pitched about the turret's pitch bone, turned about its turret bone and then by its unit (`Weapon::calcProjectileLaunchPosition`); a weapon of none launches from its source's center. The turn of a weapon's barrel is the server's, a part no client holds, set after the strike, as a launch is the server's alone. The bones stand as the default state's first animation places them at its first frame, or its last of `PRISTINE_BONE_POS_IN_FINAL_FRAME`, as `validateCachedBones` sets a state's pristine bones, so an infantry's launcher stands in its arms and not at its bind pose. Refused: the barrel in the actions' shot from the strike, which the predicting client's strike would have to read; the bones of the state its unit shows, which the logic reads in the game, where the engine's sim holds no look, so the default state's stand for every state.
- **A slow weapon delays its damage.** An `attack` of no projectile takes `speed`: its hit lands ⌈distance ÷ speed a tick⌉ ticks after its shot, on the unit it was fired at, which takes nothing if it is dead by then, as Zero Hour's `WeaponStore::setDelayedDamage` holds a weapon's damage; the held hit is state of the match.
- **On-hit effects as League of Legends applies them.** A weapon's `on_hit` runs after its damage whenever the damage reaches a unit above zero life, shields absorbing all of it or not.
- **A weapon's own period may speed up by a stat.** An `attack` of `period_ms` takes `rate_bonus = "<stat>"`: its period and its clip's reload, in ticks, are divided by 1 plus the stat's value, rounded down, and the period stays longer than its windup, as Zero Hour's `getDelayBetweenShots` divides its frames by the summed `RATE_OF_FIRE` and floors, and OpenRA's `ReloadDelayMultiplier` truncates once ([Zero Hour continuous fire](../../research/zero-hour-continuous-fire.md)). A bonus of base 0, so a type that holds no value of it fires at its weapon's own period, and modifiers add to it as Zero Hour's bonuses add. Refused: a multiplier of base 1, which every type of the weapon would have to hold, or fire at no rate; a `rate_bonus` beside the stat `rate`, which the stat already speeds.
- **A shot streak gives a stage's modifier.** An `attack` takes `streak = { coast_ms, stages = [{ shots, modifier }] }`, the stages' shots strictly growing: a unit's shot of it counts one more when it hits the target of the shot before, or another before the coast ends, and starts again at one otherwise; at each shot the stage rises by one when the count passes the next stage's shots, and the streak cools down when the count is below its own stage's; and in the first tick past the coast with no shot, it cools down too. A cool-down ends the stage and forgets the count and the target, so the next shot counts one, as Zero Hour's `FiringTracker::coolDown` does at a fall and past the coast, stage or none. The unit holds the stage's modifier from itself while the stage lasts. Practice: Zero Hour's `FiringTracker`, and OpenRA's `GrantConditionOnAttack` with a `ReloadDelayMultiplier`. Refused: a weapon's own continuous-fire steps, a second way to change a weapon's rate beside the stats; a count kept across a cool-down, which a later shot at the same target counts on from, where the original counts one.
- **A streak's fall holds a modifier.** A streak may take `fall = { modifier, ms }`: a cool-down from a stage leaves the unit holding the fall's modifier until a shot rises to the first stage or the streak's next cool-down, past the coast of its last shot or, after a cool-down past the coast, `ms` later, as Zero Hour's `CONTINUOUS_FIRE_SLOW` holds from `coolDown` to `speedUp` or the next `coolDown`, a second after one past the coast (`FiringTracker::update`). Refused: the fall as a stage below the first, which a shot would count up from.
- **The streak is the unit's, the stages the weapon's.** One streak a unit, as Zero Hour's tracker is the object's, held from its spawn by every unit with slots, as a script may grant a streak weapon; a shot of a weapon of no streak leaves it as it is, and the stage's modifier is that of the weapon of the last shot. The coast ends `coast_ms` after the tick the shot's weapon is ready again, as the original counts it from the next possible shot.
- **A stage's modifier by a hold of its own.** The streak's grant holds the modifier as an item's holds its own, from the unit itself, under a hold no passive and no item shares, so neither releases the other's. Refused: a timed application refreshed at each shot, which needs its own removal at each fall.
- **The streak is the server's.** Only a role that computes state counts, after the strike, from the shots it lists; a predicting client derives its stats from the modifiers the server sends, so its own attacks' period follows a stage a tick late and corrects. Refused: a predicted streak, whose stage would need the server's targets as they are, not as the client holds them.
- **A secret roll.** An attack draws one number on the secret stream as its windup ends, and no client learns it before the server applies it; `calc_damage` gives it its meaning.

## Damage and heals

- **One pass a tick.** Every damage and heal of a tick applies in Resolve in one queue, in a fixed order of source and queue; a heal a hook or leech gives comes next, once the entry that caused it is done, as Dota 2 and League of Legends apply a reaction's heal at once. Every effect reads the units as the pass began, so two units can kill each other in one tick.
- **Damage queued after the pass is the next tick's.** It is state, which a snapshot at the boundary holds, and the next pass deals it first.
- **Armor by damage kind.** The mode declares its armors, each a share of each damage kind, and a unit type names one; the pass multiplies each damage by its share once, rounded down, before the mode's `calc_damage`, as Warcraft III's table of attack and armor types sits under its scripts.
- **Armor by tags.** A unit type's `armors` lists entries of a tag test, in the filter's syntax of tags, and an armor; the first whose test the unit's tags pass replaces the type's `armor`, as OpenRA's `Armor` takes `RequiresCondition` and Zero Hour takes the armor set whose conditions match best (`ArmorTemplateSet`), which the import orders first. Refused: an armor stat for each damage kind, which a modifier would have to rewrite kind by kind.
- **`calc_damage` and `calc_heal` decide the rest.** The mode's pure hooks turn each amount into its final one; with none, or a failed call, the amount stays, and neither counts against a pool.
- **Shields that end soonest absorb first.**
- **Leech counts what life took.** Leech heals by a share of the life an attack or other damage took, not what shields absorbed, as League of Legends' life steal counts.

## Death and respawn

- **A unit lives by one test.** `stats` decides whether a unit lives, not dead and its life above zero, and every pass reads that test: no heal or regen lifts a life at zero, only a respawn.
- **A target lives.** A target is a unit that lives, has a life, and whose tags do not block `target`, so a unit at zero life that has not died yet is no target, as Zero Hour's `isEffectivelyDead` keeps such an object out of targeting; one predicate in `actions` decides it for every check. A unit has a life when it holds one or is a body unit whose type has one, so a client that a mode keeps from an enemy's life still targets the enemy. Refused: a held life alone, which on such a client takes no enemy as a target, its checks of orders and its bots' searches among them ([Replication audiences](../10-replication-audiences.md)).
- **Deaths at the end of Resolve.** A unit at zero life dies at the end of Resolve; one whose life reaches zero later in the tick, as `ctx.kill` in a mode's hook leaves it, dies at the next tick's.
- **Kill credit.** The source whose damage took the life to zero is the killer; the units that damaged the victim within the mode's assist window assisted.
- **A respawn is at least a tick later,** at its spawn place, its pools full.
- **What aims at a unit learns of its death and its respawn.** A death drops the held hits on its unit and makes the projectiles that home on it lose it, and a respawn tells them it was put elsewhere at once, as a teleport does, since a respawn in the next tick's inputs puts the same unit back before they move again. Refused: each reader's test of life at its own step alone, which a unit that came back passes.
- **A death records its victim,** whose tags bounty and experience filter by, so Zero Hour's rules skip a victim under construction by its tag. Refused: `Has<Site>` in `Bounty::pay`, which names one game's rule in a capability ([A capability serves every game](../../../AGENTS.md#direction)).
- **A death keeps its unit for its animation.** A type's `on_death` may keep its dead unit for a time, `{ keep_ms = <ms> }`, a body of no collision, no target and no order, as every pass already passes over a dead unit that stays; the server despawns it at the end of the tick its time ends, by a timer of its own the client never receives, so every client draws its dying state, as Zero Hour's `SlowDeathBehavior` keeps its object for its `DestructionDelay`. The import keeps each object for the delay of the slow death a normal death most likely takes, of the first veterancy and no status, the one of most weight, the first of a tie; the draw among several by weight and overkill, the delay's variance, a death of another type or of an object under a status, as a site's, which a module's `ExemptStatus` may destroy at once, and the kin of `SlowDeathBehavior`, as a helicopter's crash, stay a loss ([research](../../research/zero-hour.md#animation)). Refused: a client's own copy of a unit that despawns, which no client can tell from a unit that leaves its sight; a timer of every client, which the server's despawn already reaches.

## Events

- **A unit's modifiers hear its combat events.** An attack, an interval, a hit, damage taken and a kill each run a hook on the modifiers of the unit concerned, in a fixed order, in the pool of the modifier source's player.
- **A depth bound ends the pass.** A hook an event of depth `k` causes runs at `k + 1`, and one at depth 16 fails, so a chain of reactions ends within the tick; `ctx.attack_hit` marks its damage so a hook can refuse to answer it, as Dota 2 marks reflected damage.
- **Combat's rules as data, planned.** Each comes with the slice of the game that needs it, its default today's behavior: damage stages before `calc_damage`, a bonus by tags and flat or ratio armor, closed and in data; a weapon's own damage scaled by a stat; leech rows by cause and kind; what a splash procs, for each weapon, each target by default; a splash edge that the data names, `<` or `≤`; weapon choice criteria; several turrets; line attack projectiles; a clip's reload from its start or its shot, for each weapon, as Zero Hour counts from the shot.

## Network

- **Damage only as the server confirms it.** A client predicts the start of its own attacks, never their damage or a death ([Replication audiences](../10-replication-audiences.md)).
