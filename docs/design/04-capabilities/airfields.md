# Airfields

A building's places and runways, and the sorties of the units it parks there. The facts are [Zero Hour's jets](../../research/zero-hour-jets.md).

## Sorties

- **An airfield is a capability.** The new capability `airfields` holds an airfield's places and runways, a jet's place, its taxi, takeoff, landing, reload, heal and return, as Zero Hour's `ParkingPlaceBehavior` and `JetAIUpdate` hold them apart from production and the locomotor. Refused: a part of production, which makes units and does not land them; a part of navigation, which walks units and does not refill their clips.
- **A jet takes off and lands on its runway.** A jet waits for its column's runway, taxis by forced dashes to its prep point, waits its takeoff pause, rolls from the runway's start to its end and climbs; to land it waits for the runway, flies to an approach point past the runway's end as far as it flies while it sinks from its height at its climb, lands from the end to the start and taxis to its place; one jet holds a runway at a time, as Zero Hour's jets do (decided 2026-10-27). Refused: OpenRA's vertical takeoff and landing on the place, under which a jet lifts off as a helicopter.
- **A jet goes home.** A jet whose every clip that reloads at home is empty, or that stands idle in the air for its sortie's idle time, returns to its airfield, lands, reloads and heals at its airfield's heal a second while it rests there, as `JetAIUpdate::update` returns it; one whose airfield is gone takes the nearest airfield of its player with a free place, and with none circles where it stands, losing its sortie's share of its life a second, as `JetOrHeliCirclingDeadAirfieldState` does; an airfield that dies kills the jets it holds, as `ParkingPlaceBehavior::killAllParkedUnits` does. The heal and the loss go through combat's pass as its `Upkeep`. Refused: OpenRA's flight idle with no damage, under which a jet with no airfield circles for ever; a heal or a loss written to the life pool, past combat's hooks and deaths.
- **An order waits for the air.** An order takes a jet that still holds a shot off its way home; one given from its runway's approach to its roll waits, held by the tag `airfield_held`, and runs after its reload; a jet sent home empty drops its attack and takes none up, as Zero Hour's `aiDoCommand` holds a command while a jet lands or takes off and resumes only a guard or a hunt. Refused: a stored command run again after every reload, which Zero Hour does not do for an attack.
