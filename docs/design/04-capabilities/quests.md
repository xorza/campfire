# Quests

- **One mechanism for quests, dialogue and objectives.** Planned: quests with stages and objectives, and dialogue with topics and choices, serve an RPG's quests, an MMO's quest givers and an RTS mission's objectives, as Skyrim's quests and Warcraft III's objectives track stages.
- **Objectives complete from events.** A `reach`, `kill`, `bring` or `talk` objective completes from the event it names; a `custom` one only from its script.
- **Lines go to their player alone,** in the player's language.
