# Campfire — RTS foundations

What the engine gained for RTS games: box bodies, unit types in every package, group orders, and production's first cut. The rules live in the capability documents; this one gives the decisions, and what waits. Zero Hour's skirmish is the match that plays them ([Zero Hour](12-zero-hour.md)).

## Decisions

- **D1. A building's body is a box, in every system.** Pathing, placement, collision and reach all read the box, as 0 A.D. keeps a structure as a static obstruction square while a unit stays a circle ([Bodies](04-capabilities/00-overview.md#space-and-map)). StarCraft II keeps a radius for reach beside a footprint for pathing, which costs less, but a range to a building's long side is then wrong by up to the difference between the two shapes.
- **D2. A box turns to any angle, and its shape rounds once, as it spawns.** Every test after reads the rounded half edges exactly, so no test rounds again. Degrees, not radians, make the right angles exact, as no multiple of π/2 is a `Num`.
- **D3. Construction has three styles, chosen by the build action:** `alone`, as StarCraft II's Protoss buildings grow; `builder`, one builder for the whole time, as a Terran SCV builds; `builders = [...]`, a rate for each count of builders, as Age of Empires and 0 A.D. speed a site less for each one more. A table is exact and states any curve: 0 A.D.'s n^0.7 has no exact form in fixed point, so a mode writes it rounded, `["1", "1.62", "2.16", "2.64"]` ([Construction](04-capabilities/production.md#construction)).
- **D4. One gatherer at a node; gatherers pass through each other.** A node takes one gatherer at a time. A worker that finds its node taken goes to the nearest free node of the same resource near it, or waits, as a StarCraft II worker moves to another mineral patch. Workers in the gather loop do not collide with each other, so the income does not depend on the order collision parts them in.
- **D5. Supply is derived, not a player resource.** Supply used and the cap follow from the living units, the queued trains and the complete providers, so no script keeps it and it cannot drift from them ([Supply](04-capabilities/production.md#supply)).
- **D6. Every package may hold unit types that stand, each in its own scope.** An avatar's summon, a loadout's ward and a faction's units are unit types of their own packages, which only their packages' data and scripts name. A faction's unit types stay in its mode's package; a faction as a package of its own, which a mode offers as a choice, waits ([Unit types](08-script-api.md#data-files)).
- **D7. One command orders a group, which shares one route.** An order names its units, so a group's move is one command, and the group plans one long route. Each unit keeps its place in the group when the goal lies outside the box of their positions, and goes to the goal itself when it lies inside, as StarCraft II's selection keeps its formation only for a move away from it ([Groups](04-capabilities/navigation.md#groups)). A wave of a spawn group shares its route the same way.
- **D8. A seen enemy body blocks a placement; a friendly or hidden one is moved out.** A placement fails where its box overlaps a wall, a static body, or the body of a walker whose team is not friendly to the builder's and that the builder's group sees. A refusal for a hidden walker would tell the builder it is there, so a hidden enemy is moved out as a friendly walker is, at the cost that a player may move a hidden enemy by building on it. A walker in the box goes to the nearest cell it may stand in, as a teleport places a unit: collision would push a walker caught between the new box and another static body from one into the other every tick.

## What waits

- **Order queues** (shift-click) and the `hold` order; formations, flow fields and crowds, with the imported game that needs them.
- **A faction as its own package**, and the `choices` offer that lists them (D6).
- **A client to play it:** the build placement, the gather and rally clicks and the queue's buttons, with Zero Hour's control bar. The client draws box bodies already.
