# Driving

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish sees a tank slow as it turns and turn in place past 45°, an Overlord gather speed over a second and brake into its goal, a Humvee swing round its rear axle in an arc and back out of a dead end, infantry lean into their turns, and a vehicle crawl once badly damaged, each as the release game drives it. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Driving; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/navigation.md` for movement models and their state, `04-capabilities/stats.md` for a hurt modifier, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's ground locomotors](../research/zero-hour-locomotors.md).

## Decisions

- **A type moves by its movement model.** Navigation's walk moves each walker by its type's model, an enum: `step`, today's turn and step along its leg, the default; `wings`, today's jet; and Zero Hour's locomotors, in `navigation::zero_hour`, as the design makes an imported game's locomotors a backend of the movement interface (02-engine-core.md, One interface, pluggable backends) and `09-zero-hour.md`'s Generic modules are capabilities, Zero Hour's own are Rhai names them; the route, the arrival, collision and the client's prediction stay one for every model. Refused: options of the generic model for each constant of the release game's movers, its `1.5`, its two cells and its 60 %, which no other game sets; a trait object, where the set of models is closed.
- **A mover keeps a velocity.** A unit of a model that moves by physics keeps its velocity on the ground plane, state that is saved, restored and predicted, integrated each tick as `PhysicsBehavior::update` does each frame, in fixed point, each step rounded once; Zero Hour's mode ticks at its 30 frames a second, so a tick is a frame. Refused: a speed alone along the facing, which loses the slide that the lateral friction leaves in a turn.
- **The release game's forward speed** (decided 2026-10-28). The model measures a mover's forward speed as the release game does, `√((vₓdₓ)² + (v_yd_y)²)`, so a mover drives up to `√2` times as fast on a diagonal as on an axis, as `PRESERVE_RETAIL_PHYSICS_FORWARD_SPEED_DISCREPANCY` keeps it by default. Refused: TheSuperHackers' uniform 1.1716 times, which no build plays by default; the authored speed on every heading, today's, slower than the release game's on every heading but an axis.
- **Each appearance moves as its mover.** `TREADS` and `TWO_LEGS` slow with their turn and turn in place past 45°, `FOUR_WHEELS` and `MOTORCYCLE` hold their turning speed in a turn, back from a stop toward a goal behind them and turn in three points, each with its slow-down distance and braking, as `moveTowardsPositionTreads`, `moveTowardsPositionLegs` and `moveTowardsPositionWheels` do. Refused: one model for every appearance, whose differences a player feels in every move.
- **A turn moves the body round its pivot,** a `TurnPivotOffset` times its bounding radius along its facing, and about its center while it brakes, as `rotateObjAroundLocoPivot`.
- **A wheel's arc is tested on the pathing grid.** A wheeled mover that turns more than 15° projects its arc half a second ahead and turns in place with no force when the arc's middle or end lies in a cell its walker kind may not stand in, as `validMovementTerrain` tests it.
- **A braking mover lands on its waypoint.** Within its slow-down distance of its route's end, a mover moves straight toward the end by its forward speed, at least a cell a second and no farther, as `IS_BRAKING` places it, so it arrives where its route ends.
- **The route feeds the mover.** The model drives toward the route's next waypoint, with the route's length left as its distance to its goal, as Zero Hour's path follower gives its locomotor its next point and its distance on the path; a waypoint is passed when the mover comes within its step of it.
- **A hurt type may hold a modifier.** A type's `hurt = { below, modifier }` holds the modifier while its life share is at most `below`, as Zero Hour moves at its locomotor's `Damaged` numbers from `MovementPenaltyDamageState`; the import's modifier adds the differences to `move_speed`, `turn_rate` and a new stat `acceleration`, each exact. Refused: the damaged numbers in the locomotor's data, which no slow or haste would then reach.
- **Collision stays the engine's.** A mover that collision parts keeps its velocity, where `PhysicsBehavior::onCollide` pushes it: a loss, as collision leaves no overlap by its own passes (04-capabilities/navigation.md, Collision leaves no overlap).
- **What the slice leaves.** Hover, thrust, wings and climbers move as today; a leg's wander; collision forces; a mover's bounce and its height's physics; a wheeled attacker that drives to aim (09-zero-hour.md's A turn in place); the release game's f32 rounding; each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The navigation tests drive a tread, a leg and a wheel mover from a stop to its speed, through a turn and into its goal, each tick's place, facing and velocity against values worked by hand from the release game's formulas, on an axis and on a diagonal; turn a tread in place past 45°; swing a wheel round its pivot; back a wheel to a goal behind it and turn it in three points; turn one in place before an arc into a wall; land a braking mover on its waypoint; predict a client's mover as its server drives it; keep a velocity over a save and a restore; and give a `step` walker today's goldens.
- The stats tests hold a hurt modifier at and below its share and drop it above.
- The import's tests give a Crusader, a Humvee and a Ranger their locomotors and the Humvee its hurt modifier; the Zero Hour check pins the movers of each appearance and its goldens move, each move named, and the check's benches measure the sim's worst tick before and after; its bots' match still ends.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert drives a Humvee round a corner, an Overlord into its goal and a damaged tank home, and sees each move as the release game's.
