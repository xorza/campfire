# Shroud and minimap

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish on Tournament Desert sees the cells their team never saw black, the cells it saw before and does not see now dark, and the buildings it saw there as it last saw them; sees no enemy their team does not see; and, once they hold a radar, sees a minimap of the terrain, their team's sight, the units they see and the camera's view, and moves the camera and orders a move from it. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Shroud and minimap; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/vision.md` for what a team sees and keeps, `10-replication-audiences.md` for how it reaches its player, `02-engine-core.md` for the client's fog, ghosts and minimap, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's shroud and radar](../research/zero-hour-shroud.md).

## Decisions

- **Zero Hour sees by the vision capability.** The imported mode holds `vision`; each map's grid has cells of 40 m, the partition's cell; a type sees by its `ShroudClearingRange`. Refused: a grid of the terrain's 10 m cells, 16 times the cells for a shroud the game draws at 40 m.
- **A team keeps the cells it explored.** Each vision group keeps the cells it ever saw beside those it sees now, when the mode starts its map shrouded, as Zero Hour's `CELLSHROUD_FOGGED` and OpenRA's explored cells; a MOBA's map starts known. They are state, saved and restored, as no stage can derive them again. Refused: a client's own memory of what it saw, lost on a reconnect and a restore.
- **The server sends a team's cells to its player** (decided 2026-10-27). The Vision stage writes, on each player's entity, the cells its team sees and explored, a new kind `sight` sent to its owner alone, written only when a cell changes; a predicting client's build point then reads them. Refused: the client's own count over its team's units, which shows no reveal, a server resource, and runs one rule in two places; the match's entity, whose cells every client would receive.
- **The fog is the one material's.** The file material samples one texture of the team's cells, a texel for each cell, filtered between them, and multiplies its color by the cell's level, so the terrain, its decals, the models and the particles darken alike, as `W3DShroud` multiplies the terrain and its material pass the objects; the levels and the color are the mode's, the import taking `GameData.ini`'s. A cell's level changes at once, as the release game's (`DO_FOG_INTERPOLATION` is not defined); past the grid the fog is shrouded, as the game's border. Refused: a pass over the screen, which darkens no blended particle by its own place; a mesh over the terrain, which darkens no model.
- **An immobile unit leaves a ghost** (decided 2026-10-27). A unit of a type of no movement that leaves the client, not dying, stays drawn where it was, as it was last seen, until its team sees its cells again, as Zero Hour's `GhostObject` of `KINDOF_IMMOBILE`; a ghost is not picked, selected or barred. The client draws only what its team saw, so a ghost shows nothing hidden. Refused: the server's last state of each unit for each team, which no client needs.
- **A minimap by a radar tag** (decided 2026-10-27). The client shows the minimap while its player holds a unit of the mode's radar tag, and at all times in a mode of none; the import gives the tag to an object of a `RadarUpgrade` once its trigger is researched, as Zero Hour's `Player::hasRadar` counts its radar objects. Refused: a minimap from the start, which hands a Zero Hour player a radar the game sells.
- **The minimap is drawn from the terrain at load.** Its image is the composed terrain texture's color, averaged over each cell of the minimap and shaded by its height between the mode's bright and dark shares, as `W3DRadar::buildTerrainTexture`; the client darkens it by the team's cells, draws each unit it holds and the radar may show as a square of its team's color, and the camera's view as the trapezoid of its corners on the ground. Refused: a map image written at import, which only a game of such an image could give; a top-down camera rendering each frame, which draws the shroud's own darkness and no unit as a dot.
- **A minimap click moves the camera, a right click orders a move,** as Zero Hour's radar takes them.
- **What the slice leaves.** A sight from the seer's cell, which Zero Hour reveals from; a site's sight of its own radius; reveals of a power, a scan or a spy; stealth; a spectator's fog; a target of a ghost; the radar's power, its events, its dish's animation and a beacon; water on the minimap: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The vision tests count a team's explored cells, keep them over a save and a restore, and change a player's cells only when a cell changes; the leak test holds the kind `sight` to its owner.
- The import's tests give a type its sight, a map its grid, the mode its vision and its shroud, and a `RadarUpgrade` its tag; the Zero Hour check pins the counts, and its bots' match still ends.
- The client's tests give the fog's texels and their place on the ground, spawn a ghost of an immobile unit that leaves and none of one that dies, drop it when its cells are seen, shade the minimap's terrain, place a unit and the camera's view on it, and show it by the radar tag.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert scouts the enemy's base under the shroud, sees its buildings stay as ghosts when the scout leaves, researches the radar and moves the camera from the minimap.
