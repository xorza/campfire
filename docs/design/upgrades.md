# Upgrades

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player in a Zero Hour skirmish researches player upgrades and object upgrades from the panel, each with its cost, time and requirements, and sees them change units in battle: their armor, weapon sets, weapon damage and life. Its plan is `PLAN.md`'s Upgrades; when the slice ends, each lasting decision moves into the document of the system it governs, and this file is deleted.

The facts it takes from: Zero Hour's `Upgrade.ini` holds 82 upgrades; its command sets hold 58 player-upgrade and 37 object-upgrade buttons; its objects hold 154 `ArmorUpgrade`, 119 `WeaponSetUpgrade`, 62 `WeaponBonusUpgrade` and 52 `MaxHealthUpgrade` modules, every one of the last of change type `ADD_CURRENT_HEALTH_TOO`; 85 of its weapons take `WeaponBonus = PLAYER_UPGRADE DAMAGE`, 84 of them at 125 % and one at 112 % (the Steam install, read 2026-10-25).

## Decisions

- **Research is an action kind of production.** `research` joins its producer's train queue, pays as it joins, takes its time, and as it ends gives a modifier: its player's for a player research, its producer's own for an object research, as Zero Hour's `ProductionUpdate` queues an upgrade beside its units and StarCraft II's research is an ability of its building. Refused: a research as a train of a hidden unit, as OpenRA provides a prerequisite by an actor (`ProvidesPrerequisite`), which spends a unit on a fact; a queue of the player's apart from its producers'.
- **An upgrade is a modifier.** What a research gives is a modifier the mode declares, so a requirement names it as it names a player modifier now (`requires.modifiers`) and it replicates as player modifiers do ([Replication audiences](10-replication-audiences.md)); a research of a modifier its player, or for an object research its producer, holds or queues anywhere is refused, as Zero Hour's panel refuses an upgrade its player has or is making (`Player::hasUpgradeInProduction`). Refused: a set of upgrades beside the modifiers, a second record of one fact.
- **A unit type takes modifiers by upgrades.** A type's `upgrades` lists entries of `triggers`, modifier ids any one of which, or with `all` every one, its player's modifiers and its own must hold, `conflicts` none of which they may hold, and the `modifier` the unit takes while they are met, held by a hold of its own ([Stats](04-capabilities/stats.md), An instance lives while something keeps it), as Zero Hour's `UpgradeMux` tests `TriggeredBy`, `RequiresAllTriggers` and `ConflictsWith` and OpenRA's `GrantConditionOnPrerequisite` grants a condition. A unit tests its entries as it spawns, as its site completes, and as it or its player gains or loses a modifier, and the test visits only the units of the types that name that modifier. Refused: a player modifier for each type an upgrade changes, whose filter would need a tag for each of Zero Hour's 643 types of its factions, past the 256 a match holds; the effect written into the research, which differs by type.
- **Armor by tags.** A unit type's `armors` lists entries of a tag test, in the filter's syntax of tags, and an armor; the first whose test the unit's tags pass replaces the type's `armor`, as OpenRA's `Armor` takes `RequiresCondition` and Zero Hour takes the armor set whose conditions match best (`ArmorTemplateSet`), which the import orders first. Refused: an armor stat for each damage kind, which a modifier would have to rewrite kind by kind.
- **An action may need tags.** An action's `when` is a tag test in the same syntax; an action whose test fails does not start, holds no passive, and no weapon choice or scan takes it, as OpenRA's `Armament` takes `RequiresCondition` and Zero Hour's weapon set of `PLAYER_UPGRADE` replaces the one of none; an order of it is refused, as Zero Hour's panel disables the button of an upgrade not held, though an order a block stops waits: a stun ends, and a missing upgrade may never come; it keeps its slot, so a client binds its keys to the same slots whatever its unit holds ([Actions](04-capabilities/actions.md), Slot kinds in the mode's order). Refused: actions granted and taken away by modifiers, which move a unit's slots as it is upgraded.
- **Zero Hour's upgrade flags are tags and stats.** `ArmorUpgrade` and `WeaponSetUpgrade` grant the tags of the armor set's and the weapon set's `PLAYER_UPGRADE`, which the import's `armors` and `when` test; `WeaponBonusUpgrade` raises the stat each weapon of a `PLAYER_UPGRADE` damage bonus names as its `damage_bonus`, one stat for each factor ([Combat](04-capabilities/combat.md), One bonus scales both rings); `MaxHealthUpgrade` adds to the life maximum, whose rise raises life by as much ([Stats](04-capabilities/stats.md), A rising maximum raises the amount by as much), as its `ADD_CURRENT_HEALTH_TOO`. The import shares one modifier among the types an upgrade changes alike, named by its upgrade and its effect, so no name passes `DeclaredName::MAX_LEN`. Every other upgrade module is dropped, counted and a loss. Refused: the game's other change types of life, which no object uses.
- **The panel shows a research as an action.** A research is an action in its producer's slots, so the panel shows it with its cost and time as it shows a train, and a held one as done, with no panel of upgrades apart.

## Checks

- The scripted match: a research pays as it joins, takes its time and gives its player its modifier; a second research of that modifier is refused while one is queued and once it is held; an object research gives its producer the modifier; a type's upgrade holds at a spawn after the research, at a research after the spawn and as a site completes, and an entry of `all`, of any and of a conflict each hold exactly as their triggers say; an armor by tags changes the damage taken, and a weapon of `when` replaces the one of none, each by an amount computed by hand.
- The Zero Hour check counts the upgrades, the research buttons and the upgrade modules imported and dropped, by cause; its skirmish play researches a player upgrade of each effect the slice imports and an object upgrade, and pins its units' armor, weapons, damage bonus and life after them.
- In the client, a player researches Composite Armor and sees a Crusader's life rise by 100, and a China player researches Uranium Shells and sees a Battlemaster's shots deal 125 %.
