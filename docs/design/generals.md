# Veterancy and generals

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish sees their units rise to veteran, elite and heroic by their kills, each with its chevron and its bonuses; sees their general's rank rise by the kills of their side; buys a science with the rank's points, which shows the buttons it unlocks; and, at rank 3, fires Emergency Repair from their command center onto damaged vehicles. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Veterancy and generals; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/progression.md` for tracks, shares and perks, `04-capabilities/games.md` for a death's experience, `04-capabilities/combat.md` for the bonuses, `04-capabilities/actions.md` for a player's cooldown, `02-engine-core.md` for the client's icons and panels, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's veterancy, general's ranks, sciences and powers](../research/zero-hour-generals.md).

## Decisions

- **Veterancy is progression's data** (decided 2026-10-28). The imported mode's `level` track is its veterancy, each trainable type on it, so a unit's level is public as every client draws it, as OpenRA's `GainsExperience` is a trait of data. Refused: Rhai in the rules package, as `09-zero-hour.md`'s Generic modules are capabilities, Zero Hour's own are Rhai named veterancy, a mechanism OpenRA shares, which an `on_level_up` call for each rise would run; this changes that decision.
- **A type's own thresholds.** A type's entry in `tracks` may give its own `levels`, of the count of the track's, as Zero Hour's `ExperienceRequired` and OpenRA's `GainsExperience` are each type's; the track's own are the default. Refused: a track for each list of thresholds, 15 tracks for one veterancy.
- **A level holds a modifier.** A track's `modifiers` name one modifier for each level from 2, which its unit holds while at that level, as a passive is held, as `Object::onVeterancyLevelChanged` sets one bonus condition and clears the others and OpenRA's `GainsExperience` grants a condition for each level. Refused: an `on_level_up` that applies them, a script whose state undoes the level before; stats' `per_level`, which grows in equal steps where the health bonus goes 120 %, 130 %, 150 %.
- **A weapon's bonus is a sum of stats.** `damage_bonus` and `rate_bonus` take a list of stats whose sum is the bonus, as `WeaponBonusSet::appendBonuses` adds each condition's bonus less 1; every imported weapon reads the veterancy's stats, a weapon of continuous fire its stages' too, and one of an upgrade bonus its upgrade's. Refused: veterancy modifiers that add to each upgrade's stat, which ties the veterancy to the list of upgrades; one rate stat for every weapon, which speeds a unit's other weapons with its streak (09-zero-hour.md, Only a weapon of a rate bonus reads the stat).
- **A life's rise by a level is the stats' rule.** The health bonus is a percent of the life maximum, whose rise raises the life by as much (04-capabilities/stats.md, A rising maximum raises the amount by as much, and a falling one keeps it), where Zero Hour keeps the share (`PRESERVE_RATIO`): a loss.
- **A death's experience goes by shares.** The mode's `[[experience]]` lists shares, each its track, to whom, its victims and its killers, and a type's `gives` serves each share, as no Zero Hour object sets a `SkillPointValue` apart from its `ExperienceValue`; a list by level counts the victim's `level`. Refused: a type's `gives` for each share, which no game of the engine needs; a victim's level on the share's own track, which a player's track does not have.
- **A share's killer passes a filter.** A share's `killer` is a filter from the victim's side, as a bounty's is (04-capabilities/games.md, A kill's bounty), so Zero Hour's `enemies` gives nothing for the neutral player's civilians, as `Object::scoreTheKill` gives none for a side that is not playable.
- **A track of players.** A track of `holder = "player"` keeps its experience and its points on each player's entity, sent to its owner, and a share `to = "player"` gives a death's experience to its killer's player, as OpenRA's `GivesExperience` feeds the killer's player's `PlayerExperience` and Zero Hour's `addSkillPointsForKill` its general. Refused: a hidden unit for the general, which a kill or a script can reach; a player resource, which a cost spends and a bounty pays.
- **A track gives points by level.** A track's `points` give the points of each level from 1, one each by default, as a MOBA's hero gains one a level and Zero Hour's `Rank.ini` grants 1, 1, 1, 1 and 3; the veterancy gives none.
- **A science is a perk of a player.** The mode's `[perks.<name>]` gives each its cost in points, the perks it requires, the level of its track it needs and the modifier its player holds once it has it; a player learns one by an order of no units, applied in Inputs in input order, and a mode's script grants one with `grant_perk(player, id)`, as Zero Hour's `attemptToPurchaseScience` and `grantScience`. A perk of no cost is granted alone, as a science of cost 0; a rank's science is the level it needs; a faction's science is a perk the mode's start grants. Refused: a research of production, which queues at a producer, pays in resources and takes its time; the planned perks of units, which wait for their slice.
- **A perk's modifier is a player modifier.** So a train's `requires.modifiers`, an action's `when` by the tag it grants and a stat it raises read it by the paths they have, as a science turns on Zero Hour's units, powers and starting levels alike; the science prerequisites the import drops convert.
- **A start level by a modifier.** A type's entry in `tracks` may give `start = { level, requires }`: its unit spawns at that level, with its experience at the level's threshold, while its player holds the modifier, as `VeterancyGainCreate` and OpenRA's `ProducibleWithLevel` start a unit at a level.
- **A power is a cast of its building.** Emergency Repair is a cast in its command center's command slot at a point anywhere, `range = "global"`, that lands an area once, which restores its amount of life to each living vehicle of its caster's allies within 100 m, its own included; the cast's `when` tests the tag its first perk's modifier gives the command center, and its amount is a stat each of its three perks raises by 100, as `UpgradeOCL` picks the list of the highest science held and the lists heal 100, 200 and 300. Refused: actions of the player's entity, which casts nothing and stands nowhere; three actions, one for each science, which the panel would hide and show by each other's tags.
- **A power's cooldown may be its player's.** An action's `cooldown_holder = "player"` keeps its ready tick on its player's entity, shared by every unit of the player that holds the action, and none yet held is ready, as Zero Hour's `SharedSyncedTimer` and OpenRA's `SupportPowerManager` share one timer among a player's buildings, and a purchase readies its power. Refused: each building's own timer, under which a second command center fires twice.
- **The import converts a marker's burst.** An `OCLSpecialPower` of `CREATE_AT_LOCATION` whose lists each create one object of a single-burst `AutoHealBehavior` converts to such a cast; every other power is dropped, counted and a loss.
- **A bot buys its skill set.** The import writes each faction's skill sets, and the bot draws one by the match's random as it first holds a point and from then learns each perk of it it can, in its order, as `AIPlayer::doUpgradesAndSkills`. Refused: a bot that fires its powers, which waits for a slice of the AI's targeting.
- **A level shows its icon.** The HUD file's `level_icons` give an icon for each level from 2, drawn beside the bars' stand of each living unit drawn, selected or not, as `Drawable::drawVeterancy` draws its chevrons; the import gives `SCVeter1` to `SCVeter3`.
- **The perk panel by a choice.** The client file `client/perks.toml` names a choice, and for each of its values the pages of perks in a grid, each perk at its place with its icon, as the sound file plays music by a choice; its chord opens and closes the panel, which shows the rank, its progress and the points, each perk learnable, held or dimmed, and learns the one clicked; the import writes each faction's three purchase sets. Refused: every perk of the mode, under which a player sees another faction's.
- **The command panel shows casts.** A cast of a shown slot is a button, hidden while its action's `when` fails, as a power button of `NEED_SPECIAL_POWER_SCIENCE` hides, dimmed while it cools with its time left in its tooltip, and aimed at the ground as a command's point is (02-engine-core.md, The input's modes are states).
- **A button of an unmet requirement is dimmed,** as Zero Hour's `canMakeUnit` restricts one of no prerequisite, so a train's science shows before it is bought.
- **What the slice leaves.** The powers' shortcut bar, a power's cursor of its radius, the repair's cloud and its healed icons, the promotion's sounds and animation and the rank's speech, the experience scalar of Advanced Training, a mob's experience sink, a hack's cash and experience by veterancy, upgrades of a veterancy, the share of life kept as the maximum rises, every other power, the AI's powers and the public timers: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The progression tests give a type its own thresholds, a level its modifier while held, a track its points by level, each share its experience to its killer or its killer's player by the victim's level, none past its killer's filter, a perk learned for its points, its level and its requires and refused without them, a perk granted, and a start level by a modifier; the combat tests sum a weapon's bonus stats; the actions tests share a player's cooldown between two units and keep it over a save and a restore; the leak test holds a player's experience and points to its owner.
- The import's tests give a trainable type its track, thresholds and gives, the mode its veterancy modifiers from `GameData.ini`, its rank from `Rank.ini`, each science its perk, a science prerequisite its requirement, a `VeterancyGainCreate` its start, Emergency Repair its cast and perks, and each faction its skill sets and pages; the Zero Hour check pins the counts, a play of a unit rising by its kills and of a rank rising by the side's, a learned perk that unlocks its train, and a repair that heals a vehicle and waits its cooldown; its bots' match still ends.
- The client's tests place a level's icon, lay a faction's perk pages, learn the clicked perk, show a cast hidden and dimmed, aim it at a point, and dim a train of an unmet requirement.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert sees a tank turn veteran after its kills, reaches rank 3, buys the Paladin and sees its button come on, buys Emergency Repair and fires it onto damaged vehicles from the command center.
