# Combat response

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish sees a unit that takes fire from beyond its weapons' reach bring its idle, armed allies nearby after the attacker, who chase it for a while and walk back; sees a tank's turret turn onto an enemy it passes and fire while the tank drives on; and sees a unit of two weapons fire the better one at each target, as the Comanche its cannon at infantry and its missiles at a tank. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Combat response; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/control.md` for the scan, the chase and retaliation, `04-capabilities/combat.md` for the turret and the weapon choice, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's retaliation, turret scan and weapon choice](../research/zero-hour-combat-response.md) and [Zero Hour automatic attack](../research/zero-hour-auto-attack.md).

## Decisions

- **Combat chooses the weapon.** For each unit with an attack target, combat chooses, each tick, the weapon it attacks with: of the weapons whose `when` its tags pass, whose filter selects the target and whose kind the target's armor does not spare, of those that reach the target one ready before one cooling, one the target's tags make preferred before one they do not, then the one of the most estimated damage, then the lowest slot, and while none reaches it the first, as `WeaponSet::chooseBestWeaponForTarget` walks its slots by `PREFER_MOST_DAMAGE`, passes over a weapon out of range and falls back to its primary. Refused: the first weapon that selects the target, today's, which fires a Comanche's missiles at infantry; OpenRA's every armament at once, which Zero Hour does not fire; a weapon of an estimate of 0 passed over, which a weapon of no amount of its own, whose hits' lists deal its damage, would never fire.
- **An estimate is the weapon's damage as the armor takes it.** A weapon's estimate is its amount times its bonus, as the target's armor takes its damage kind, as `Weapon::estimateWeaponDamage`; the mode's `calc_damage` does not run for it. Refused: a script call for each weapon and target each tick, past what the pass that applies the damage spends.
- **An attack may prefer its targets.** An attack's `prefers`, a filter of tags, ranks the weapon above every one it does not prefer for a target it selects, and a preferred weapon cooling ranks as ready, as a weapon set's `PreferredAgainst` counts a weapon of huge damage and ready unless out of ammunition against a target of all its kinds, `isKindOfMulti`. Refused: a list of tags of which a target holds one, which prefers what Zero Hour does not, as a Comanche's missiles at every vehicle by `SECONDARY VEHICLE STRUCTURE`.
- **The chosen weapon is the attack's.** The choice is kept in the unit's progress, so its start, its turret's aim and its chase read one weapon, as `Armed::weapon_against` holds today. Refused: each reader choosing again, which a tick's change of readiness would split.
- **The scan reaches by any weapon.** An idle scan takes the nearest target that a weapon of the unit selects and reaches, where it takes the first weapon's reach today. Refused: the reach of the weapon the choice would take, which depends on readiness the next tick changes.
- **A turret has its own target.** A unit of a turret that walks with nothing under way and no target takes, by the scan of each period of its `acquire`, the nearest target a weapon of it selects and reaches as its turret target, which its turret aims at and fires at while the unit walks on, as `TurretAI::friend_checkForIdleMoodTarget` takes its unit's mood target; the target goes once no weapon reaches it with no attack in its windup, or once the weapon chosen against it is one the turret does not carry, and an attack target ends it, as the unit's own comes first. The scan, of `orders`, reads which types mount a turret from a book of `actions`' `TurretMount` that combat builds. Refused: the unit's attack target from that scan, which stops the walk as an attack-move's scan does; a turret target beside an attack target of a weapon the turret does not carry, as a unit's progress holds one attack at a time; the scan in combat, which reads neither the scan's period, `orders`', nor what a team sees.
- **Retaliation is the mode's data.** The mode's `[retaliation]` gives the aggressor's filter and its most distance from the damaged unit, the radius round the damaged unit its allies answer within, and how long they chase; a type's `retaliate = { leash, reach }` makes its units answer, which the load refuses on a type of no weapon or no walk, as `RetaliationFriendsRadius`, `MaxRetaliationDistance` and `GuardChaseUnitsDuration` are `AIData.ini`'s and an immobile or `CANNOT_RETALIATE` object does not answer. Refused: a Rhai hook for each hit, called by every damage of a battle; OpenRA's return fire, which only the damaged unit answers.
- **A damaged unit calls its idle allies.** Once a tick's damage applied and its dead died, in Resolve, on the server, by the hits a report of `units` keeps, each unit damaged by a living aggressor its filter passes within its distance calls each living unit of its own player's allies, its own included, within the radius past its body, that answers, stands idle as the scan's idle, and has a weapon that selects the aggressor; each takes the aggressor as its attack target, dead or not the damaged unit, as `ActiveBody::attemptDamage` calls even as it dies. The pass visits the units damaged that tick alone.
- **A retaliation's target is chased to its leash.** A target of retaliation is chased as an ordered one is, and dropped when the chase's time passes, when the target lies past its `reach` from the point the unit stood at, or when the unit lies past its `leash` from it; then the unit attack-moves back to that point, as `AIGuardRetaliateReturnState` returns to its guard point. Refused: an ordered target, chased with no end; a scanned target, dropped past the weapons' reach, which leaves the unit standing under artillery.
- **Every player's units answer** (decided 2026-10-28). A bot's units retaliate as a player's, where Zero Hour's computer players' do not, as the engine's bot units scan only what their weapons reach, as a human player's, and would stand under fire past it. Refused: a human player's units alone, which leaves a bot's army that a scan of Zero Hour's AI would send after its attacker.
- **The import converts what the choice and the answer read.** A weapon set's `PreferredAgainst` becomes its weapon's `prefers`, the filter of all the tags of its kinds, `infantry`, `vehicle`, `structure` and a new `aircraft`, and goes with a kind of no tag, as `BALLISTIC_MISSILE`, as no missile is a target; a slot whose `AutoChooseSources` names no source drops its weapon, which the game fires only at a target a lock gives it, as the Patriot battery's assist weapon; `AIData.ini`'s retaliation values become the mode's `[retaliation]`, aggressors `enemies:!air_target`; and a type of an AI module that walks, armed, not `IMMOBILE`, `DRONE` or `CANNOT_RETALIATE`, gets `retaliate` of its `VisionRange` times 1.8 and times 4.0, `GuardInnerModifierHuman` and its sum with `GuardOuterModifierHuman`.
- **A play walks its units out of the base starts** (decided 2026-10-28). The Zero Hour check's plays of the choice, the turret and the retaliation take the units each player's base start gives, a Comanche, Crusaders, Rebels, a Scorpion and a rocket buggy, and walk them by orders to the places each play needs in the open middle of Tournament Desert, so their logs replay, as every order is an input; OpenRA's test maps place their actors by a map script's `Actor.Create`. Refused: a start of the check's that a mode script places, which adds a placement no game uses where the base starts hold every unit the plays need; a play's write of units past its inputs, which its replay does not hold; the user's play in the client alone, which no later change is checked against.
- **What the slice leaves.** The player's retaliation option, always on; the return's own scan of 1.8 times the vision range, where the unit attack-moves; a computer player's wider scan and pursuit; a stealthed unit's silence; a turret's idle sweep; a turret still aiming counted as not ready; a locked weapon, command sources and pitch limits in the choice; the estimate's set values for shrubbery, a sniper at an empty structure, a garrison's clearing, disarm and deploy: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The combat tests choose a ready weapon over a cooling one, a preferred one over a stronger one, the stronger of two by armor, the lower slot of two equal, and none of an estimate of 0; the turret tests aim and fire a walking unit's turret at a target it passes, keep its walk, drop the target out of reach, and give the unit's own target first; the orders tests chase with the chosen weapon's range, scan by any weapon's reach, call the idle allies within the radius and none past it, none busy and none that cannot hit the aggressor, drop a retaliation at its time, its leash and its reach, and walk the unit back.
- The import's tests give the Comanche its preferences, the mode its retaliation and a Crusader its leash and reach; the Zero Hour check pins the counts, and plays of a Comanche that fires its cannon first at a Rebel and its missiles first at a Scorpion, two Crusaders that answer a rocket buggy firing on one of them from beyond its reach and walk back to their places, and a Crusader whose turret fires on a Rebel it drives past and that reaches its goal; its bots' match still ends.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert sees their idle tanks go after a rocket buggy that hits one of them and come back, a driving tank fire on the move, and a Comanche switch weapons by its target.
