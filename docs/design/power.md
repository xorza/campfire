# Power

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish as America builds past what its power plants give and sees the power bar turn red, its Patriot batteries go dark and silent, its minimap go out and its trains and builds slow; builds another power plant, or buys the Control Rods, and sees all of it come back; and as GLA trains a Rebel in the original's 10 s. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Power; its facts are [Zero Hour's power](../research/zero-hour-power.md). When the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/production.md` for the count, the shortage and the speed, `02-engine-core.md` for the client's bar and marks, `03-game-scripting.md` for the bot and `09-zero-hour.md` for the import and its losses, and this document goes.

## Decisions

- **Power is production's.** The mode's `[power]` turns power on, and the holdings that count each player's supply count its power in the same follow of the units, as supply is derived there and production's planned capacities sit beside it. Refused: a capability of its own, as OpenRA keeps `Traits/Power` apart from its `ProductionQueue`, which would need production's complete site below it and its speed above it, a contract crate between them, a second follow of the units and a capability in every manifest, command and log.
- **A unit's power is a stat.** `[power] stat` names a unit stat whose value a unit gives while above 0 and takes while below, its type's base the stat's, so a modifier changes it as Zero Hour's `EnergyBonus` joins by an upgrade and OpenRA's `Power` takes its modifiers; a unit counts while it lives, its player owns it and its site is complete, a rider and a building under sale among them, as `Player::becomingTeamMember` and `onStructureConstructionComplete` count an object and `BuildAssistant::sellObject` leaves its power, and a change of its stat counts it again. Refused: a fixed amount per type with a bonus an upgrade names, a second route beside the modifiers for a number that changes.
- **Short while it uses more than it gives.** A player is short while its units take more than they give, as `Energy::hasSufficientPower`, so a player that gives and takes nothing is not short. Refused: OpenRA's three states, as no game the engine imports splits low from critical.
- **A shortage is a player modifier.** `[power] short` names a modifier that the player holds while short: the count adds it to the player's modifiers as the player falls short and removes it as power returns, so it holds on each unit its `affects` selects and changes them by the mode's data, as OpenRA's `GrantConditionOnPowerState` grants the condition `^DisableOnLowPower` turns into `disabled`, and every reader of player modifiers, upgrades, requirements, scripts and the client, sees it with no route of its own; a restore's check holds the modifier equal to the count. Refused: a second, derived set of player modifiers beside the held ones, which every reader would read twice; a tag the count writes on units, which no modifier grants and no script, upgrade or requirement sees.
- **The speed by the ratio.** `[power] speed = { penalty, min, max, empty }` gives a short player's speed `p = 1 − (1 − r) × penalty` at least `min` and at most `max`, `r` the share of its use that it gives, and a time at that speed of the time over `p` rounded down, exactly from the two counts, as `ThingTemplate::calcTimeToBuild` divides by its rate; `empty`, `full` or `none`, is the share of a player that gives and takes nothing, `none` in Zero Hour, whose `getEnergySupplyRatio` returns 0 so that a GLA player always builds at half speed, which TheSuperHackers keep as the game's balance ([#1390](https://github.com/TheSuperHackers/GeneralsGameCode/issues/1390)). OpenRA's single `LowPowerModifier` is a `min` equal to its `max`. Refused: a table of steps by ratio, Red Alert's, which cannot hold Zero Hour's linear part exactly; a curve of points and jumps, a format for one rule.
- **What power slows is the mode's.** `[power] slows` lists the action kinds whose time the speed changes, `train` and `build` in Zero Hour, whose `UpgradeTemplate::calcTimeToBuild` leaves a research unslowed, where OpenRA's queue slows every item. Refused: every production kind, which would slow Zero Hour's research.
- **A train's end follows the speed.** A queue's head keeps the tick it reached the head, and ends at that tick plus its time at its player's current speed; as a player's speed changes, the count moves each of its queues' heads in the index of the queues, so a head past its new end ends in the next tick, as `ProductionUpdate::update` divides the frames spent by the time at the current power each frame. Refused: a train's progress accumulated at each tick's speed, as OpenRA and the planned one progress model by rate, under which a train half done when the first power plant completes no longer ends at once, as the original's does.
- **A site grows at the speed.** A site's progress each tick is its builders' rate times its time over its time at its player's speed, so at a constant speed it completes in that time, as a dozer adds `100 / calcTimeToBuild` each frame at the current power; a predicting client grows it at the speed its player's received count gives. Refused: a site's time fixed at its placement by the speed then, which a power plant completed during the build would not reach.
- **The count runs as each tick ends.** The server counts each player's power as each tick ends, after its deaths, captures and completions, writes it on the player's entity, sent on change to the player's audience as supply is, and applies the shortage's edge and the queues' moves there, so every system of the next tick reads one count, one tick after the original applies it. Refused: a count at each reader, as the holdings follow, which would give the sites and the trains of one tick two speeds.
- **The radar falls by an upgrade's conflict.** The import writes the shortage's modifier into the `conflicts` of each radar upgrade that is not `DisableProof`, so the radar's tag drops while its player is short and the minimap hides by the rule it has, as `Player::disableRadar` spares only a `DisableProof` radar, the GLA Radar Van's. Refused: a minimap rule in the HUD with a second tag for a radar that is proof.
- **A disabled unit is the rules package's.** Zero Hour's rules data declares the tag `powered`, which the import gives each type of `KindOf = POWERED`, the shortage's modifier `underpowered`, which holds on units of that tag and grants the tag `disabled`, and `disabled`'s blocks of moving, attacks, casts, uses and production, as `AIUpdate` runs under no disabling but `HELD` so that a disabled object neither moves nor fires; the import writes `[power]` from `GameData.ini` and each type's stat from its `EnergyProduction`. Refused: the engine naming a disabled state, which a mode's tags already say.
- **The Control Rods give power.** The import's modifier of a `PowerPlantUpgrade` adds its type's `EnergyBonus` to the power stat beside its tag, one modifier for each bonus, `power_plant_upgraded_<n>`, shared by every type of that bonus, as a hurt's modifier is shared (09-zero-hour.md, The import writes the template's numbers).
- **The panel shows the power bar.** The HUD file's `power_bar` gives the bar's images, its logarithm's base, its intervals over the bar's width and the margin under which it turns yellow; the client draws the given power as the bar and the used as its needle, green, yellow within the margin and red when short, as `W3DPowerDraw` draws them, and the import writes it from `GameData.ini`'s `PowerBar*` and its mapped images. Refused: a linear bar, OpenRA's, which a mode may add as an option when it needs one.
- **A tag marks its unit.** The HUD file's `tag_marks` give a tag's tint, added to its unit's colors and faded in and out over a time, its looping icon over the unit's bars, and whether it stills its unit's animations, unless the unit's look keeps them, as `Drawable` tints a disabled object by `DARK_GRAY_DISABLED_COLOR` over 30 frames, draws the `Disabled` animation over its health bar and pauses a model whose draw does not set `AnimationsRequirePower = No`; the import marks `disabled` so. Refused: a mark of the disabled state alone, as Zero Hour's poisoned, irradiated and frenzied tints are marks of other tags.
- **A bot builds power while short.** The rules package's bot builds one of its faction's power plants, ahead of its base list, while its player is short and no power plant of its is under way, as `AISkirmishPlayer::processBaseBuilding` does; a player's `power_given` and `power_used` are script reads, as its supply is.

## Losses

- **Overcharge.** China's power plants have no Overcharge toggle, its bonus nor its drain.
- **Sabotage and disabled producers.** No saboteur cuts a player's power, and a power plant that another cause disables still gives, as no such cause is imported.
- **One tick late.** A shortage and a speed apply from the tick after the change, where the original applies them in its frame.
- **Rounding.** A speed is exact from the counts, where the original computes it in `f32`.
- **Sounds and EVA.** The disabled and re-enabled sounds and EVA's low-power warning, as the sound losses count.

## Checks

Each check states exact values, hand-computed in its test.

- The production tests:
  - A player's given and used power: a unit counts as it completes, not as a site; leaves as it dies or is captured, joining its new owner; counts while it rides and while it is sold; and counts again as a modifier changes its stat.
  - A player that falls short holds the shortage's modifier from the next tick, a unit of the affected tag gains its blocking tag and stops moving and attacking, and both go as power returns; a player that gives and takes nothing holds none.
  - A train of 150 ticks at `penalty = 1`, `min = 0.5`, `max = 0.8`: 150 ticks at full power; 187 at a share of 4/5 (150 / 0.8 = 187.5); 300 at none with `empty = "none"` and 150 with `full`; as the player's share goes from 0 to 1, one 160 ticks into its time ends in the next tick and one 100 ticks into it ends 50 ticks later; a research is unslowed when `slows` leaves it out.
  - A site of 150 ticks completes in 300 ticks at a share of 0, and one that reaches half at that speed when the share goes to 1 completes 75 ticks later; a predicting client's site grows alike from the count it receives.
  - A restore of a short player holds the modifier; a snapshot whose player holds it while not short fails its check.
- The import's tests give `AmericaPowerPlant` a power of 5 and its Control Rods' modifier 5 more, `AmericaPatriotBattery` −3 and the tag `powered`, the command center's radar upgrade the shortage's conflict and the Radar Van's none, the mode's `[power]` speed of 1.0, 0.5 and 0.8 with `empty = "none"`, and the HUD's power bar of base 7, 3 intervals and a margin of 5 and its mark of `disabled`.
- The Zero Hour check plays power: America builds a Patriot battery with no power plant, which stands disabled while its player is short, then a power plant, under which it fires; GLA trains a Rebel in 300 ticks.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert as America against the China builder bot builds a war factory, a supply center and two Patriot batteries on one power plant, sees the bar red, the batteries grey, still and silent and the minimap out, builds a second power plant and sees each come back; then as GLA trains a Rebel in 10 s.
