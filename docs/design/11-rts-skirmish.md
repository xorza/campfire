# Campfire — RTS skirmish

What the first half of Stage 7 adds so that a tiny RTS skirmish plays to its golden: box bodies, unit types in every package, group orders, and production's first cut, its supply, requirements, cancel, rally, construction and gathering. The rules live in the capability documents this one points to; this one gives the decisions, the skirmish mode that proves them, and what waits. The RTS mission, with region events, objectives, carry and save converters, is the second half of the stage, with a proposal of its own.

## Decisions

- **D1. A building's body is a box, in every system.** A unit type that does not walk, on a planar map, may have a box body in place of a circle: pathing, placement, collision and reach all read the box, as 0 A.D. keeps a structure as a static obstruction square while a unit stays a circle ([Space and map](04-capabilities/00-overview.md#space-and-map)). The other way, StarCraft II's, keeps a radius for reach and a footprint for pathing beside it; it costs less, and a range to a building's long side is then wrong by up to the difference between the two shapes. A box needs no second shape for any test, so every capability that measures reach measures the same building.
- **D2. A box turns to any angle, and its shape rounds once, as it spawns.** A box's angle is in degrees: a multiple of 90° turns its half edges exactly, and any other angle turns them by math's sine and cosine; each component of a half edge is then rounded once to a `Num`. The rounded half edges are the box: every test after reads them exactly, in wide integers, so the rounding is the shape's, and no test rounds again. 0 A.D. keeps a structure as its centre and two half-edge vectors in fixed point. Degrees, not radians, make the right angles a builder uses most exact, as no multiple of π/2 is a `Num`.
- **D3. Construction has three styles, chosen by the build action.** `alone`: the building grows by itself once placed, as StarCraft II's Protoss buildings do. `builder`: one builder stays for the whole time, and the building waits while it has none, as a Terran SCV builds. `builders = [...]`: many builders together, at the rate the table gives for their count, the last entry for any count past it, as Age of Empires and 0 A.D. speed a site with each builder, less for each one more. A table is exact, and it says any curve a mode wants: 0 A.D.'s n^0.7 has no exact form in fixed point, so a table states it as the mode rounds it, `["1", "1.62", "2.16", "2.64"]` up to four builders.
- **D4. One gatherer at a node; gatherers pass through each other.** A node takes one gatherer at a time. A worker that finds its node taken goes to the nearest free node of the same resource near it, or waits, as a StarCraft II worker moves to another mineral patch. Workers in the gather loop do not collide with each other, so the income does not depend on the order collision parts them in.
- **D5. Supply is derived, not a player resource.** A unit type costs supply and may provide it; a player's supply used is what its living units and its queued trains cost, and its cap what its complete providers give, within the mode's maximum. No script keeps it: a death, a cancel or a completion changes it through the units themselves, so it cannot drift from them. A train reserves its supply when it joins the queue, as it pays its whole cost then ([Production](04-capabilities/production.md#supply)).
- **D6. Every package may hold unit types that stand, each in its own scope.** An avatar's summon, a loadout's ward and a faction's units are unit types of their own packages, which only their packages' data and scripts name. The skirmish's factions stay in the mode's package; a faction as a package of its own, which a mode offers as a choice, waits ([Unit types](08-script-api.md#data-files)).
- **D7. One command orders a group, which shares one route.** An order names its units, so a group's move is one command, and the group plans one long route. Each unit keeps its place in the group when the goal lies outside the box of their positions, and goes to the goal itself when it lies inside, as StarCraft II's selection keeps its formation only for a move away from it ([Groups](04-capabilities/navigation.md#groups)). A wave of a spawn group shares its route the same way.
- **D8. A seen enemy body blocks a placement; a friendly or hidden one is moved out.** A placement fails where its box overlaps a wall, a static body, or the body of a walker whose team is not friendly to the builder's and that the builder's group sees. A refusal for a hidden walker would tell the builder it is there, so a hidden enemy is moved out as a friendly walker is, as the cost of telling nothing: a player may move a hidden enemy by building on it. A walker in the box goes, as the building spawns, to the nearest cell it may stand in, as a teleport places a unit, with no disjoint. Collision would push it out too, but a walker between the new box and another static body, closer than its width, would be pushed from one into the other on every tick; the grid's nearest cell is clear of all of them, so it lands once and stays.

## The skirmish mode

A test mode, `source/packages/test/modes/skirmish`, owned by the engine's tests: two players of one faction on a small map with a wall between them, a neutral team that holds the mineral nodes, and ground and air layers. It is not a game. Each capability it declares is one the [StarCraft row](04-capabilities/genres.md#target-games) needs: `combat`, `stats`, `projectiles`, `orders`, `navigation`, `vision` and `production`.

| Unit type | Body | What it proves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Town hall | Box, 5 × 5 m | A drop-off; trains workers; provides supply; a box turned at a placed angle |
| Worker | Circle | Gathers; builds in each of the three styles; passes through other gatherers |
| Mineral node | Box, 2 × 1 m | A node of a neutral team that depletes and despawns |
| Depot | Box, 2 × 2 m | Provides supply only once complete; built by one `builder`, at 45° |
| Barracks | Box, 3 × 3 m | Requires a depot; built by `builders`; trains soldiers; a rally point |
| Airfield | Box, 4 × 3 m | Requires a barracks; grows `alone`; trains a flyer |
| Soldier | Circle, ground | A weapon that reaches a box from its edge |
| Flyer | Circle, air | Flies over the wall and over ground units, which block no air body |

Its scripted match, one run as [one scripted match for each mode](02-engine-core.md#testing-and-diagnostics) asks, plays each rule in its own tick and asserts its event there: income arriving at a drop-off, exact by the trip; a worker sent on from a taken node, and one waiting when no free node is near; a node that runs out; the three styles, a builder that leaves and one that comes back, and many builders at the table's rates; a placement refused for a seen enemy, and a friendly walker and a hidden enemy moved out of a new box; a building seen with only its edge in sight; a cancel at each place and its refund; a train refused for supply and for a requirement, and accepted once the depot completes; a rally point that sends a worker to gather; a group move away from the group and one into it; the flyer crossing the wall; and the end when a player owns no building. The golden plays on every OS in CI, and each platform's verifier replays every platform's log.

## What waits

- **The RTS mission:** region events, `quests` objectives, carry and campaigns, save converters (Stage 7's second half).
- **Order queues** (shift-click) and the `hold` order; formations, flow fields and ORCA crowds (Stage 17).
- **A faction as its own package**, and the `choices` offer that lists them (D6).
- **Gas:** a building placed on a node, as a refinery is on a geyser; creep and power fields beyond the `near` and `away` rules.
- **What each client receives of production**: no client receives queues, supply, loads, a site's progress, a node's amount or player resources until Stage 8 sends them ([Production](04-capabilities/production.md#network)). A box body replicates with this stage, so the client draws it.
- **A client to play it:** the build placement, the gather and rally clicks and the queue's buttons, with the reference game's client (Stage 10). The client draws box bodies from this stage on, so a box is never shown as a circle.
