# Entries and blocking

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish orders a group of soldiers into a Humvee, an Overlord's bunker or a Helix and sees each walk straight to it and enter, the container standing where it stood, or driving where its own order sends it with the soldiers following it; and sends columns through gaps, corridors and each other's way and sees each unit keep its line and wait behind the unit ahead, with no unit sliding sideways or turning back and forth. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Entries and blocking; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/navigation.md` for the walk into a unit and the blocking, `04-capabilities/interaction.md` for the entry's walk, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import's movers keep, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's portable structures and entries](../research/zero-hour-portable-structures.md) and [Crowd movement](../research/crowd-movement.md).

## Decisions

- **A walk may go into a unit.** A capability may walk a unit into another, by `WalkItem::to_unit`, a goal of the `units` contract that names the unit and holds its place, beside a walk to a point; an entry's approach walks so into its container, as `AIEnterState` sets the container as the rider's ignored obstacle (`ignoreObstacle`) and its goal to the container's place. Refused: a walk to the container's place alone, today's, under which navigation takes the container for any unit on the way; a separate part that names the ignored unit beside `Destination`, which a capability that walks to a point could leave set.
- **A walker passes through the unit it walks into.** Navigation treats the unit a walk goes into as no unit of the walker's: no step aside of it, no blocker of the walker's short route, no price or wall of its cells on the walker's long route or its straight line, no crowd at the walker's goal, and no contact of collision between the two, either way, as Zero Hour's `checkForMovement`, `moveAllies` and `PhysicsBehavior::onCollide` skip the ignored obstacle; the bodies may overlap while the walk lasts, and collision parts them once it ends, as a rider in reach stands for its windup. Refused: a container that stands firm while a rider walks to it, under which riders still steer round it and arrive short of it at its personal space; a container that waits for its riders, which Zero Hour's transports do not do; a pass through that lasts the windup, which would put the cast's target into collision for an overlap that only a container driving into its rider within a tick leaves, as the reach is measured from the container's body.
- **A walk into a unit follows it.** The walk's capability gives it the unit's place each tick, as an approach aims at its target each tick; the route's last waypoint moves there while the line from the waypoint before is clear, and the walker's progress toward it grows by each such move, the farthest the goal may have gone, so a walker that keeps pace with a unit that drives away is never stuck; past a line that is not clear, the route is planned again only when the unit stands more than a tenth of the walker's distance from the route's goal, as one walk, keeping the walker's progress, as `AIInternalMoveToState` plans again by `isSamePosition`. The rule of progress serves every goal that moves, a chase's too. Refused: a new route at each move whose line is not clear, today's, which restarted the walker's progress each tick; a waypoint that moves counted as a new one, today's, under which a rider that kept pace with a humvee was stuck in 5 ticks; Zero Hour's least 10 frames between plans, as a walk is visited only as its goal moves, so a unit that stops within the wait would leave the walker on a route to where it was.
- **A walker that steps moves no unit that drives aside.** A walker moves aside only the idle friends its gait may move: one that steps, a soldier, moves no unit that drives, a vehicle, and steers round it, unless its route goes through a cell a unit of its team stands in or it is stuck, as Zero Hour's `moveAllies` lets infantry push a vehicle only on a path blocked by an ally, a path through a cell an ally that stands holds (`getBlockedByAlly`), and a blocked unit that is stuck plans its path again (`m_isBlockedAndStuck`); the planner says of each route whether it goes through one, and the route keeps it. The Zero Hour check's entry play measured a Troop Crawler and an Overlord pushed off their places by soldiers that walked past them to another container; under this rule the Overlord keeps its place and the crawler is pushed by the soldiers whose routes go through it. Refused: a soldier that never pushes a vehicle, under which the transport, combat and garrison plays never ended and the bases play stalled 1,611 times; the route's exception alone, under which the combat and garrison plays never ended either; the stuck exception alone, under which the bases play stalled 860 times; a price of a vehicle as a unit a soldier may not move, which Zero Hour's equal price of every ally does not make; a rule by weight, under which a Humvee could not move an Overlord, which Zero Hour lets it; the mode's tag filters of who moves whom, a configuration for a rule the gaits already hold (04-capabilities/navigation.md, Who may move whom is the game's).
- **A walker brakes behind a unit that blocks it.** A walker is blocked by a unit whose body its step would reach before it turns at its next waypoint, within 45° of its way, 33.69° for a unit that stands, the cone of tangent 2/3 that stands for Zero Hour's 33.75° and is tested exactly; its step, or a mover's speed, is cut so that it ends touching that unit, on by the step that unit is to take along the way, the one its own brake let it take the tick before or its speed, none for one that comes toward it, as `blockedBy` and `calculateMaxBlockedSpeed` brake a Zero Hour mover to the speed of the one ahead. The walkers' brakes read the units as the Move stage starts, so the order they move in changes none of them; a walker braked behind a unit that moves counts its stuck ticks by the step it was let take, and one braked to a stop by its whole step, so it is stuck. Refused: the push of two walkers within their personal space, which slid a crossing walker 2.9 m across its way with its facing fixed; 0 A.D.'s push damped by pressure and a least push, which still pushes along the centers, so it narrows the slide and keeps it; velocity obstacles, ORCA's or DetourCrowd's sampled velocities, a linear program or dozens of samples a unit a tick, for units that also turn at a rate; each walker's walk of the tick before kept as state, which, sent to a predicting client each tick, rolled it back for each bit a waypoint's snap left between it and the server.
- **Walkers that step pass those that step another way (planned).** Of two walkers that step, one brakes the other only while the two face the same way, the dot of their facings over a quarter, and collision parts no pair of them that does not, so soldiers that cross pass through each other a moment, as Zero Hour's `blockedBy` takes infantry as blocked by infantry only so and lets the rest walk through; the bases play's Angry Mob stood stuck 555 times of its 1 s samples, its members braked by one another. Refused: soldiers kept apart and braked by any walker in their cone, which jams a crowd that mills; the brake so with collision parting every pair, which slides crossing soldiers sideways round each other, the wobble this slice ends. It replaces the refusal of kinds that pass through each other (04-capabilities/navigation.md, Who may move whom is the game's).
- **A unit outside the cone neither blocks nor pushes.** A unit beside a walker or behind it neither brakes it nor takes a push from it, as Zero Hour applies no force between two units that move on the ground; collision parts an overlap of two walkers by moving the walker whose facing holds the other within 45°, and shares one that neither or both hold, a graze of hundredths of a meter that two walkers closing in at a slant leave. Refused: an overlap shared by weight whichever went into the other, which moved the walker that did not walk in; a brake on any unit the step would reach, whatever its bearing, under which a walker that rounding left a bit inside a unit beside it would never step on.
- **A head-on block steps the lower priority aside.** Of two walkers that each brake the other to a stop, each in the other's cone, the one of the lower weight, then of the higher stable id, steps aside to the right of its way by the two radii, by a waypoint at the head of its route, or to its left where the walls or a static body leave no room on its right, and walks on to its own goal, as `processCollision` moves the lower path priority away, `hasHigherPathPriority` ranking a vehicle over a soldier and the lower id first of two that face each other, and walkers here pass on the right. Refused: the stuck walker's short route that counts each walker it touches half their reach to its left, a guess at where the other goes that steers round a walker the lower priority now moves; Zero Hour's pass through after a second blocked, which leaves bodies inside each other (04-capabilities/navigation.md, Who may move whom is the game's).
- **No personal space between walkers.** With no push, two bodies' radii alone measure how near a walker comes another, so a walker stuck by a crowded goal is one within the two radii and its step of a unit that stands, as its goal is; collision still looks for contacts within 5/4 of a body's radius, as a pass may move a body into one it did not touch. Refused: personal space kept for the crowded goal, which measures by a gap no unit keeps any more.

## Checks

Each check states exact values, hand-computed in its test, and each crowd case also fails on any overlap past collision's slack in any tick and on a facing that turns away from the walker's way and back.

- The units tests keep a walk into a unit through `Walk::into` and drop it with a walk to a point or a stop.
- The interaction tests:
  - Enter three riders at once into an idle container that drives, each of its places one: the container's place never changes, and each rider enters, in the ticks the test computes.
  - Enter a rider into a container that a block of idle friends rings: the friends step aside, the container stays, and the rider enters.
  - Enter a rider into a container that drives away round a wall: the rider follows, its route planned again only as the container leaves the line from the waypoint before more than a tenth of the rider's distance, never without a goal and never stuck, and enters once in reach.
  - Enter a rider that keeps pace with a container that drives away: never stuck.
  - Enter a rider into a container that drives toward it: the rider enters in the tick the test computes, and the container drives on to its goal.
- The navigation tests walk a walker into an idle friend on its way, which stays where it stands until the walk ends, a client predicting the walk alike, then part the two; collision parts no walker from the unit it walks into, either way; a crowd's price leaves out the cells of the unit a walker walks into alone; a route serves a goal that moved at most a tenth of the walker's distance; and a walker's progress grows by each move of its goal.
- The navigation tests:
  - The cone's bounds, exactly: a unit at 45° from the way blocks when it walks and not when it stands, one of tangent 2/3 blocks when it stands, and one a bit past either does not; a brake stops at the touch, on by the unit's step along the way, none for one that comes toward the walker; the unit a walk goes into, one of another layer and one passed through block nothing; of two head on, the lighter, then the higher id, steps aside.
  - Three walkers abreast and touching, walking the same way: each keeps its line to the bit; a walker that touches a unit beside its line as it passes walks as alone.
  - A walker behind a slower one on the same line: it brakes to the slower one's step from the tick it touches it, and the slower one walks as alone.
  - Two columns of three crossing at right angles: each walker keeps its line to the bit, the first column arrives as alone and the second waits.
  - A column of four through a gap 1.5 m wide: in its order, 4 ticks apart, none stepping back.
  - Two walkers head on in the open: the higher id steps 1 m to its right and both arrive; in a lane one body and a half wide, it steps into an alcove, and with none both stop.
  - A walker whose goal lies just past a unit that stands reaches it; one whose goal lies inside the unit's reach arrives short at the nearest cell; a crowded goal lies within the two radii and the step, both bounds in.
- The Zero Hour check plays China's infantry into a Troop Crawler, a Helix and an Overlord's bunker, each entering up to its places with the container's place unchanged, and its bases and attack plays end, their stalls measured against today's.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert as China against the America builder bot puts a group of soldiers in a standing Troop Crawler and in a Troop Crawler that drives, as China holds no Humvee, and sends two groups across each other's way and through the gap between two buildings, and sees no unit slide or turn back and forth.
