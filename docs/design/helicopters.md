# Helicopters

A slice's proposal. At its end, a USA player of a Zero Hour skirmish on Tournament Desert trains a Chinook that flies to a supply dock and brings its boxes home, and a Comanche that flies over cliffs and buildings and fights; only weapons that hit the air reach them in flight, and a Comanche shot down spirals, hits the ground and blows up. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Helicopters; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/navigation.md` for flight, `04-capabilities/stats.md` and `04-capabilities/combat.md` for the airborne tag and its targets, `04-capabilities/production.md` for the flying gatherer, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's helicopters](../research/zero-hour-helicopters.md).

## Decisions

- **A flier holds a height.** A unit type's `flight` gives its height above the ground, its climb in meters a second and its airborne height; each tick after the walk, its height in the world moves toward the sim's ground under it plus its height at its climb, never below the ground, so it lifts off its pad and rises over a cliff at that rate, as OpenRA moves an aircraft's altitude at its `AltitudeVelocity`. Refused: Zero Hour's lift and gravity, a vertical physics no ground unit has, for a motion a player barely tells from a fixed rate; a height locked to the ground, which steps at every cliff edge.
- **A flier walks in the plane.** Its route, its steps and its arrival are planar on its layer, and the flight alone writes its height, so a waypoint on the ground never pulls it down.
- **Airborne is a tag of height.** A flier holds the engine's tag `air_target` while its height above the ground passes its airborne height, granted and taken as it crosses it, as Zero Hour marks `OBJECT_STATUS_AIRBORNE_TARGET` and OpenRA grants its `AirborneCondition`; a weapon picks air or ground targets by it; its name says what it serves, as `airborne` is a game's own word, the test MOBA's knock-up. Refused: the tag of the type's layer, fixed per type, under which a helicopter on its pad is immune to ground weapons.
- **Fliers share a layer.** A flier's body lies on the mode's air layer, so fliers collide and part with each other and pass over ground units, walls and buildings, as Zero Hour pushes airborne objects apart in 3D and never meets a ground unit below.
- **Range stays flat.** A weapon reaches a flier by its planar range, as Zero Hour measures every attack's range in 2D, and its projectile flies to the flier's volume at its height.
- **A flying gatherer docks over its dock.** A gatherer that flies docks within its reach of the dock's box on the ground between them, by the planar metric every reach of a planar map takes, at its own height, as Zero Hour places a flier over its dock's center and keeps its height there.
- **The import flies a hovering locomotor.** An object whose normal locomotor flies on `AIR` with the `HOVER` appearance becomes a flier on the rules package's `air` layer, of its locomotor's speed and turn rate, its `PreferredHeight` its height and its `AirborneTargetingHeight` its airborne height; its climb is the mean speed of the original's climb from the ground to that height by its `Lift` and gravity, so it reaches it in the original's time.
- **A weapon's anti flags are its targets.** A weapon of `AntiGround` and `AntiAirborneVehicle` targets every enemy, of one of them the enemies that are not, or are, `air_target`, and of neither is dropped.
- **A helipad is an exit.** An airfield's `ParkingPlaceBehavior` gives its trains of units `PRODUCED_AT_HELIPAD` an exit at its model's `HeliPark01` bone, as the original makes them there.
- **What the slice leaves.** Jets, of the `WINGS` appearance, which need parking, runways and reloads; the Chinook's and the Helix's transport; the drones vehicles spawn; a hover's tilt by its speed and turn, which a later slice draws with a tank's suspension; the taxi locomotor; countermeasures; the Helix's upgrades; each counted as a loss.

## Checks

- Navigation's tests climb a flier from the ground to its height at its rate, ease it over a step of the ground and never below it, walk it in the plane, part two fliers, and pass one over a ground unit and a wall.
- The tests give and take `air_target` as a flier crosses its airborne height, and show a ground weapon that selects a flier only on its pad and an air weapon only in flight.
- A flying gatherer's test gathers a full load at a dock and brings it home at its height.
- The import's tests convert a hovering locomotor's flight, its climb from its lift, a weapon's anti flags, an airfield's helipad exit and the Chinook's gather, and count each loss.
- The Zero Hour check's play brings a Chinook's boxes to a USA player's money and shoots a Comanche down with an air weapon, its ticks pinned.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert sees a Chinook fly to a supply dock and back, and a Comanche fly over a cliff, fight, and fall when shot down.
