# Riders and upgrades

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish orders any of its soldiers into a Helix and into an Overlord's battle bunker and sees each walk to it and enter, following it as it drives; buys one of the Overlord's and the Helix's three upgrades, and no other once it holds one; and sees its carrier fire the Gattling cannon's guns at ground and air and the Helix's riders fire from its bunker. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Riders and upgrades; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/actions.md` for the approach, `04-capabilities/interaction.md` for the entry and the riders' fire, `04-capabilities/production.md` for the research, `04-capabilities/navigation.md` for the landing, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's portable structures and entries](../research/zero-hour-portable-structures.md).

## Decisions

- **An approach that cannot reach ends.** A cast whose walk to its target arrived short of it ends its order, as a build's and a gather's walk give up by `gives_up`, and as Zero Hour's `AIInternalMoveToState` fails when its path does, so the unit stands idle and takes the next order. Refused: today's order that stays approaching with no walk, which a player sees as a unit that ignored its order.
- **A flier lands where Zero Hour's transport lands.** A flier that lands for its riders takes the flight goal `land`, under which navigation walks it first to the first point of rings about where it hovers at which a circle of the mode's probe clears the walls, the static bodies and the ground units, rings the mode's ring apart and sampled as `findPositionAround` samples them, from 0° each way; then to the first cell of the mode's spiral about that point a walker of its radius stands in clear of the ground units, as `adjustToLandingDestination` walks its cells, or to the point itself when none is; and it climbs while it walks. Navigation finds the point once, as the landing begins, and holds it in the goal, `LandAt`, so a unit that walks under the flier after, its rider among them, moves it no more, as Zero Hour's landing state takes its point once, in `onEnter`; it finds one again only once the flier hovers away from the point, at no arrival there; a rider follows a container that walks, as an approach aims at its target each tick. Zero Hour's rules give a probe of 5, rings 5 apart and 400 cells, as `tryPosition` tests a sphere of 5; a mode of none takes the flier's radius, the pathing grid's cell and every cell. Refused: the nearest cell a walker of its whole radius stands in, the slice's first rule, under which China's Helix at its base start landed 245 away where Zero Hour's lands about 25 away ([Zero Hour's portable structures and entries](../research/zero-hour-portable-structures.md)); a landing where the flier stands, under which a Helix over its airfield lands where no rider reaches; Zero Hour's random start angle, a draw of the logic's random stream that a ring's first clear point serves as well; Zero Hour's search out to 100 times the flier's radius, which the grid's extent bounds here; a search again each tick the ground under the flier is not clear, under which the flier flew off as its rider came under it.
- **A conflicting upgrade is refused.** A research is refused, and its button dimmed, while its holder holds or researches a modifier that the upgrade it triggers conflicts with, read from the type's upgrades, as `CommandSetUpgrade` swaps the carrier's set for one of no other upgrade. Refused: a research that pays for an upgrade its conflict then withholds, today's; a command set for each upgrade, which the panel would hold for one game.
- **A portable structure is a part of its carrier.** The import folds a portable structure that an upgrade makes inside its carrier into the carrier: the upgrade gives the carrier the structure's creation tag, its first trigger suffixed `created`, and the structure's starting weapons join the carrier's slots past its own, named for the structure and armed while the tag holds; its model is drawn at its bone (A model may be drawn at another's bone), and the propaganda tower's heal is an aura (A propaganda tower is an aura). Refused: a structure as a unit of its own riding its carrier, the original's, which needs a container of a container (04-capabilities/interaction.md, A bunker is an entry a tag opens).
- **A rider's fire follows its container's tags.** A container's `fire` may name `when`, the tags its unit holds for its riders to fire, which the fire pass tests each tick, as `PassengersFireUpgrade` lets the Helix's riders fire once its bunker is bought. Refused: a rider's fire fixed as it enters, today's, under which a rider that entered before the upgrade never fires.
- **A pool regains a share of its maximum.** A pool's `regen_share` names a stat of the share of its maximum it regains a second, besides its `regen`, rounded down and carried as regen is, so a propaganda tower's aura heals each unit 1 % of its own most life a second, as `PropagandaTowerBehavior` heals `HealPercentEachSecond` of the unit's maximum. Refused: a heal by a script each pulse, beside the aura's pass that already holds the modifier; a regen scaled by a param, which reads its source's stats, the tower's, not the healed unit's.
- **A held modifier may hold from one source.** A modifier's `held_sources = "one"` keeps, of the holds a carrier takes of it in a tick, the lowest source's alone, so two towers heal a unit once, as `attemptHealingFromSoleBenefactor` takes one benefactor and Dota's auras of one name do not stack. Refused: an instance from each source, under which every tower in reach adds its heal.
- **A propaganda tower is an aura.** The import gives an object of `PropagandaTowerBehavior` its aura, of its `Radius`, on its allies but the structures, of its heal, as an upgrade of no trigger, held while the unit is no site, for a building, as `PropagandaTowerBehavior::update` skips a tower under construction, and as its upgrade's modifier for a portable tower, and adds the upgraded heal past the base while its player holds `UpgradeRequired`; the tower's rate of fire, `ENTHUSIASTIC` and `SUBLIMINAL`, adds to every weapon's rate, as `WeaponBonus::appendBonuses` adds a bonus less 1. Refused: the pulse's scan every `DelayBetweenUpdates`, under which a unit takes the heal for up to 2 s after it leaves, which an aura of no linger does not keep (04-capabilities/stats.md, Auras with no linger).
- **A model may be drawn at another's bone.** A state's model may name `bone`, a node of the state's first model, and the client stands it where that node stands each frame, its host's animation and turret turn applied, at its own scale alone, as Zero Hour's `W3DDependencyModelDraw` draws a portable structure at its container's `AttachToBoneInContainer`, whose bone transform is rigid, a container's scale moving its pivots alone (`HTreeClass::Scale`); the import adds each portable structure's default model to its carrier's states at that bone, hidden from its root down but while its carrier holds its upgrade's tag. Refused: a model merged into its carrier's file by the import, which writes a second copy of each carrier's model; a reparent of the model under the node, which its host's despawn would take with it from under its draw.
- **A carried model's barrels are its carrier's.** The import gives the carrier's states the barrels of each weapon slot its structure's weapons join, from the structure's model, and the carried model hides what the structure's state hides, its barrels' flashes among them; a state's barrels name the place of their model among its models, under whose node the client finds a barrel's bone and flash, and the carrier's unit launches each of those slots' projectiles from the structure's launch bones placed at its carrier's bone at rest, at the structure's scale, as Zero Hour's cannon fires and flashes from its own model's `Muzzle` and `MuzzleFX` bones (`W3DModelDraw::handleClientRecoil`). Refused: today's carried model shown whole, whose flashes show at all times and whose shots play at the carrier's own `Muzzle01`, the first node of that name under its drawing.
- **What the slice leaves.** A portable structure's own life, under which the original's cannon dies apart from its carrier, and its own turret, which aims apart from the carrier's: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The actions tests end a cast whose walk arrives short of its target.
- The interaction tests land a flier over a static body at the nearest point clear of it, walk a rider into a container that drives away, end the walk of one to a container walled off, and fire a rider once its container's tag holds.
- The production tests refuse a research whose upgrade conflicts with a modifier its holder holds or researches.
- The import's tests give a carrier its structure's weapons under its upgrade's tag past its own slots, give a transport its riders' fire under its passengers' upgrade's tag, and keep the upgrades' conflicts.
- The Zero Hour check plays China's infantry into a Helix and into an Overlord's bunker, every one entering up to its places, and its bots' match still ends.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert as China against the America builder bot puts soldiers in a Helix and an Overlord's bunker, follows a moving transport, buys the Overlord's Gattling cannon and sees the Overlord fire at ground and air with it, is refused a second upgrade, and sees the Helix's riders fire from its bunker.
