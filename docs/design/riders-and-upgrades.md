# Riders and upgrades

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish orders any of its soldiers into a Helix and into an Overlord's battle bunker and sees each walk to it and enter, following it as it drives; buys one of the Overlord's and the Helix's three upgrades, and no other once it holds one; and sees the Gattling cannon, the propaganda tower or the bunker stand on its carrier, the cannon firing at ground and air, the tower healing the units round it and the Helix's riders firing from its bunker. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Riders and upgrades; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/actions.md` for the approach, `04-capabilities/interaction.md` for the entry and the riders' fire, `04-capabilities/production.md` for the research, `02-engine-core.md` for the client's order, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's portable structures and entries](../research/zero-hour-portable-structures.md).

## Decisions

- **An approach that cannot reach ends.** A cast whose walk to its target arrived short of it ends its order, as an attack's chase gives up by `gives_up`, and as Zero Hour's `AIInternalMoveToState` fails when its path does, so the unit stands idle and takes the next order. Refused: today's order that stays approaching with no walk, which a player sees as a unit that ignored its order.
- **An entry walks into its container.** The approach of an entry ignores its container's body and aims each tick at the container's point, so its rider reaches a container that stands among others' bodies and follows one that drives, as `AIEnterState` ignores its goal as an obstacle and sets its goal to the container's position each frame. Refused: a walk to the container's point that stops at its body; a walk to the point the order found, which a container that drives leaves.
- **A conflicting upgrade is refused.** A research is refused, and its button dimmed, while its holder holds or researches a modifier that the upgrade it triggers conflicts with, read from the type's upgrades, as `CommandSetUpgrade` swaps the carrier's set for one of no other upgrade. Refused: a research that pays for an upgrade its conflict then withholds, today's; a command set for each upgrade, which the panel would hold for one game.
- **A portable structure is a part of its carrier.** The import folds a portable structure that an upgrade makes inside its carrier into the carrier: its weapons, its aura and its detection become the carrier's, held while the upgrade's tag holds, and its model joins the carrier's models at its bone (`AttachToBoneInContainer`), its nodes shown by the tag as a `SubObjectsUpgrade`'s are; the carrier's dummy weapon goes. Refused: a structure as a unit of its own riding its carrier, the original's, which needs a container of a container (04-capabilities/interaction.md, A bunker is an entry a tag opens); a model attached at run time, a feature of the pipeline for what the import can merge.
- **A rider's fire follows its container's tags.** A container's `fire` may name `when`, the tags its unit holds for its riders to fire, which the fire pass tests each tick, as `PassengersFireUpgrade` lets the Helix's riders fire once its bunker is bought. Refused: a rider's fire fixed as it enters, today's, under which a rider that entered before the upgrade never fires.
- **The client's order of an entry is tested.** Why the client sends no order into an Overlord whose bunker opened, where the server's check takes every soldier, is found by a test of a predicting client's world, and fixed where the client's world differs from the server's; the slice decides where once it is found.
- **What the slice leaves.** A portable structure's own life, under which the original's cannon dies apart from its carrier, and its own turret, which aims apart from the carrier's: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The actions tests end a cast whose walk arrives short of its target.
- The interaction tests walk a rider into a container that stands among static bodies and into one that drives away, and fire a rider once its container's tag holds.
- The production tests refuse a research whose upgrade conflicts with a modifier its holder holds or researches.
- The client's tests order a selected soldier into a container that its tag opened, in a predicting client's world.
- The import's tests give the Overlord and the Helix the Gattling cannon's weapons and the propaganda tower's aura under their tags, merge each structure's model at its bone, and refuse the conflicting researches.
- The Zero Hour check plays China's infantry into a Helix and into an Overlord's bunker, every one entering up to its places, and its bots' match still ends.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert as China against the America builder bot puts soldiers in a Helix and an Overlord's bunker, follows a moving transport, buys the Overlord's Gattling cannon and sees it fire, and is refused a second upgrade.
