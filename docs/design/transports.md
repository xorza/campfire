# Transports and garrisons

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish orders infantry into a Humvee, which its riders fire from as it drives; trains a Troop Crawler, which comes with its Red Guards; flies infantry and a vehicle in a Chinook, which lands to take and let out its riders; and garrisons a civilian building, which turns to their side and which its riders fire from at a longer reach; and lets riders out one by one or all at once from the panel. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Transports and garrisons; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/interaction.md` for entering, riding and leaving, `04-capabilities/combat.md` for a rider's fire, `04-capabilities/navigation.md` for a flier's landing, `02-engine-core.md` for the client's clicks and panel, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's transports and garrisons](../research/zero-hour-transports.md).

## Decisions

- **An entry is an action of the container's.** A container's type gives its `enter` section, which makes the `enter` order put its entry, an action of kind `enter`, in the rider's use slot, run through the casts' pipeline as a use is, its reach the container's body, as `interaction.md`'s One mechanism for using a unit plans and Zero Hour's `AIEnterState` walks a rider to its container. Refused: a system of its own for the walk and the reach, which the use's pipeline has.
- **A container says who rides.** Its `enter` gives its `capacity`, whether a rider counts its type's `size` or one, a filter of the tags it takes, and the time between two riders' exits, as `TransportContain` counts `TransportSlotCount` against `Slots`, `GarrisonContain` counts each rider against `ContainMax`, and OpenRA's `Cargo` weighs each `Passenger`; a type of no `size` rides nothing, as one of `TransportSlotCount = 0`.
- **A rider may enter its own player's container, or a free one.** It enters one of its player's, or one no other player's rider holds that is no faction's building, as `ActionManager::canEnterObject` refuses a container of another player's riders and another player's faction structure; a container past its `full` share of life, or dying, takes none.
- **A rider leaves the world and keeps its entity.** As it enters, a rider loses its body, so no grid, collision, scan or target finds it, its sight, and every audience but its owner's, and takes `Riding`, which keeps its container; its state, its stable id and its parts stay, as OpenRA's `Cargo` keeps its passengers' actors out of the world and Zero Hour removes an enclosed rider from its partition. Refused: a despawn and a spawn again, which lose a rider's state and its id; a rider left in the world under its container, which every system must then skip.
- **A container keeps its riders in order.** Its `Riders` hold their stable ids in the order they entered, state its owner receives, so its panel lists them and an exit lets the first out, as a contain list does.
- **A rider leaves at a free cell by the container.** An exit order of one rider, or an `evacuate` of all, lets each out a container's exit time after the one before, at the free cell nearest the container, leaving through its team's units as a trained unit leaves its exit (04-capabilities/navigation.md, A new unit leaves its exit), with its body, its sight and its audiences back. Refused: the exit paths of each container's bones, which the engine would place by the model of one game.
- **A container's fall hurts its riders.** As a container dies, each rider takes its `damage` share of its life maximum, of a kind no armor reduces, and dies at 100 %, and the survivors leave; as a container's life falls below its `full` share, its riders leave, as `OpenContain::onDie` and `GarrisonContain::onBodyDamageStateChange` do.
- **A free building takes its riders' side.** A container of `capture` takes the owner of its first rider while it holds one and goes back to the owner it had once empty, an insert of `Owner` each time (10-replication-audiences.md, An owner changes by an insert alone), as `GarrisonContain::recalcApparentControllingPlayer` sets the building's team.
- **A container may come full.** Its `payload` gives the riders it spawns with, inside, as `InitialPayload` and OpenRA's `InitialUnits`.
- **A container may let its riders fire.** Its `fire` is `none`, `center`, from which riders of a filter fire as from the container's place, or `points`, places of its frame from which each rider fires, the free one nearest its target as its target changes, as `TransportContain` lets infantry fire and `GarrisonContain::trackTargets` moves a rider to its nearest fire point; a firing rider scans and attacks as an idle unit does, from that place, and takes no order but its exit.
- **A container's modifier holds on its riders,** as the weapon bonus condition `GARRISONED` gives Zero Hour's riders 133 % range and 125 % damage; an attack's `range_bonus` takes a list of stats, as `damage_bonus` and `rate_bonus` do (generals.md, A weapon's bonus is a sum of stats). Refused: a garrison's bonus as a field of the container, which a modifier already says.
- **A container holding riders holds a tag,** the mode's `holding` tag of its `enter`, which looks test, as `MODELCONDITION_GARRISONED`, and a scan's filter may select, as a garrisoned building that can attack is a target of Zero Hour's scan.
- **A flier lands for its riders.** A flying container that stands with a rider walking to it or waiting to leave takes the ground as its flight goal, takes and lets riders only on the ground, and climbs again once none waits, as `ChinookAIUpdate` lands and takes off. Refused: riders that enter a flier in the air, which Zero Hour's Chinook does not take.
- **The client orders entries by the right click.** A right click on a container the selection may enter orders each selected unit that may enter it in, as a click on a node gathers; a rider is not drawn; the panel of a container shows a button of each rider, its type's icon, that lets it out, and the command `evacuate`, at the places its look gives.
- **What the slice leaves.** Exit paths and doors, a rider's heal inside, the fire points of a damaged building, station points, the Overlord's and the Helix's bunkers, tunnels, the combat cycle's rider, parachutes, the hospital's and the Internet Center's riders, combat drops, a garrison's clearing, a stealthed garrison's look to its enemies, and a bot that rides or garrisons: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The interaction tests enter a rider in reach and none past its capacity, its filter or its rule of whose container; keep its stable id, state and parts while it rides and give it nothing to see, hit or scan; let one rider and all out, each its exit time apart, at a free cell; deal a dying container's riders their share and let the survivors out; let them out below `full`; give a free building its first rider's owner and back; spawn a payload inside; and keep riders over a save and a restore; the leak test holds a rider to its owner.
- The combat tests fire a rider from its container's center and from the fire point nearest its target, with its container's modifier, and refuse it every order but its exit; the navigation tests land a flier for a rider that walks to it and lift it once none waits.
- The import's tests give the Humvee, the Troop Crawler, the Chinook and a civilian building their `enter`, each rider its `size`, a garrison its fire points from its `GARRISONED` model's `FIREPOINT` bones, the mode its garrison modifier, and the Humvee its panel's exit and evacuate places; the Zero Hour check pins the counts, and plays of Rangers that enter a Humvee and fire from it as it drives, a Troop Crawler trained with its 8 Red Guards that die at 10 % as it falls, a Chinook that lands for a Crusader and a Ranger and lets them out across the map, and Rebels that garrison a civilian building, turn it theirs, outrange a Ranger and leave at 35 %; its bots' match still ends.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert right-clicks a Humvee with Rangers selected and sees them get in, fires from it, lets one out from the panel and evacuates the rest, and garrisons a building and sees it turn their colour.
