# Transports and garrisons

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish orders infantry into a Humvee, which its riders fire from as it drives; trains a Troop Crawler, which comes with its Red Guards; flies infantry and a vehicle in a Chinook, which lands to take and let out its riders; and garrisons a civilian building, which turns to their side and which its riders fire from at a longer reach; and lets riders out one by one or all at once from the panel. Its plan is `.notes/PLAN.md`'s Transports and garrisons; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `04-capabilities/interaction.md` for entering, riding and leaving, `04-capabilities/combat.md` for a rider's fire, `04-capabilities/navigation.md` for a flier's landing, `02-engine-core.md` for the client's clicks and panel, and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's transports and garrisons](../research/zero-hour-transports.md).

## Decisions

- **An entry is an action of the container's.** A container's type gives its `enter` section, whose `action` names its entry, an action of kind `enter`, which the `enter` order puts in the rider's use slot, run through the casts' pipeline as a use is, its reach the container's body, as `interaction.md`'s One mechanism for using a unit plans and Zero Hour's `AIEnterState` walks a rider to its container. Refused: a system of its own for the walk and the reach, which the use's pipeline has.
- **The `enter` kind resolves into the container** (decided 2026-10-28). A kind's section may give an effect of its own, which ends its action's `on_resolve` with no data writing it, and the `enter` kind's puts its rider in the container it reached when the container takes it. Refused: a listed effect `enter` that each entry's data must write, which an entry could forget; a pass of interaction's own over the resolved casts, which the casts' pipeline would have to tell.
- **A container says who rides.** Its `enter` gives its `capacity`, whether a rider counts its type's `size` or one, filters of the riders it takes, any of which takes one, and the time between two riders' exits, as `TransportContain` counts `TransportSlotCount` against `Slots`, `GarrisonContain` counts each rider against `ContainMax`, `isValidContainerFor` takes a rider of any kind of `AllowInsideKindOf` (decided 2026-10-28), and OpenRA's `Cargo` weighs each `Passenger`; a type of no `size` rides nothing, as one of `TransportSlotCount = 0`. Refused: one filter, whose tags all hold, which cannot say the Chinook's infantry or vehicle.
- **A rider may enter its own player's container, or a free one.** It enters one of its player's, or a container of `capture` that no player holds, by a rider or as its own, as `ActionManager::canEnterObject` refuses a container of another player's riders and another player's faction structure; a container at its `full` share of life or below, or dying, takes none, as Zero Hour's body is really damaged at its threshold's ratio and below it (`calcDamageState`).
- **A rider leaves the world and keeps its entity** (decided 2026-10-28). An entering rider takes `Riding`, its container's stable id, state whose hooks insert Bevy's `Disabled` and remove it, so every query that does not name it, of the grid, the scans, the targets, collision and vision, passes over the rider with no system changed, while its state, its stable id and its parts stay, as Bevy's entity disabling keeps an entity and its relationships and OpenRA's `RideTransport` takes its passenger out of the world into its `Cargo`; a rider's fire, its exit and its owner's views name it by `Allow<Disabled>`, and the state's copy names it already. Refused: a disabling marker of the engine's own, as `Unpredicted` is, or `Riding` itself, as bevy_replicon's visibility filters name `Allow<Disabled>` alone and unwrap a query that a marker of their own hides (decided 2026-10-28); removing a rider's body and sight, which each part added later must remember on its way in and out; a despawn and a spawn again, which lose a rider's state and its id; a rider left in the world under its container, which every system must then skip.
- **A rider's team holds it** (decided 2026-10-28). As a rider enters, its `SeenBy` becomes its own team, which vision leaves as it is while the rider is disabled, so its enemies' clients drop it and its allies' hide it by `Riding`. Refused: its owner alone, which needs a room of each player slot beside each team's and drops a rider from its team's spectators; the rooms it had as it entered, by which an enemy that saw it enter keeps receiving it.
- **A rider's timers wait and catch up** (decided 2026-10-28). A pass that finds no unit for a due entry drops it, so each timed index observes `Disabled`'s removal and pushes the unit again from its parts, as it pushes a unit a client starts to predict; a rider's cooldowns and modifiers' ends are ticks, so they pass inside and act on its first tick out. Refused: riders that tick inside, which every timed pass would name and refuse one by one; an exit event of interaction's own, which the sim cannot name.
- **A container keeps its riders in order.** Its `Riders` hold their stable ids in the order they entered, state of the data kind `riders`, which its owner receives and a mode may give others, so its panel lists them and an exit lets the first out, as a contain list does.
- **A rider leaves at a free cell by the container.** An order of the container, `exit` of one rider or `evacuate` of all, lets each out a container's exit time after the one before, a rider being out of the world, which no order of its own reaches, at the free cell nearest the container, leaving through its team's units as a trained unit leaves its exit (04-capabilities/navigation.md, A new unit leaves its exit), with its body, its sight and its audiences back. Refused: the exit paths of each container's bones, which the engine would place by the model of one game.
- **A container's fall hurts its riders.** As a container dies, every rider leaves and loses its `damage` share of its life maximum, of a kind no armor reduces, and one that lost all of it dies at the next deaths; as a container's life falls to its `full` share or below, its riders leave each its exit time apart, as `OpenContain::onDie` and `GarrisonContain::onBodyDamageStateChange` do.
- **A free building takes its riders' side.** A container of `capture` takes the owner of its first rider while it holds one and goes back to the owner it had once empty, an insert of `Owner` each time (10-replication-audiences.md, An owner changes by an insert alone), as `GarrisonContain::recalcApparentControllingPlayer` sets the building's team.
- **A container may come full.** Its `payload` gives the riders it spawns with, inside, as `InitialPayload` and OpenRA's `InitialUnits`.
- **A container may let its riders fire.** Its `fire` is `none`, `center`, from which riders of a filter fire as from the container's place, or `points`, places of its frame from which each rider fires, the free one nearest its target as its target changes, as `TransportContain` lets infantry fire and `GarrisonContain::trackTargets` moves a rider to its nearest fire point; a firing rider takes the core's mark `FiringRider`, by which the scan and the attacks name it beside the units in the world, while no search for a target finds it, and scans and attacks as an idle unit does from its place, taking no order, as it is out of the world (decided 2026-10-28). Refused: those systems naming `Riding`, a part of interaction, which combat and orders, below it, cannot name.
- **A garrison's riders see from it** (decided 2026-10-28). A container's `sight` lets its riders reveal the cells round their places, their own sight, as the core's mark `SeeingRider` lets vision name them beside the units in the world, as Zero Hour's `Object::look` lets a rider of a garrisonable container look and no other; the import gives it to each `GarrisonContain`. Refused: a rider that sees in every container, which reveals what a Humvee's riders do not; a building's own sight while it holds riders, which Zero Hour's garrisons, of `VisionRange = 0`, lack.
- **A container's modifier holds on its riders,** held each tick as another capability's hold, as the weapon bonus condition `GARRISONED` gives Zero Hour's riders 133 % range and 125 % damage, and stats derive a rider's stats out of the world; an attack's `range_bonus` takes a list of stats, as `damage_bonus` and `rate_bonus` do (generals.md, A weapon's bonus is a sum of stats), and stretches its range by 1 plus their sum wherever a reach is tested, by the actions' `RangeBonus` that combat books, as the scan and the chase know no weapon. Refused: a garrison's bonus as a field of the container, which a modifier already says.
- **A container holding riders holds a tag,** the engine tag `holding` of interaction, which looks test, as `MODELCONDITION_GARRISONED`, and a scan's filter may select, as a garrisoned building that can attack is a target of Zero Hour's scan (decided 2026-10-28). Refused: a mode's tag that each `enter` names, as a unit's status tags hold the engine's alone.
- **A flier lands for its riders.** A flying container that stands with a unit ordered to enter it or a rider waiting to leave takes the ground as its flight goal, takes and lets riders only on the ground, and climbs again once none waits, as `ChinookAIUpdate` lands and takes off; a unit whose entry finds it in the air waits on the ground and enters again once it lands, as the casts' pipeline walks a rider in reach in the plane (decided 2026-10-28). Refused: riders that enter a flier in the air, which Zero Hour's Chinook does not take.
- **The client orders entries by the right click.** A right click on a container the selection may enter, its own player's or a free one, orders each selected unit that may enter it in, as a click on a node gathers; a unit out of the world is neither drawn nor selected, as Zero Hour's rider leaves its group; the panel of a container shows at each place of the command `exit` the next of its riders, by its type's icon, which a look gives as Zero Hour's `ButtonImage` (`ControlBar::populateButtonProc`), and lets it out, and the command `evacuate` while it holds one, at the places its look gives, as the Humvee's `Command_TransportExit` and `Command_Evacuate` (decided 2026-10-28). Refused: a click that ignores the container's side, which would board an enemy's Humvee instead of attacking it; a button of each rider laid past the look's places, which a game lays at its own.
- **The import says who rides by tags, or drops the container** (decided 2026-10-28). A container's `AllowInsideKindOf` and `ForbidInsideKindOf` become filters of the mode's tags, `huge_vehicle` and `no_garrison` among them, a garrison refusing `NO_GARRISON` as `GarrisonContain::isValidContainerFor` does; a kind of no tag drops out only where no type that rides holds it without a tagged kind, the import testing each, else the container is a counted loss, as is one whose `Allow*Inside` no relation of the engine says. Refused: a tag for every kind a mask names, which no rider tells apart; a mask that drops a kind it cannot test, which takes riders the game refuses.
- **The import's entry reaches the pathfinder's cell** (decided 2026-10-28). Every container's entry reaches 10 m from its body, as a dozer docks in the free cell beside the building it builds (09-zero-hour.md, A gatherer docks as a dozer builds), since the pathfinder stops a walker at the cell beside a body, where Zero Hour's `AIEnterState` enters on contact. Refused: a reach of contact, which a walker blocked a cell away never closes.
- **A garrison that encloses no rider fires from its place** (decided 2026-10-28). A `GarrisonContain` of `IsEnclosingContainer = No`, whose riders fire from its `STATION` bones, lets them fire from its place, the station points a counted loss; one whose `GARRISONED` model names no `FIREPOINT` bone does too, as `loadGarrisonPoints` starts every point at the building's place.
- **What the slice leaves.** Exit paths and doors, a rider's heal inside, the fire points of a damaged building, station points, the Overlord's and the Helix's bunkers, tunnels, the combat cycle's rider, parachutes, the hospital's and the Internet Center's riders, combat drops, a garrison's clearing, a stealthed garrison's look to its enemies, and a bot that rides or garrisons: each a loss of Zero Hour.

## Checks

- The interaction tests enter a rider in reach and none past its capacity, its filter or its rule of whose container; keep its stable id, state and parts while it rides and give it nothing to see, hit or scan; let one rider and all out, each its exit time apart, at a free cell; deal a dying container's riders their share and let the survivors out; let them out below `full`; give a free building its first rider's owner and back; spawn a payload inside; and keep riders over a save and a restore; the leak test holds a rider to its owner.
- The combat tests fire a rider from its container's center and from the fire point nearest its target, with its container's modifier, and refuse it every order but its exit; the navigation tests land a flier for a rider that walks to it and lift it once none waits.
- The import's tests give the Humvee, the Troop Crawler, the Chinook and a civilian building their `enter`, each rider its `size`, a garrison its fire points from its `GARRISONED` model's `FIREPOINT` bones, the mode its garrison modifier, and the Humvee its panel's exit and evacuate places; the Zero Hour check pins the counts, and plays of Rangers that enter a Humvee and fire from it as it drives, a Troop Crawler trained with its 8 Red Guards that die at 10 % as it falls, a Chinook that lands for a Crusader and a Ranger and lets them out across the map, and Rebels that garrison a civilian building, turn it theirs, outrange a Ranger and leave at 35 %; its bots' match still ends.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert right-clicks a Humvee with Rangers selected and sees them get in, fires from it, lets one out from the panel and evacuates the rest, and garrisons a building and sees it turn their colour.
