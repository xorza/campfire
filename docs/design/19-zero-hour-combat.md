# Zero Hour combat

The first Zero Hour units that fight, and the first skirmish that ends: eight teams of one player, the original's life, weapons and armor, and its rule of defeat. The player starts Tournament Desert against an idle server bot, trains rangers, sends them to the bot's start, and destroys its command center; the bot's dozer dies with it, and the match ends with the player as the winner. It builds on [Combat](04-capabilities/combat.md), [Zero Hour production](18-zero-hour-production.md) and [Units that drive](17-units-that-drive.md). The facts it takes from are [Zero Hour research](../research/zero-hour.md#combat).

## Decisions

- **D1. A skirmish is eight teams of one.** The imported mode declares the teams `player_1` to `player_8`, each of one slot, hostile to each other, as a pair no relation names is; `neutral` keeps no slot. This is the original's default skirmish, every player alone against every other, up to its eight players; alliances chosen before the match wait for a lobby. The mode script places a start only for a slot a player or a bot holds as the match starts, as the original places none for an empty slot, through the new core read `ctx.held(player)`.
- **D2. An attack may give its damage and its period itself.** An `attack` action takes `amount`, a number, or one for each rank, in place of `damage`, the stat of its attacker; and `period_ms`, in place of `rate`, the stat of attacks a second; the load requires exactly one of each pair. StarCraft II holds a weapon's damage and period on the weapon, as Zero Hour does; the MOBA keeps its stats, which its items and levels raise. A period is whole ticks, rounded up, and longer than the windup, as the rate's is.
- **D3. An attack may fire in clips.** An `attack` action takes `clip = { shots, reload_ms }`: after the shot that empties its clip, its slot is ready again `reload_ms` after that shot in place of its period, and its clip is full again, as Zero Hour's `Weapon::reloadWithBonus` sets the next shot; a weapon of no clip fires without end. The slot's times count the shots its clip has left, state its owner's client predicts as it predicts the slot's ready tick. A stop, a move or a new target keeps the count, as the original's clip stays as it was.
- **D4. Armor is the combat capability's.** The mode declares its armors, `[combat] armors = { <name> = { <kind> = "<share>", ... } }`, each a share by damage kind, of any size not below 0, as 200 % is; a kind an armor does not name takes the damage whole. A unit type names its armor, `combat = { armor = "<name>" }`, none taking every damage whole. The damage pass multiplies each damage by its target's share of its kind, once, rounded down to a `Num`, before the mode's `calc_damage`, as Warcraft III's table of attack and armor types sits under its scripts. No script runs for it, and the load checks each name once.
- **D5. Life is the original's.** An object of an `ActiveBody` or a `StructureBody` has the life pool `life`, its maximum `MaxHealth` and its start `InitialHealth`, and `combat = { on_death = "despawn" }`, so it can be damaged and dies. An object of another body, `ImmortalBody`, `HighlanderBody`, `UndeadBody`, `HiveStructureBody` or `InactiveBody`, has no life yet, so no weapon targets it, and the import reports it. A site starts at the least life above 0, where the original's starts at 1.
- **D6. The weapons of one target that hit at once.** Each unit type's weapons are those of its weapon set of `Conditions = None`, `PRIMARY` then `SECONDARY` then `TERTIARY`. A weapon of no `ProjectileObject`, no `PrimaryDamageRadius`, no `PreAttackDelay`, no `MinimumAttackRange` and one `DelayBetweenShots`, not a `Min:` and `Max:` range, becomes the attack action `weapon_<name>`, once for the whole import, in the slot kind `weapon`: `targeting = "enemies"`, `range` its `AttackRange`, `amount` its `PrimaryDamage`, `damage_kind` its `DamageType`, of `EXPLOSION` when it names none, `period_ms` its `DelayBetweenShots`, and `clip` its `ClipSize` and `ClipReloadTime` when its size is above 0, each time as design 12's rule of times writes it, so each is the original's count of frames, rounded up as `parseShotDelay` and `parseDurationUnsignedInt` round it. The mode's damage kinds are the original's damage types, as the import's lowercase; its armors are `Armor.ini`'s, `DEFAULT` written as a share of each damage kind but `unresistable` and `subdual_unresistable`, which the original passes whole. Any other weapon is dropped, and the import reports it by cause, as it reports buttons: a projectile, a radius, a delay before its attack, a minimum range, or a random delay between shots.
- **D7. A player is defeated when its last building that counts for victory dies.** An object of both `KindOf` `STRUCTURE` and `MP_COUNT_FOR_VICTORY` has the tag `counts_for_victory`. In `on_unit_died`, when the dead unit holds the tag and its player holds no other living unit of it, the rules package's mode script holds the player out of play, `ctx.set_out`, and kills its other units, `ctx.kill` over `ctx.units_of(player)`, as the original's `Player::killPlayer` does; when the players in play that started are all of one team, it ends the match, `ctx.end`, that team the winner, as `VictoryConditions::update` marks the last alliance victorious. `ctx.units_of(player)` is the living units a player owns, by stable id, and `ctx.kill(unit)` kills a living unit at once, with no killer, both new calls of the core.

## Data

- **Mode** (`manifest.toml`, `data/mode.toml`): the capability `combat`; `teams` `player_1` to `player_8` of one slot each, and `neutral`; `[combat] damage_kinds`, the damage types in the order of the original's list, `life = "life"`, `armors`; the pool `life`; the tag `counts_for_victory`; `[[slots]] name = "weapon"` before `command`.
- **Unit types** (`data/units.toml`): `pools = { life = { max, start } }`, `combat = { on_death = "despawn", armor }`, `slots = { weapon = [...] }`, and `tags = ["counts_for_victory"]` where the object's kinds give it.
- **Actions** (`data/mode.toml`): `[actions.weapon_<name>]`, `kind = "attack"`, `targeting = "enemies"`, `range`, `amount`, `damage_kind`, `period_ms`, `windup_ms`, `clip`; each with its `name`, the weapon's own, as the load requires a name only of a train and a build.
- **Rules package** (`source/packages/zero-hour/scripts/mode.rhai`): a start for each held slot; `on_unit_died`, the defeat and the end.

## Losses

What the slice does not hold against the original:

- **Range.** The engine measures a weapon's range from the edge of each body, a box's from its box, where the original measures from each one's bounding circle, which holds a box's corners: a ranger stops nearer a building, up to 32 m nearer the command center, beside its long sides.
- **Weapon choice.** A unit attacks with its first weapon whose targeting selects the target, where the original picks the best of its weapons for each target.
- **Dropped weapons.** A weapon that fires a projectile object, damages a radius, waits before its attack (`PreAttackDelay`, of 29 weapons), keeps a minimum range (`MinimumAttackRange`, of 107) or waits a random delay between shots (4) is not imported: tanks, missiles, artillery, flame weapons and snipers do not fight yet.
- **Auto attack.** A unit attacks only what it is ordered to, where the original's idle units attack an enemy in their vision range.
- **Weapon sets.** Only the set of `Conditions = None` is imported, so an upgrade or veterancy does not change a weapon; `WeaponBonus` lines are not read.
- **Other bodies.** Objects of an immortal, highlander, undead, hive or inactive body have no life.
- **Deaths.** A unit that dies goes at once: no death animation, no rubble, no object a death creates, no death type.
- **Alliances.** Each player is its own team; the original's lobby can ally players.

## Checks

- Combat: an attack of `amount` deals that damage at each rank; one of `period_ms` fires once each period, rounded up to whole ticks; one of a clip of 3 shots 100 ms apart and a reload of 700 ms, at 30 ticks a second, fires in ticks 0, 3, 6, 27, 30, 33, 54, and a client predicts the same ticks; an armor of a share of 0.5 for one kind halves that kind's damage, rounded down, takes a kind it does not name whole, and applies before `calc_damage`; a unit type of no armor takes every damage whole; the load refuses an attack of both `amount` and `damage`, or of neither, an armor of an unknown kind, and a unit type naming an unknown armor; the goldens do not move.
- Core: `ctx.held` is true for a slot of a player or a bot and false for an open one; `ctx.units_of` gives a player's living units in stable id order; `ctx.kill` kills a living unit with no killer, and does nothing to a dead one.
- Import: a fixture's objects give their life, their armor, their weapons of the starting set in slot order with their numbers, and the tag of victory; a weapon of each cause is dropped by that cause; `DEFAULT` sets every kind but the two unresistable ones; the teams are eight of one slot.
- The Zero Hour check: the counts of weapons converted and dropped by cause, of armors and of damage kinds; on Tournament Desert against an idle second slot, player 1's rangers, ordered to attack player 2's command center, destroy it in their pinned ticks, player 2's dozer dies in the same tick, and the match ends with `player_1` the winner.
