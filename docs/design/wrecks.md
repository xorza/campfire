# Wrecks

A slice's proposal. At its end, a player of a Zero Hour skirmish sees a destroyed tank leave its burning hulk, which sinks and goes, throw its turret and its debris, which fly, bounce and sink, a destroyed helicopter or jet fall and blow up, and a tank drive with dust at its treads and lay track marks that fade, as the game's death objects and tank draws show them. Its plan is `PLAN.md`'s Wrecks; when the slice ends, its lasting decisions fold into `02-engine-core.md` for the formats and the client and `09-zero-hour.md` for what the import converts and loses, and this document goes. Its facts are [Zero Hour's deaths](../research/zero-hour-deaths.md).

## Decisions

- **A wreck is the client's.** A death's hulk and debris are drawn by the client alone, from the death it sees, as an effect is (effects.md, An effect is the client's, from the state it holds): Zero Hour's hulks are `NO_COLLIDE` and its debris `UNATTACKABLE`, so no rule reads them. Refused: sim entities, which every link would carry and every tick would run for nothing a rule reads, as OpenRA's husks and Spring's wreck features are, which a player can capture or reclaim.
- **A death names its wrecks by phase.** A look's death names, beside its effects, the wrecks each phase throws: a hulk, a model left where the unit stood, which falls with gravity, lives its time and sinks; and debris, one of its models drawn at random for each piece, thrown by a force, which bounce, rest and sink; each with its offset, count, launch and spin, as `CreateObject` and `CreateDebris` give them. The format names no game. The import reads `ObjectCreationList.ini`, converts an `OCL` of a slow death's phase whose objects are `HULK`s, with the hulk's model, its `LifetimeUpdate` and its own slow death's sink, and its `CreateDebris`, with `GenericDebris`'s sink, the launches `SEND_IT_FLYING`, `SEND_IT_UP`, `RANDOM_FORCE`, `SEND_IT_OUT`, `LIKE_EXISTING` and `ON_GROUND_ALIGNED`, a force over its mass as one step's change of velocity as `PhysicsBehavior::applyForce` takes it, at the import's length and time scales; it converts each model a wreck names as a look's, and counts a `FLOATING`, `WHIRLING` or `INHERIT_VELOCITY` launch, and an object of gameplay a creation list makes, a loss.
- **Wrecks move by the client's step.** A wreck steps at the effects file's step, as particles do (effects.md, A system steps as its file says), falls by the file's gravity, Zero Hour's −64 per second squared converted, bounces on the drawn ground, spins at its rate and rests, and draws from the client's own random sequence, as nothing of it is state. Refused: Avian or Rapier, a dependency for pieces that need only a fall and a bounce.
- **A tread throws dust while it drives.** A look names the particle systems its treads run while its unit moves on the ground, at a velocity and a count scaled by its speed, as `W3DTankDraw` scales them, by half the speed and a tenth up to 1 for the velocity across and by the speed and a tenth up to 1 for the count and the rise, and stops them as it stands, as a system on its drawing the effects already follow. The import keeps a look's tread systems in the effects file as it keeps a projectile's trail.
- **A tread lays track marks.** A look names a track texture; the client lays a strip of quads behind the unit as it moves, an edge each fixed distance, up to a fixed count, the oldest fading, drawn over the ground among its overlays, as `W3DTerrainTracks` lays them. The import converts each `TrackMarks` texture as it converts a road's.
- **An aircraft's death falls.** A look's death may name a fall: the dead unit's drawing falls from where it died by its type's share of gravity, rolls or spirals at its rates, plays its hit's effects as it meets the drawn ground and its final blow-up a time after, as Zero Hour's `JetSlowDeathBehavior` and `HelicopterSlowDeathBehavior` play them; a jet that dies on the ground plays its ground death. The import converts the two, their phases' effects and wrecks as a slow death's, and keeps the unit dead for the fall from its locomotor's `PreferredHeight` at its share of gravity and the time after it, so the unit stays until its blow-up. Refused: a fall in the sim, which moves a dead unit no rule reads; a battle bus's and a neutron missile's deaths, of objects the import does not write, counted with them.
- **What the slice leaves.** A slow death's weapons, a fling and a sink of the unit itself, an object of gameplay a creation list makes, a debris piece's bounce sound, and a truck's dust and dirt spray: each a loss of the import, counted.

## Checks

- The import's tests convert a slow death's hulk and debris of each disposition, a jet's and a helicopter's deaths and their keep, a tank draw's tread systems and its track texture, and count each loss; the Zero Hour check pins them.
- The client's tests step a wreck to hand-computed places: its fall, its bounce on the ground, its rest and its sink, and its end; an aircraft's fall to its hit and its blow-up; a tread's systems run as its unit moves and stop as it stands, scaled by its speed; a track lays its edges at their distance, fades the oldest and drops past its count.
- In the client, a player of Tournament Desert sees a destroyed Crusader's hulk sink and its turret fly, a destroyed Comanche spiral down and blow up, and a tank's dust and track marks as it drives.
