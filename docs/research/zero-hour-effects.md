# Zero Hour's effects

How Zero Hour shows a shot, a hit and a death, read on 26 October 2026: the game's code from [GeneralsX](https://github.com/fbraz3/GeneralsX) (`Core/GameEngine/Source/GameClient/FXList.cpp`, `Core/GameEngine/Source/GameClient/System/ParticleSys.cpp`, `Core/GameEngine/Include/GameClient/ParticleSys.h`, `Core/GameEngineDevice/Source/W3DDevice/GameClient/W3DParticleSys.cpp`, `GeneralsMD/Code/GameEngine/Source/GameClient/Drawable.cpp`, `W3DModelDraw::handleWeaponFireFX`), and the data of the install's `Data/INI/INIZH.big`.

## How the game makes them

- **An FX list is a list of nuggets.** `FXList.ini` names each list; a list holds nuggets of eight kinds: `ParticleSystem`, `Sound`, `Tracer`, `RayEffect`, `LightPulse`, `ViewShake`, `TerrainScorch` and `FXListAtBonePos` (`FXList.cpp`). A `ParticleSystem` nugget names a template and its `Count`, `Offset`, `Radius`, `Height`, `InitialDelay`, `RotateX`, `RotateY`, `RotateZ`, `OrientToObject`, `Ricochet`, `AttachToObject`, `CreateAtGroundHeight` and `UseCallersRadius`.
- **A particle system is a template.** `ParticleSystem.ini` names each; a template holds its `Priority`, `IsOneShot`, `Shader` (`ADDITIVE`, `ALPHA`, `ALPHA_TEST`, `MULTIPLY`), `Type` (`PARTICLE`, `DRAWABLE`, `STREAK`, `VOLUME_PARTICLE`, `SMUDGE`), its texture `ParticleName`, angles and angular rates and damping, `VelocityDamping`, `Gravity`, a `SlaveSystem` and a `PerParticleAttachedSystem`, `Lifetime`, `SystemLifetime`, `Size`, `StartSizeRate`, `SizeRate`, `SizeRateDamping`, eight alpha keys and eight color keys, `ColorScale`, `BurstDelay`, `BurstCount`, `InitialDelay`, `DriftVelocity`, an emission velocity (`ORTHO`, `SPHERICAL`, `HEMISPHERICAL`, `CYLINDRICAL`, `OUTWARD`), an emission volume (`POINT`, `LINE`, `BOX`, `SPHERE`, `CYLINDER`), `IsHollow`, `IsGroundAligned`, `IsEmitAboveGroundOnly`, `IsParticleUpTowardsEmitter` and a wind motion (`ParticleSys.cpp`, the template's field parse table; `ParticleSys.h`).
- **Who plays them.** A weapon plays its `FireFX` as it fires, at the bone its model's state names for the weapon's barrel (`WeaponFireFXBone`), or at its object's position when the state names none (`W3DModelDraw::handleWeaponFireFX`, `Weapon.cpp`); its `ProjectileDetonationFX` where its projectile detonates, or at its victim for a weapon of no projectile; its `ProjectileExhaust` is a particle system on its projectile. An object's `FXListDie` plays its `DeathFX` as it dies, for the death types and conditions its die module takes. A model's condition state names particle systems on its bones (`ParticleSysBone`), as a damaged building's smoke.

- **They step with the logic.** The particle systems update once each logic frame, 30 a second, so a rate, a damping and a key's time count logic frames (`ParticleSystemManager::update`).
- **A cap by priority.** A system that makes a particle past `MaxParticleCount` first removes as many of the oldest particles of a priority below its own, lowest first; an `ALWAYS_RENDER` particle passes every cap (`ParticleSystem::createParticle`, `ParticleSystemManager::removeOldestParticles`). `GameData.ini` sets 2,500, and the three detail levels of `GameLOD.ini` 500, 1,500 and 3,000; `MaxFieldParticleCount`, 30, caps an `AREA_EFFECT` system's field particles.

## In the install

- **Templates.** 1,084 particle systems: by type 1,049 `PARTICLE`, 30 `STREAK`, 4 `VOLUME_PARTICLE`, 1 `DRAWABLE`; by shader 587 `ADDITIVE`, 469 `ALPHA`, 26 `ALPHA_TEST`, 1 `MULTIPLY`, 1 none; by emission velocity 488 `OUTWARD`, 405 `ORTHO`, 80 `SPHERICAL`, 60 `CYLINDRICAL`, 50 `HEMISPHERICAL`; by emission volume 430 `CYLINDER`, 263 `POINT`, 204 `SPHERE`, 171 `LINE`, 15 `BOX`. 6 are one-shot, 145 ground-aligned, 178 hollow, 8 move with the wind. 60 name a slave system and 119 a system attached to each particle. They draw 81 textures.
- **Priorities.** 394 `CRITICAL`, 239 `WEAPON_EXPLOSION`, 154 `DEATH_EXPLOSION`, 60 `WEAPON_TRAIL`, 59 `UNIT_DAMAGE_FX`, 52 `AREA_EFFECT`, 40 `CONSTANT`, 34 `DUST_TRAIL`, 24 `SEMI_CONSTANT`, 18 `BUILDUP`, 5 `DEBRIS_TRAIL`, 5 `ALWAYS_RENDER`.
- **FX lists.** 430 lists, of 870 `ParticleSystem` nuggets, 321 `Sound`, 140 `ViewShake`, 65 `LightPulse`, 47 `TerrainScorch`, 18 `Tracer` and 5 `FXListAtBonePos`.
- **Weapons.** Of 360 weapons, 285 name a `FireFX`, 158 a `ProjectileDetonationFX`, 89 a `ProjectileExhaust`, 58 a `VeterancyFireFX` and 74 a `VeterancyProjectileExhaust`; 277 fire a projectile object, and 234 name a `FireSound`.
- **Objects.** 1,068 `FXListDie` modules, 698 `TransitionDamageFX`, 21,064 `ParticleSysBone` lines in model states.
- **The import's models keep the barrels' bones.** The imported `client/units.toml` names the bones `muzzlefx01` 2,309 times, `muzzlefx02` 1,081, `muzzlefx03` 575 and `muzzleakfx01` 504.
