# Zero Hour's angry mob

Read on 23 October 2026: Zero Hour from GeneralsX's source and the Steam install's `INIZH.big`, OpenRA's port from the CameoReloaded mod's `OpenRA.Mods.CA` source, and the Campfire match from the Zero Hour check's base play, probed every 60 s.

- **The nexus is an immortal leader of no harm.** `GLAInfantryAngryMobNexus` (`GLAInfantry.ini`) has an `ImmortalBody` of `MaxHealth` 99,999, the weapon `GLAAngryMobNexusHarmlessWeapon` in its three slots, the kinds `MOB_NEXUS`, `NO_COLLIDE` and `SELECTABLE`, a model of no mesh, the locomotor `AngryMobNexusLocomotor`, a `QueueProductionExitUpdate` and a `DestroyDie`; it costs 800 and takes 15 s.
- **The nexus keeps ten members.** Its `SpawnBehavior` has `SpawnNumber` 10, `SpawnReplaceDelay` 30,000 ms, seven `SpawnTemplateName`s taken in turn (`Pistol01`, `Rock02`, `Molotov02`, `Pistol03`, `Rock04`, `Molotov02`, `Pistol05`), `InitialBurst` 5, `ExitByBudding`, `AggregateHealth`, `SlavesHaveFreeWill` No and `OneShot` No.
- **A member is a weak soldier.** `GLAInfantryAngryMobPistol01` has an `ActiveBody` of 50, the weapon `GLAAngryMobPistolWeapon`, and under `PLAYER_UPGRADE`, the upgrade `Upgrade_GLAArmTheMob`, an AK-47; `AutoAcquireEnemiesWhenIdle`; a `MobMemberSlavedUpdate` of `MustCatchUpRadius` 40, `NoNeedToCatchUpRadius` 15, `Squirrelliness` 0.05 and `CatchUpCrisisBailTime` 30; the kinds `SELECTABLE` and `IGNORED_IN_GUI`; a cost of 100.
- **The spawner makes and replaces its members.** `SpawnBehavior::update` makes each member as soon as it can, the first `InitialBurst` through the door of the barracks that made the nexus, each later one at the member nearest the nexus (`ExitByBudding`); a member that dies comes back `SpawnReplaceDelay` later (`onSpawnDeath`); with `AggregateHealth`, the last member's death destroys the nexus, and its killer scores the kill of the nexus (`GeneralsMD/.../SpawnBehavior.cpp`).
- **The nexus's health is its members'.** `SpawnBehavior::computeAggregateStates` sets the nexus's health each frame to the sum of its members' health over that of a full mob at full health, shares one selection between the nexus and every member, and moves each member's veterancy to the nexus.
- **A member follows its nexus.** Every 16 frames `MobMemberSlavedUpdate::update` moves a member farther than `MustCatchUpRadius` from the nexus to the nexus's goal, or to the nexus while it stands, and kills one three times that far for longer than `CatchUpCrisisBailTime`; a member that stands near it goes idle when the nexus is idle, and attacks the nexus's victim, or, at the chance `Squirrelliness` gives, a target of its own; a member whose nexus is gone dies (`GeneralsMD/.../MobMemberSlavedUpdate.cpp`).
- **OpenRA's port makes the nexus virtual.** The `MobSpawnerMaster` of CameoReloaded's `OpenRA.Mods.CA`, a port of Generals' mob, sets the nexus each tick at the centre of its living members and its health to theirs (`AggregateHealth`), gives its members the nexus's move, attack, attack-move and stop when `SlavesHaveFreeWill` is false, spawns a later member at a living one (`ExitByBudding`), replaces the dead on a timer, and disposes of the nexus when the last member dies ([MobSpawnerMaster.cs](https://github.com/Elpollo315/CameoReloaded/blob/master/OpenRA.Mods.CA/Traits/MobSpawnerMaster.cs)).
- **Campfire's import makes the nexus immortal and harmless.** The import gives the nexus no life, as an `ImmortalBody`, keeps its harmless weapon, and converts no member, as a `SpawnBehavior` of a unit of no production section drops; in the check's base play, eight nexuses fight China's bunker, speaker tower and Gattling cannon from 840 s to 1,140 s, each target losing about 0.004 life a minute.
