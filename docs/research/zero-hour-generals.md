# Zero Hour's veterancy, general's ranks, sciences and powers

What the released source (GeneralsX, read as a specification) and the install's `INIZH.big` say of a unit's veterancy, a player's rank and sciences, and the general's powers, and what other engines do. Read 28 October 2026.

## Veterancy

- **Four levels.** `LEVEL_REGULAR`, `LEVEL_VETERAN`, `LEVEL_ELITE`, `LEVEL_HEROIC` (`TheVeterancyNames`: `REGULAR`, `VETERAN`, `ELITE`, `HEROIC`).
- **Each type's own thresholds and values.** `ExperienceRequired` and `ExperienceValue` hold one integer for each level, `parseIntList` reading exactly 4 and leaving any more; both start at 0 and `IsTrainable` at `No` (`ThingTemplate`'s constructor). The install's 267 `IsTrainable = Yes` types use 15 distinct lists of `ExperienceRequired`, the commonest `0 100 200 400` (71 types), then `0 150 450 900` (50), `0 100 150 300` (40) and `0 200 300 600` (36). A Crusader gives `ExperienceValue = 100 100 200 400` and needs `0 200 300 600`; a Ranger gives `20 20 40 60` and needs `0 40 60 120`; a command center gives `200 200 200 200` and is not trainable.
- **A kill's experience goes to the killer.** `Object::scoreTheKill` returns for a victim of a side that is not playable, as the neutral player's, and for a victim not `ENEMIES` to the killer or of the killer's own player; a trainable killer then gains the victim's `ExperienceValue` at the victim's level, none for a victim `UNDER_CONSTRUCTION` (`ExperienceTracker::getExperienceValue` gives 0 to an ally). Projectiles and missiles score for their launcher (`DumbProjectileBehavior`, `MissileAIUpdate`).
- **The level follows the experience.** `ExperienceTracker::addExperiencePoints` adds the gain times the tracker's scalar, then takes the highest level whose `ExperienceRequired` the experience reaches; `setMinVeterancyLevel` sets the experience to the level's minimum.
- **A sink.** A tracker with an `m_experienceSink` passes its experience to that object, as a mob member's to its mob (`SpawnBehavior`).
- **A scalar.** `ExperienceScalarUpgrade` adds its `AddXPScalar` to the tracker's scalar of 1: 87 modules, each `AddXPScalar = 1.0` triggered by `Upgrade_AmericaAdvancedTraining`.
- **What a level changes.** `Object::onVeterancyLevelChanged` sets the weapon set flag and the weapon bonus condition of the new level and clears the others, gives the object the upgrade `Upgrade_Veterancy_<LEVEL>`, and lets its body scale its most health; `ActiveBody::onVeterancyLevelChanged` multiplies the most health by the new level's `HealthBonus` over the old one's, `PRESERVE_RATIO`, and sets the armor set flag of the level.
- **The bonuses.** `GameData.ini`: `WeaponBonus = VETERAN RATE_OF_FIRE 120%`, `VETERAN DAMAGE 110%`, `ELITE RATE_OF_FIRE 140%`, `ELITE DAMAGE 120%`, `HERO RATE_OF_FIRE 160%`, `HERO DAMAGE 130%`; `HealthBonus_Veteran = 120%`, `HealthBonus_Elite = 130%`, `HealthBonus_Heroic = 150%`, regular 100 %. `Weapon::computeBonus` sums the global set's bonuses and the weapon's own extra set for the object's conditions (`WeaponBonusSet::appendBonuses` adds each bonus less 1); no weapon of `Weapon.ini` has an extra bonus of `VETERAN`, `ELITE` or `HERO`.
- **No set of a level.** No object of the install has a `WeaponSet` or an `ArmorSet` of `Conditions` `VETERAN`, `ELITE` or `HERO`; 18 `TriggeredBy` lines name a veterancy upgrade, each `Upgrade_Veterancy_HEROIC`, one of them with `ELITE` beside it.
- **A start by a science.** `VeterancyGainCreate` sets a trainable object's level to its `StartingLevel` as it is made, when its player holds its `ScienceRequired`: 14 at `VETERAN` by `SCIENCE_TechnicalTraining`, 8 at `VETERAN` by `SCIENCE_ArtilleryTraining`, 6 at `VETERAN` by `SCIENCE_RedGuardTraining`, 3 at `ELITE` by `SCIENCE_BattlemasterTraining`, 1 at `ELITE` by `Infa_SCIENCE_RedGuardTraining`.
- **The chevron.** `Drawable::drawVeterancy` draws `SCVeter1`, `SCVeter2` or `SCVeter3` for a veteran, an elite or a heroic object, every living one not `IGNORED_IN_GUI`, beside its health box at its right half-width, whether or not it is selected (`Drawable::drawIconUI`). A rise plays the type's `SoundPromotedVeteran`, `Elite` or `Hero` and the `LevelGainedAnimation` of `GameData.ini`, rising 15 for 4 s.

## A player's rank

- **Skill points from kills.** `Player::addSkillPointsForKill` adds the victim's `SkillPointValue` at its level, none for a victim under construction, whatever the killer: the field defaults to the level's `ExperienceValue` (`USE_EXP_VALUE_FOR_SKILL_VALUE`), and no object of the install sets it. `Player::addSkillPoints` multiplies by `m_skillPointsModifier`, 1 but by a script, rounds up, and caps the points at the last rank's `SkillPointsNeeded`.
- **Five ranks.** `Rank.ini`: rank 1 at 0 points, 2 at 800, 3 at 1,500, 4 at 2,500, 5 at 5,000; each grants `SCIENCE_Rank<n>` and its `SciencePurchasePointsGranted`, 1 for ranks 1 to 4 and 3 for rank 5. `Player::setRankLevel` adds each rank's points and sciences as the rank is reached; a player starts at rank 1, with its 1 point. `GameLogic`'s rank limit is 1,000 but by a map script.
- **The rise's feedback.** A rank reached plays `EVA_GeneralLevelUp` to its player.

## Sciences

- **A science's rules.** `Science.ini` gives each `PrerequisiteSciences`, `SciencePurchasePointCost` and `IsGrantable`. `Player::isCapableOfPurchasingScience` refuses a science held, disabled or hidden, one of a missing prerequisite, and one whose cost is 0 or above the points held; `attemptToPurchaseScience` spends the cost and adds the science.
- **A faction's science and the ranks' sciences.** `PlayerTemplate.ini` gives each playable template its `IntrinsicSciences`: `SCIENCE_AMERICA`, `SCIENCE_CHINA` or `SCIENCE_GLA`, all three for the Boss general; each of cost 0 and not grantable. `SCIENCE_Rank1` to `SCIENCE_Rank8` are of cost 0, each the one before as its prerequisite. A science of the panel names its faction's science and a rank's among its prerequisites, as `SCIENCE_PaladinTank` of `SCIENCE_AMERICA SCIENCE_Rank1`, and a second level the first, as `SCIENCE_Paradrop2` of `SCIENCE_Paradrop1 SCIENCE_Rank3`.
- **What a science does.** An object's `Prerequisites` line `Science = <science>` holds while its player holds it; a power's `RequiredScience` turns its power on; a `VeterancyGainCreate` starts a level; an `OCLSpecialPower`'s `UpgradeOCL` picks its creation list by the first science of its lines its player holds.
- **A purchase readies its power.** `Player::addScience` calls `onSpecialPowerCreation` and `setReadyFrame(now)` on each of the player's power modules that require the science: a power is ready the moment its science is bought.
- **The panel's pages.** A template names its purchase sets, `PurchaseScienceCommandSetRank1`, `Rank3` and `Rank8`, as `SCIENCE_AMERICA_CommandSetRank1` of `Command_PurchaseSciencePaladinTank`, `StealthFighter` and `SpyDrone`; each of the 12 playable templates has its own three, and a set's buttons sit at places 1 to 15, laid `1 4 7 10 13 / 2 5 8 11 14 / 3 6 9 12 15`. A purchase button gives its `Science`, its `ButtonImage` and, for a unit, its `Object`.
- **The rank sets' sciences.** USA's rank 1: Paladin, Stealth Fighter, Spy Drone; China's: Red Guard Training, Artillery Training, Nuke Cannon; GLA's: Scud Launcher, Marauder, Technical Training. Each faction's rank 3 set holds `SCIENCE_EmergencyRepair1` to `3`, at places 10 to 12.
- **The AI's purchase.** `Default/AIData.ini` gives each side of a `SideInfo`, by a template's `Side`, up to five `SkillSet`s of sciences, two for each of its 12 sides; the Boss general's side, `Boss`, has none; a skirmish AI draws one of the sets that hold sciences by the logic's random as it first holds a point, and from then buys, each update it holds points, every science of the set it can buy, in the set's order (`AIPlayer::doUpgradesAndSkills`).

## Powers

- **A power's template.** `SpecialPower.ini` gives a power its `ReloadTime`, its `RequiredScience`, `SharedSyncedTimer` and `PublicTimer`. `SuperweaponEmergencyRepair`: `ReloadTime = 240000`, `RequiredScience = SCIENCE_EmergencyRepair1`, `SharedSyncedTimer = Yes`, `RadiusCursorRadius = 100`.
- **A shared timer is the player's.** A power of `SharedSyncedTimer` keeps one ready frame for each player and template, which every module of the template reads (`Player::getOrStartSpecialPowerReadyFrame`); one with no frame yet starts one at the current frame, so it is ready; a use sets it to the frame plus `ReloadTime` (`SpecialPowerModule::startPowerRecharge`).
- **A command center's powers.** `FactionBuilding.ini`'s `AmericaCommandCenter` holds 9 `OCLSpecialPower` modules, `SuperweaponEmergencyRepair` among them; its command set places `Command_EmergencyRepair` at 8, China's at 7 and GLA's at 6.
- **The power's button.** `Command_EmergencyRepair`: `Command = SPECIAL_POWER`, `Options = NEED_SPECIAL_POWER_SCIENCE NEED_TARGET_POS CONTEXTMODE_COMMAND`, `ButtonImage = SSRepair`, `RadiusCursorType = EMERGENCY_REPAIR`. A button of `NEED_SPECIAL_POWER_SCIENCE` is hidden while its player lacks its power's science (`ControlBar`'s command update). A template's `SpecialPowerShortcutCommandSet` places each power's `FromShortcut` button on a bar of its own.
- **Emergency Repair creates a marker.** Its module: `OCL = SUPERWEAPON_RepairVehicles1`, `UpgradeOCL = SCIENCE_EmergencyRepair3 SUPERWEAPON_RepairVehicles3`, then `SCIENCE_EmergencyRepair2 SUPERWEAPON_RepairVehicles2`, `CreateLocation = CREATE_AT_LOCATION`. Each list creates one `RepairVehiclesInArea_InvisibleMarker_Level<n>` of `System.ini`: `NO_COLLIDE IMMOBILE UNATTACKABLE`, an `ImmortalBody`, its draw's particle systems `GPSRotisserie` and `RepairCloud`, a `DeletionUpdate` of lifetime 0, and an `AutoHealBehavior` of `HealingAmount` 100, 200 or 300, `Radius = 100.0`, `KindOf = VEHICLE`, `SingleBurst = Yes`, `StartsActive = Yes`.
- **The burst.** `AutoHealBehavior::update` with a radius heals, once for `SingleBurst`, each living object of its `KindOf` and of its player's allies, its own player's included, whose center lies within the radius on the ground (`FROM_CENTER_2D`) and whose health is below its most, by the amount (`attemptHealingFromSoleBenefactor`), and shows `GetHealedAnimation` over each.
- **Other powers.** Most of a command center's powers are creation lists of a transport, as `DeliverPayload` for the artillery barrage and the paradrops, or of a unit, as the spy drone; `CashBountyPower` raises the bounty share, with no button.

## Other engines

- **OpenRA's experience.** `GainsExperience` grants the condition of each level its experience reaches; its `Conditions` keys are each level's experience as a percentage of the actor's own value, its `Valued` cost by default (`ExperienceModifier`). `GivesExperience` gives a kill's `Experience`, the victim's cost by default, to a killer of a relationship its `ValidRelationships` hold, `Neutral` and `Enemy` by default, scaled by `ActorExperienceModifier`, 100 %, and to the killer's player's `PlayerExperience`, scaled by `PlayerExperienceModifier`, 0 % by default. `ProducibleWithLevel` gives a produced actor `InitialLevels` while its owner's tech tree holds its `Prerequisites` (`OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits`, `bleed`, read 28 October 2026).
- **OpenRA's support powers.** `SupportPowerManager` keeps one `SupportPowerInstance` for each `OrderName` of a player, shared by every actor that grants it unless `AllowMultiple`; a second actor joins its `Instances` without a timer of its own; an instance starts empty unless `StartFullyCharged`, keeps its charge as its prerequisites come, and loses it as they go; `Activate` fires from the ready instance nearest the target and starts the one timer again.
- **Dota 2.** A hero's skill points come with its levels; its talents are a choice of one of two at fixed levels.
