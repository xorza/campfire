# Test matches

How the engine seats a watcher and starts a player today, and how other games start a match of bots with an observer or a player with more than its usual start, read on 21 October 2026: the engine from its code at `3feac7a58`; the others from their sources as linked.

## The engine today

- **A session needs a player.** `Lobby::new` refuses a plan of no player seat with `LobbyError::NoPlayer` (`net/src/sim_server/lobby/mod.rs`), and a started session ends once every player left (`SimServerWorld::exit_due`), which a session of no player would meet at once.
- **The local server seats its client in seat 0.** `LocalServer::start` marks every seat but 0 and the bots' open, and opens the session with `HostVisibility::default()`, which admits no spectator (`net/src/local/local_server/mod.rs`); the client's command line refuses a server bot in seat 0 (`ServerBotError::ClientSlot`). So `--local --spectate` joins nothing: the match waits for seat 0's player.
- **The dedicated server seats a player in each seat no bot holds.** `campfire-server` opens the mode's 8 seats, and a session of two bots on Tournament Desert waits for six players.
- **A watcher exists.** A client of `JoinRole::Watch` joins as a spectator (`net/src/sim_server/offering.rs`), and `--watch <team>` picks a team's view.
- **Zero Hour's start is a script.** The rules package's `on_match_start` spawns each seated player's faction building at `Player_<n>_Start` and its start units at `Player_<n>_Rally`, from the params `start_building_<faction>` and `start_units_<faction>` the import writes (`packages/zero-hour/scripts/mode.rhai`).
- **`spawn_unit` tests a box alone for room.** A box spawned where a wall, a static body or a box the same call spawns lies is refused with `NoRoom`; a circle is never tested (`mode/src/mode_api.rs`, `NavigationColumn::room_for`).
- **A building frees its units only as its site completes.** A producer's `free` units and kept `spawns` leave as its site completes (`production/src/construction/mod.rs`); a building a script spawns whole frees none, where Zero Hour's `SpawnBehavior` spawns as its object is made, whatever made it.

## Other games

- **OpenRA's starting units.** A lobby option, `startingunits`, picks a class of start; each class names, by faction, a `BaseActor` spawned at the spawn point and `SupportActors` spawned in cells of the annulus from `InnerSupportRadius` to `OuterSupportRadius` round it, each cell drawn at random from those it may enter and reach, and a unit with no cell left is skipped ([`SpawnStartingUnits.cs`](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/bleed/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/World/SpawnStartingUnits.cs), [`MapStartingUnits.cs`](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/bleed/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/World/MapStartingUnits.cs)).
- **OpenRA's lobby starts a game of bots and a watcher.** `CheckAutoStart` starts a game once every human player and the host, who may spectate, are ready, the map's required slots are held, and any slot is held, a bot's among them; a dedicated server also needs two human players unless its `EnableSingleplayer` is set, which a server that is not dedicated always sets. A server that is not dedicated shuts down as its host leaves, and a dedicated one cleans up once every client left ([`LobbyCommands.cs`](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/bleed/OpenRA.Mods.Common/ServerTraits/LobbyCommands.cs), [`Server.cs`](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/bleed/OpenRA.Game/Server/Server.cs), [`Settings.cs`](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/bleed/OpenRA.Game/Settings.cs)).
- **OpenRA's developer mode.** A lobby checkbox, "Debug Menu", off by default, gives each player cheats of fast build, cash, all tech, build anywhere and no fog, the cash 20,000 by default ([`DeveloperMode.cs`](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/bleed/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/Player/DeveloperMode.cs)).
- **StarCraft II's placement search and complete units.** `UnitTypePlacementFromPoint(type, player, point, range)` gives a point near `point`, within `range`, where a unit of `type` may stand, or null when the placement fails, and `UnitTypePlacementTestsFromPoint` names which tests it runs; `UnitCreate` makes a unit whole unless its style holds `c_unitCreateConstruct`, which starts it under construction ([`natives.galaxy`](https://github.com/SC2Mapster/SC2GameData/blob/master/mods/core.sc2mod/base.sc2data/TriggerLibs/natives.galaxy)).
- **Age of Empires II's placement and Empire Wars.** A random map script's `create_object` takes `find_closest`, which places an object at the nearest tile that its other rules allow ([Forgotten Empires: RMS features](https://www.forgottenempires.net/age-of-empires-ii-definitive-edition/rms-features)). Empire Wars starts every player in the Feudal Age with 27 villagers at work, economic buildings and a barracks ([Age of Empires: Empire Wars](https://ageofempires.com/games/aoeiide/empire-wars)).
- **Bot matches with a watcher.** Tempest Rising's patch of 29 April 2026 lets a custom game hold AI bots alone, which a player watches from a spectator slot ([patch notes](https://support.saber.games/hc/en/tempest-rising/articles/april-29-2026-patch-notes-24)); a player on the Age of Empires forum reports that a skirmish of AI alone still seats them as a player ([forum](https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/skirmish-is-bugged-cannot-spectate-a-game-with-only-ais/268055)).

## Skirmish bots that play a whole match

Read on 22 October 2026: Zero Hour from GeneralsX's source; OpenRA and Age of Empires II from their documentation and forums, as linked; the engine from the base play of the Zero Hour check.

- **Zero Hour's skirmish AI is engine code that data and scripts steer.** `AISkirmishPlayer` builds its base from a faction's build list and keeps a count of gatherers for each difficulty, which `AIData.ini`'s `SideInfo` and `SkirmishBuildList` give (`GameLogic/AI.h`, `AISideInfo`, `AISideBuildList`); `AIPlayer` queues teams, rebuilds, and builds by supplies (`AIPlayer.h`, `processBaseBuilding`, `checkReadyTeams`, `buildBySupplies`). `SkirmishScripts.scb`, written in WorldBuilder, sends its teams in attack waves 1 to 5 along a map's `CenterPath`, `FlankPath` and `BackdoorPath`, an easy AI to wave 3 and a hard one to wave 5 ([Project Perfect Mod: editing the AI](https://ppmforums.com/post-170660/editing-the-ai-zh/), [Generals AI editing](https://cnc.projectperfectmod.com/genaiediting/)).
- **OpenRA's bot is a set of engine modules that YAML configures.** A `ModularBot` turns on its modules by a condition of its bot type: `BaseBuilderBotModule`, `HarvesterBotModule`, `UnitBuilderBotModule`, `SquadManagerBotModule`, `BuildingRepairBotModule`, `McvManagerBotModule`, `SupportPowerBotModule`, `CaptureManagerBotModule` and `MinelayerBotModule`, each of which a mod may replace; modules that fight over one unit are a known flaw ([OpenRA traits](https://docs.openra.net/en/release/traits/), [OpenRA forum](https://forum.openra.net/viewtopic.php?p=312089)).
- **Age of Empires II's AI is rules of conditions and actions.** A `.per` script holds `defrule` blocks, conditions above `=>` and actions below it, and strategic numbers that tune the engine's own behaviour; the language has no loops or functions ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/813780/discussions/4/3082142648910029808/), [daily.dev summary](https://daily.dev/posts/ai-plays-age-of-empires-ii-cbfqxg1w5)).
- **The base play's bots stall, and not for money.** Two bots of orders at ticks, China and America, each from a base, train and attack: after 20 minutes China held 7 of its 12 base buildings and America 10 of its 11, and neither base fell. With a start of 1,000,000 each, the match ended alike, each bot holding over 930,000 after 15 minutes; with every train of each producer queued every 20 s, each spent some 170,000 in 20 minutes, and each army stayed near 18 units. Waves every 3 minutes in place of 40 s ended alike. Each army arrives in waves that the base defenses and the other army wear down as fast as its producers train them. The USA also earns nothing, as its one gatherer, the Chinook, flies and the import trains no unit that does not walk ([Zero Hour](../design/09-zero-hour.md), The USA's income waits for aircraft).
- **Dota 2's bots are server-side Lua scripts.** A bot script runs at the server level, reads the game state and orders its units directly, sees only what a human of its team sees, and commands only its own units; a `Think()` in `bot_generic.lua` takes a hero over whole, and else each bot scores its modes and the highest one acts, with team-level desires feeding the scores ([Dota Bot Scripting](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Dota_Bot_Scripting)).
- **Spring's AIs come in two kinds.** A Lua AI runs synced on every client; a native skirmish AI, loaded through an interface library so it may be written in another language, is not synced, and its commands look to the engine like a player's; Spring 1944's CRAIG was rewritten to run unsynced, so one client alone pays its cost ([Spring AI development](https://springrts.com/wiki/AI:Development), [Spring forum](https://springrts.com/phpbb/viewtopic.php?p=451048)).
- **0 A.D.'s Petra is a JavaScript bot of modules.** The engine makes its object at the game's start and calls its `OnUpdate` each turn; it plays every n turns, each AI player shifted out of phase, with a headquarters that chooses actions and a queue manager that shares resources between them by priorities, and traits between 0 and 1 that a seeded draw picks ([PetraBot](https://trac.wildfiregames.com/wiki/PetraBot?version=12)).
- **OpenRA's bots are C# and YAML.** Its Lua serves missions; a bot of Lua alone was a work in progress in 2017, held back by the sandbox's limits ([OpenRA forum](https://forum.openra.net/viewtopic.php?p=301157)).
- **Campfire's bots today.** A server bot plays an order script of orders at ticks, which the server's driver turns into inputs that it signs and logs as `Bot` inputs, so a replay or a verifier runs no bot; a restore plays a script on from its tick, as the script holds no state (`net/src/sim_server/bot_driver.rs`). Rhai runs in the sim as the roles action, modifier, mode and AI, the AI role a unit's `on_think` within the think pool (`script/src/scripts/script_role.rs`).
- **OpenRA's bot modules keep state and save it.** `BaseBuilderBotModule` keeps its base center, its defense center and its requested refineries, and saves the first two in a game's save; `SquadManagerBotModule` keeps its squads, the idle units round its base and its timers, and saves them. Its timers start at random offsets, so two bots do not act in one tick ([BaseBuilderBotModule.cs](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/bleed/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/BotModules/BaseBuilderBotModule.cs), [SquadManagerBotModule.cs](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/bleed/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/BotModules/SquadManagerBotModule.cs)).
- **OpenRA attacks with a squad of a size.** An assault squad forms once the idle units round the base reach `SquadSize`, 8 by default, plus a random bonus below `SquadSizeRandomBonus`, 30, drawn anew for each squad; a rush every `RushInterval`, 600 ticks, targets an enemy construction yard and the enemies within 15 cells of it. A building attacked by a preferred enemy makes the bot send its squads and idle units within reach at the attacker, every 30 ticks at most (`RespondToAttack`, `ProtectOwn`) (SquadManagerBotModule.cs).
- **OpenRA places a building near its base.** Its base lies in the ring of 2 to 20 cells round a construction yard (`MinBaseRadius`, `MaxBaseRadius`), defenses go toward the last attacker, and three failed placements pause its building for 1,500 ticks (BaseBuilderBotModule.cs).
- **Zero Hour's skirmish AI builds by a build list of places.** `AIData.ini`'s `SkirmishBuildList` gives each faction's buildings at places round its command center, which `AISkirmishPlayer` turns by one of eight angles to the player's start (`AISkirmishPlayer.cpp`); a supply center that does not fit near its dock is moved by steps of a pathfind cell until it does (`AIPlayer::buildBySupplies`).
- **OpenTTD's script AIs save their own state.** An AI gives a table to save, and its `Load` gets that table and the version that saved it; an AI that saved nothing is not loaded ([OpenTTD: Save and Load](https://wiki.openttd.org/en/Development/Script/Save%20and%20Load)).

## The bots of Rhai in the Zero Hour check

Measured on 22 October 2026, the Zero Hour check's base play and attack play, with debug runs of the check.

- **No match ends.** Two attackers from base starts stand at 5 and 8 buildings after 20 minutes, and an attacker against a builder from standard starts at 9 and 8 after 15 minutes; limits of 60 and 40 minutes give the same stands.
- **Money ends at about minute 6.** Each bot spends its 50,000 and earns nothing after: the bot orders no gather, and America's supply center frees a Chinook, which the import drops as it flies.
- **The filter `enemies` takes neutral oil derricks.** An army that attacks the nearest unit of `enemies` aims at a tech oil derrick of the neutral team, which no army destroys; `hostiles` takes the players alone.
- **An attack order of a group goes to one target.** An army ordered at an Inferno cannon 550 off holds no attack target 10 s later: the orders give an attack only to each unit whose weapons hit its one target (`unit_orders.rs`), so the others stand.
- **Zero Hour's AI and OpenRA's bots gather and attack-move.** Zero Hour's AI wants a count of trucks for each supply center by difficulty, plus the free one, trains more when it lacks them, and moves its teams by `aiAttackMoveToPosition` (GeneralsX, `AIPlayer::checkForSupplyCenter`, `queueSupplyTruck`, `AIStates.cpp`). OpenRA's `HarvesterBotModule` orders each idle harvester to harvest every 50 ticks and builds one while it holds fewer than 4 or than its refineries; its ground squads move and fight by `AttackMove` alone ([HarvesterBotModule.cs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/bleed/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/BotModules/HarvesterBotModule.cs), [GroundStates.cs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/bleed/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/BotModules/Squads/States/GroundStates.cs)).
- **A move each second keeps the army near home.** An army ordered to the enemy start each second stood at most 886 from its home after 15 minutes, and one ordered each 10 s 1,405.
- **An army of 12 dies against a base's defenses.** With 12, the builder's base lost no building in 15 minutes; with 30, three, and its dozer.
- **With gathering and attack-move, the builder's match ends.** China's attacker beats GLA's builder from standard starts at tick 26,198, 14.6 minutes; an attack-move ends beside a structure, as the scan passes over structures, so the army's idle units within 300 of their target attack it by an order.
- **Two attackers stand.** From base starts, China and GLA stand at 9 and 8 buildings after 20 minutes and after 40; from standard starts, at 2 and 3 after 60 minutes. Each side's near nodes empty and its gatherers stand idle or die, its money stays under 500 from about minute 20, and the small armies left sit before the other's defenses.
- **Zero Hour's AI expands to supply and OpenRA's bots rush the base.** Zero Hour's AI builds a supply center by a supply source with a minimum of cash left, the one nearest its base, by its skirmish scripts (GeneralsX, `AIPlayer::buildBySupplies`, `findSupplyCenter`). OpenRA's `SquadManagerBotModule` rushes every `RushInterval`, 600 ticks, with at least `SquadSize` ground units, at an enemy within `RushAttackScanRadius` of an enemy construction yard, or the yard itself ([SquadManagerBotModule.cs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/bleed/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/BotModules/SquadManagerBotModule.cs)).
