## Test matches › 4. The base start: a bots' match that ends

- **The item.** The plan's check asks for "a match of the two bots that ends with a result within its limit". It does not end: both bots spend their 50,000 before either base falls. After 20 minutes, China holds 7 of its 12 base buildings and America all 11, and the armies are gone. America's base has no gatherer, as the import drops its Chinook, so it earns nothing.
- **What was done.** The check pins how the match stands when its limit passes: the tick it ended and its result, if it did, and the buildings each seat holds (`golden.toml`, `[base]`). The item is done on that pin.
- **The options.**
  1. Keep the pin of how the match stands. It holds the bots' play fixed and costs nothing more.
  2. Bots that gather and rebuild: a bot language of rules, not orders at ticks, as OpenRA's and Zero Hour's skirmish AIs are. A slice of its own.
  3. A mode option of more start money, so the armies outlast the defenses. It moves the import away from the original's choices.
- **Recommendation.** Option 1 now, and option 2 as its own slice when bots that play a whole match are wanted.
- **Blocked.** Nothing: the fold records the decision as it stands.
