# Issues

The issue log: every open problem of the project, under the step it waits for. The rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#issue-log).

## Decide

- **Client.** **Plan: S2.** The camera turns and zooms by keys only on a keypad, as Zero Hour's command map gives them (4 and 6, 8 and 2, 5), so a keyboard with no keypad, as a laptop's, turns it only by a middle-button drag, which a trackpad lacks. Options: keep the original's keys alone; add keys a keyboard with no keypad has.
- **Zero Hour.** The import writes W3D's materials into glTF's material model, which holds one pass, no ambient color and no fallback texture, so each part of a W3D material that glTF lacks is lost: the next two items, a missing texture and the ambient color, and the second pass under Research, are its cases. Options: an extension of glTF's material that the client draws; a material file of Campfire's beside each model; or glTF's model, as now, each loss decided alone.
- **Zero Hour.** A texture no archive holds leaves its triangles untextured in the import, where the game draws `MissingTexture`, a solid color of `0x7FFF00FF`: draw them as the game does, or untextured, as now.
- **Zero Hour.** The import ignores a vertex material's ambient color, as glTF's material has none: 336 vertex materials of converted models have one other than their diffuse color, which the game lights by the scene's ambient light. Options: a material file with the ambient color, which the client draws; or the diffuse color for both, as now.

## Research

- **Client.** **Plan: I2.** The view tests' units spawn by `world.spawn` with `BodyUnit` and no unit type (`client/src/view/tests.rs:62`), where every unit a client receives holds its type, which the server sends with it: the view reads a unit's type as optional, `Option<&UnitType>` (`client/src/view/mod.rs:121`), and draws a unit of none, which no client meets. Which type such a unit takes, with the looks a test then needs, is not known.
- **Combat.** Systems read the match's role each tick, a fact the build fixes, where the plan's Direction makes such a fact one of the schedule: `Attacks::strike` checks `Predicting` for each attack going off (`combat/src/attacks.rs`), the held pass for each instance it keeps (`stats/src/held_pass.rs`), and vision's start of `SeenBy` for each unit that spawns (`vision/src/lib.rs`).
- **Common.** On Windows, CI fails two of `common`'s workspace tests, `tests::a_required_dependency_is_named_by_production_code` and `tests::no_crates_modules_import_each_other_in_a_cycle` (`common/src/tests.rs:758`): the first finds no dependency named by production code, so it lists every required dependency as unnamed. Both pass on Linux and macOS. `master`'s CI fails so at `7a29072b`.
- **Declare.** An install adds more than its systems, where the plan's Direction gives it its systems alone: resources it builds, `UnitGrid` and the unit trackers (`units/src/units/mod.rs`), `BodyIndex` (`navigation/src/lib.rs`), `Deaths` and the non-send `CombatHooks` (`combat/src/lib.rs`) and `LiveCarriers` (`stats/src/lib.rs`), each declared as a cache or a report that the installer does not put in, and the component hook of `UnitType` (`units/src/units/mod.rs`), which no declaration names and the declaration audit does not see.
- **Navigation.** The levels by size of B2 make the Collide stage slower for walkers of ordinary sizes, with no wide body present, and the 3v3's ticks faster. On an i9-13980HX, with each walker's cell one `CellKey`, `integration/collision/spread` takes 95.5 µs against 59.8 µs with one level of cells twice the widest walker's radius, `crowded` 325.0 µs against 257.4 µs, `boxes` 681.4 µs against 613.8 µs, and `bridge`, with a 1,117 × 100 m box 2 m past the crowded scene, 715.4 µs against 650.9 µs; the scenes' walkers of 0.2 to 1.19 m spread over four levels, and each occupied cell searches the nine cells around its own at each coarser level. `integration/server_tick/mean_3v3` takes 479.2 ms, and 519.4 ms with one level.
- **Net.** The LAN check fails at times in CI on Ubuntu: a bot's link logs `Received a ping that is not present` from `lightyear_sync::ping::manager`, the check counts it a failure, and a rerun of the same commit passes (run 38538783614, commit `0bc73b7c`).
- **Package.** `mode_package::every_flaw_of_a_package_fails_its_load_with_its_own_problem` runs past the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, 1.05 s with its 273 flaws, each a load of the 3v3's packages.
- **Quic.** The send queue keeps `quinn-proto`'s 1 MiB: the delay a full queue adds is its size over the connection's sending rate, tens of seconds at the minimum window, and Lightyear queues a reliable message again while its last copy still waits there. No scenario measures the largest burst one client receives, as at its join, or the delay the queue adds: the harness's link models delay and loss, not a rate.
- **Quic.** No bench measures the frame's two systems, the thread's batch, or the frame's waits for the mutex, nor a flood of handshakes from many addresses: what a handshake costs the thread under the mutex, and how long a batch of them holds the frame, which sets how many new connections a pass takes ([QUIC transport](../docs/design/15-quic-transport.md), D14). No pass bounds its new connections.
- **Quic.** `quic_endpoint::tests::a_connection_a_server_ends_before_its_frame_is_never_linked` fails at times (`quic/src/quic_endpoint/tests.rs:447`): the connection ends with `Transport { code: 12 }` where the test expects `Ended`. Five runs after the failure passed.
- **Quic.** The thread allocates under the mutex at times `quinn-proto`'s random numbers choose: 8 times at a connection's first key update, within its first 1,000 packets, and once when the packet numbers a connection received first hold three ranges, as each packet number a sender skips leaves a gap until the ranges before it are acknowledged. A read also allocates a new arena when `RecvBatch`'s ring comes back to an arena that a message slice Lightyear keeps still holds.
- **Runner.** `resume::the_3v3_match_resumes_from_each_checkpoint_to_the_same_hashes` runs past the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, with `--test-threads 1`, 1.41 to 1.45 s at commit aa1e8aac, where `source/.config/nextest.toml` gives it 0.79 s on an M5.
- **Runner.** `proving_match::a_snapshot_of_drawn_values_restores_or_is_refused_and_never_panics` runs past the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, with `--test-threads 1`, 1.00 to 1.03 s at commit aa1e8aac and after it. The five ticks after each restored draw take most of it, in the navigation's broadphase and collider.
- **Runner.** `proving_match::a_snapshot_restores_and_a_flawed_one_never_panics` runs at the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, with `--test-threads 1`, 1.11 s at commit e43f95af, and 0.87 s in a later run.
- **Runner.** Two measurements of `integration/server_tick/worst_3v3` disagree: 0.83 ms on a Ryzen 7 6800U, with a mean of 114.4 µs from the same run, and 0.51 ms in a later measurement for the checkpoints' cost. Which is the engine's worst tick now is not known.
- **Script.** Scripts get no completion, signatures or descriptions in an editor. Rhai's VS Code extension highlights syntax only, and Rhai's language server, the one tool that reads a definition file such as `Engine::definitions` writes, is unpublished, marked experimental, and unchanged since October 2022.
- **Vision.** `Bitmap::set` is the largest function of `integration/server_tick/mean_3v3`, 6.9 % of it, for runs of one or two words a row; which of its parts costs that, the words' writes, the brush's mask or the record of each word set, is not known.
- **Zero Hour.** The import draws a mesh's first material pass and its first texture stage alone; 275 meshes of converted models have a second pass, which the game draws over the first.
- **Zero Hour.** The import makes a unit of every object a map places but a road's or bridge's end and a waypoint, whatever its template: the game spawns no object whose template no INI object names, adds props, optimized trees and, in multiplayer, fluff to the client alone, and spawns no shrub when trees are off (`GameLogic::startNewGame`).

## Ready

- **Actions.** **Plan: G1.** The projectile section's `gravity` is planned: no projectile falls, so no grenade arcs and no bullet drops.
- **Client.** **Plan: S2.** The client has no shop and no item keys: a player cannot buy, sell or use an item, which design 04's items give as orders.
- **Import.** The modules of `zero_hour` import each other in two cycles, which the cycle test of top modules does not see: `w3d_file` with `hierarchy`, `hlod` and `w3d_mesh` (`w3d_file` takes `Hierarchy`, `Hlod` and `W3dMesh`, and each of the three takes `w3d_file`'s `Chunks` and `Fields`); and `zero_hour` with `model_import` (`zero_hour` takes `Converted` and `ModelImport`, and `model_import` takes `ZeroHour` for `ZeroHour::texture_path`).
- **Interaction.** **Plan: U1.** `interaction` is planned: no action of the `use` kind runs.
- **Package.** **Plan: S1.** Only an avatar names its human text, its `name`; an action, a loadout's spell, a mode, a team and a choice have no message id, so a client can show none of their names, while the design says each name is a message id ([Human text](../docs/design/03-game-scripting.md#game-package)).
- **Quic.** **Plan: N1.** No limit holds live connections or new handshakes for each address, where design 15's D13 bounds both by the session's seats for each IPv4 address or IPv6 /64, and `net` gives the endpoint no seat count ([QUIC transport](../docs/design/15-quic-transport.md), D13).
- **Quic.** **Plan: N2.** UPnP finds no router behind a host firewall that tracks connections when the router answers SSDP searches from a port other than 1900, as Omada's ER605 does: `igd-next` discovers by search alone, and the router's `NOTIFY` announcements, which the firewall lets in, go unread ([QUIC transport](../docs/design/15-quic-transport.md), D12).
- **Runner.** The proving match runs no `items`, though its layout test says it holds every type of every capability the release runs: `golden/layout.txt` pins neither `items.inventory` nor `actions.use_group`, so a change of their layout at the same `StateRegistry::DATA_VERSION` passes it.
