# Issues

The issue log: every open problem of the project, under the step it waits for. The rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#issue-log).

## Decide

- **Capabilities.** **Plan: 12.** `Action` names two things: the top crate's sum of what an order tells its units to do, with each capability's own (`OrdersAction`, `ProgressionAction`, `ItemsAction`), which the bot order scripts write as `action.*`; and `actions`' action as a match runs it, the word the script API keeps for it (06-script-api.md, One vocabulary). Options: the order's becomes `Directive`, `Instruction` or `Task`, with its `*Action` types and the bot scripts' keys; or both keep `Action`, the order's then named in the design as a second meaning.
- **Test packages.** **Plan: 12.** The test packages go two ways: `packages/test/heroes/` and `packages/test/modes/` by what a package is, and `packages/test/moba/` by its game first, with its heroes, mode, spells and locales. Options: every package by what it is, the MOBA's among them; or every package by its game first, the generic ones under a game of their own.
- **Workspace.** **Plan: 12.** Two crate names break the noun form of the others: `declare` is a verb, and `state-types` the one name of `-types`. Options: `declare` becomes `declarations`, and `state-types` `states`; or both stay, as the names of what the crates do.

## Research

- **Combat.** A predicting client's strike, `Attacks::strike::<ClientForm>` (`combat/src/attacks.rs`), runs on no attacker: its query takes `&mut ClipShots`, a server part, so a predicted attack's windup stays due on the client, its cooldown is never predicted, and only the server's state ends it.
- **Import.** The imported package's `data/mode.toml` and `manifest.toml` come from `format!` templates (`import/src/zero_hour/rules_package.rs`, `mode_data` and `mode_manifest`), and its `data/units.toml` from structs that mirror the engine's sections, so a key the engine renames shows only as the import's own load of what it wrote fails.
- **Mode.** A kill's bounty (`mode/src/bounty_book.rs`, `Bounty::pay`) pays for a victim that is a site under construction, where Zero Hour's `Player::doBountyForKill` pays none for a victim `UNDER_CONSTRUCTION`; it shows once a script raises a Zero Hour player's share, as the Cash Bounty power would.
- **Navigation.** `Routing::route_units` (`navigation/src/routing.rs`) reads the change ticks of every walker's `Destination` and `Route` in each tick to find the walkers whose destination or route changed, as the many writers of `Destination` across the capabilities push no walker into an index.
- **Net-messages.** **Plan: 4.** A message's serialize fn returns a `Result`, and the send queue drops a message whose serialize fails (`net-messages/src/serialize_fns.rs`, `send_queue.rs`), but a serde message's encode through `codec` never fails, and whether a `ToBytes` write into a `Writer` can fail is not known.
- **Net.** The LAN check failed once in CI on macOS after the server's restart: a bot at its tick 299 sent an order stamped 300 while the server ran tick 303, so the server applied the two inputs stamped 300 in ticks 301 and 303 with no catch-up frame logged, and the check counts each a failure (run 39052741501, commit `f3ad15c8`).
- **Net.** **Plan: 8.** The LAN check fails at times in CI on Ubuntu: a bot's link logs `Received a ping that is not present` from `lightyear_sync::ping::manager`, the check counts it a failure, and a rerun of the same commit passes (run 38538783614, commit `0bc73b7c`).
- **Net.** The client and its local server, two apps in one process, share the global compute pool, whose scope runs the other app's tasks (`sim_server_plugins.rs:27`, `bevy_tasks task_pool.rs:310-330`).
- **Net.** **Plan: 9.** The server's 2 ms frame loop runs every Lightyear system 500 times a second, whether or not a tick or a datagram is due (`net_protocol/mod.rs:73-76`, `apps/server/src/main.rs:193`).
- **Quic.** **Plan: 8.** The send queue keeps `quinn-proto`'s 1 MiB: the delay a full queue adds is its size over the connection's sending rate, tens of seconds at the minimum window, and Lightyear queues a reliable message again while its last copy still waits there. No scenario measures the largest burst one client receives, as at its join, or the delay the queue adds: the harness's link models delay and loss, not a rate.
- **Quic.** **Plan: 8.** No bench measures the frame's two systems, the thread's batch, or the frame's waits for the mutex, nor a flood of handshakes from many addresses: what a handshake costs the thread under the mutex, and how long a batch of them holds the frame, which sets how many new connections a pass takes ([QUIC transport](../docs/design/11-quic-transport.md#connections)). No pass bounds its new connections.
- **Quic.** **Plan: 8.** The thread allocates under the mutex at times `quinn-proto`'s random numbers choose: 8 times at a connection's first key update, within its first 1,000 packets, and once when the packet numbers a connection received first hold three ranges, as each packet number a sender skips leaves a gap until the ranges before it are acknowledged. A read also allocates a new arena when `RecvBatch`'s ring comes back to an arena that a message slice Lightyear keeps still holds.
- **Release.** A test helper that changes a book in place, as `Units::give_type`, `Stats::give_type_stats` and `Actions::push` do, makes no check that `Book::load_into` makes: once a test match's first tick installs its systems, a change of a fact an install reads, as a turret, a channel or an interval, leaves the match without the passes of that feature, and nothing panics.
- **Runner.** `match_3v3::scripted_match::a_3v3_match_replays_to_the_same_hashes` runs past the 15 s that `source/.config/nextest.toml` gives a scripted match: on an i9-13980HX, alone, with `--test-threads 1`, 16.7 s at commit f16bca40, and 18.5 s with the architecture redesign over it, uncommitted.
- **Runner.** `resume::the_3v3_match_resumes_from_each_checkpoint_to_the_same_hashes` runs past the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, with `--test-threads 1`, 1.86 s at commit f16bca40, and 2.02 s with the architecture redesign over it, uncommitted; at commit 3e7a72a0 its profile was flat, no function above 3 % at 20 kHz, and the cost a resume from each checkpoint, each a replay of the log to it.
- **Runner.** `proving_match::a_snapshot_of_drawn_values_restores_or_is_refused_and_never_panics` runs past the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, with `--test-threads 1`, 1.95 s at commit f16bca40, and 2.68 s with the architecture redesign over it, uncommitted; at commit 3e7a72a0 its profile was flat, no function above 7 %, spread over restores, archetype moves, Rhai calls and movement, and the cost the sweep's size: each state type 16 draws, each restored draw 5 ticks.
- **Runner.** `proving_match::a_snapshot_restores_and_a_flawed_one_never_panics` runs past the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, with `--test-threads 1`, 1.14 s at commit f16bca40, and 1.04 s with the architecture redesign over it, uncommitted: a restore for each byte of the snapshot, and 5 ticks after each one that restores.
- **Script.** A form that returns a list of names, as `unit.queue` and `ctx.map.paths`, gives plain text with no name kind, so the load checks no literal that a script compares with an item of the list.
- **Stats.** A unit a predicting client predicts again, as its owner changes and `Unpredicted` goes (`net/src/sim_client/mod.rs`, `mark_unpredicted`), keeps the change ticks its parts had while hidden: a pass that visits what changed by those ticks, as `Holds::want_passives` and the stats refresh do, may pass over it until a part of it changes, where a pass over every unit takes it at once. Whether Lightyear's prediction writes the parts again as it starts is not known.
- **Verifier.** `binary::the_binary_logs_the_last_state_hash` (`verifier/tests/binary.rs`) and the harness's `verifier::a_matchs_journal_publishes_the_log_its_twin_of_no_journal_holds` pass nextest's 5 s timeout at times in a full run of the suite, as the old `drive.py check` ran it, on an i9-13980HX, and pass in 0.8 s together alone (commit `00c0fba2`).

## Ready

- **Areas.** **Plan: 10.** An area's `inside` and an aura do one job in two shapes, and disagree on neutral and lifeless units (`area_spec.rs:94-101`, `area_holds.rs:47-58`, `held_pass.rs:131-139`).
- **Client.** A press drags past 4 pixels along either axis, a constant of the client's code (`client/src/press.rs`, `DRAG_PIXELS`), and the Zero Hour import does not read the game's `Mouse.ini` `DragTolerance`, the pixels within which a press of the game is still a click.
- **Client.** **Plan: 11.** A slot's charges come from progression's `Learning`, so a mode without progression shows none (`progression/src/learning.rs:44`, `client/src/hud/mod.rs:103`).
- **Combat.** **Plan: 10.** A splash reaches a unit within `<` its radius, where the one reach rule is `≤` (`attacks.rs:120-122`, `metric.rs:54-73`).
- **Mode.** A spatial map's height grid and `[ground]` load into its `MapBook`, and the sim reads neither: `MapBook::point` takes a spatial map's position as its point in space.
- **Net-common.** **Plan: 3.** The stack's bench groups take their sampling from one policy, `Scale` (`net-common/src/bench/scale.rs`), and Campfire's set theirs in each group (`harness/src/bench/*.rs`): two sampling policies for one workspace's benches.
- **Net-stack.** **Plan: 3.** Three counting allocators do one job: `common`'s allocation audit (`common/src/alloc_audit/counting_allocator.rs`), the harness's (`harness/src/bench/allocations.rs`) and `net-stack`'s (`net-stack/tests/alloc/allocator/mod.rs`), each with its own report.
- **Net-stack.** **Plan: 6.** Bevy's component hooks of the stack are named `on_add_hook`, `on_insert_hook` and `on_remove_hook` (13 functions, as in `net-messages/src/event_sender.rs`), where a hook is the scripts' alone (02-engine-core.md, Names) and Campfire's own hooks are named by what they do, as `Route::pushed`.
- **Net-stack.** **Plan: 3.** No lane of `cargo drive` lints the channels without `compression_lz4`, runs rustdoc with its warnings denied, or measures coverage, which `13-net-stack/testing.md` plans.
- **Net.** **Plan: 8.** A fragment count from the peer sizes an allocation before any check, in the Lightyear fork's `transport/channel/fragments/receive.rs:137-147` at `54e6be2`: one datagram that names 2³⁵ fragments stops the server.
- **Net.** **Plan: 9.** A link that did not join uses every channel with no budget (`net_protocol/mod.rs:96-117`, `offering.rs:40, 176-193`).
- **Net.** **Plan: 9.** The offer, the input ack and the chain history allocate for each message (`offering.rs:259-263`, `input_ack.rs:18-27`, `chain_history/mod.rs:45-52`).
- **Net.** **Plan: 9.** `SimClient` holds the player's session key as a `Keypair`, a `Copy` value, which its plugin keeps for the app's life and no one erases, as `Plugin::build` takes `&self` and cannot move a `Signer` out (`net/src/sim_client/mod.rs`, `SimClient`).
- **Orders.** **Plan: 12.** `PlayerOrders`, a scratch of checked unit orders, has the `<X>Orders` name of the systems `TradeOrders` and `LearnOrders` (`orders/src/unit_orders.rs:24`).
- **Quic.** **Plan: 8.** `EndpointState::admit` (`quic/src/endpoint_state.rs`) counts an address's live connections by a walk of every peer, for each new connection past its Retry: a flood of handshakes from many addresses walks every peer for each, where `Admission` keeps the address's bucket by its key.
- **Verifier.** No log or replay starts from a checkpoint's `LogCarry` (`protocol/src/checkpoint/log_carry.rs`): the log builds, writes and compares the carry, and a verifier replays every log from tick 0, so a host that prunes its logs past a checkpoint leaves a segment nothing verifies, where designs 02 (Session log) and 05 (Checkpoints and results) plan a segment's verification from its checkpoint alone.
