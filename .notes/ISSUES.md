# Issues

The issue log: every open problem of the project, under the step it waits for. The rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#issue-log).

## Decide

- **Client files.** A look's set may name a condition of `ConditionTest::Never`, a condition of Zero Hour that no test of the engine's says, which the client counts as lacked in its choice of a state: names of one game's conditions the engine carries, where AGENTS.md's Direction asks the import to resolve them. In the import of 2026-10-26, 76 conditions are of `Never` and 13762 sets name one; 11914 of those sets no unit draws, as a set of the same tested conditions and fewer of `Never` beats each; the other 1848, in 404 draws, can be drawn and name 21 of the conditions, `sold` in 1362 of them. Dropping every condition of `Never` from the sets changes the drawn state for some unit in 391 of the 952 draws that name one. The question: what carries a set's conditions that no test says. The options: the import trims the sets no unit draws and the client keeps `Never` for the rest, which keeps Zero Hour's choice exact and the 21 names in the engine's data; the import trims those sets and writes each other set's count of conditions no test says in place of their names, `Never` removed, which keeps the choice exact and the engine free of the names; or the import drops the conditions, which changes the 391 draws' choices.
