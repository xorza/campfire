# Issues

The issue log: every open problem of the project, under the step it waits for. The rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#issue-log).

## Decide

- **Production.** A mode of `production` with no `navigation` loads a `build` action, as the load asks only for `production`, but the sim installs no system that starts a build without navigation, so the order does nothing; the client's command panel hides its button. The options: the load refuses a build in a mode of no navigation, or a build without navigation places its site with no test of room.

## Research

- **Navigation.** The levels by size of B2 make the Collide stage slower for walkers of ordinary sizes, with no wide body present, and the 3v3's ticks faster. On an i9-13980HX, with each walker's cell one `CellKey`, `integration/collision/spread` takes 95.5 µs against 59.8 µs with one level of cells twice the widest walker's radius, `crowded` 325.0 µs against 257.4 µs, `boxes` 681.4 µs against 613.8 µs, and `bridge`, with a 1,117 × 100 m box 2 m past the crowded scene, 715.4 µs against 650.9 µs; the scenes' walkers of 0.2 to 1.19 m spread over four levels, and each occupied cell searches the nine cells around its own at each coarser level. `integration/server_tick/mean_3v3` takes 479.2 ms, and 519.4 ms with one level.
- **Net.** The LAN check fails at times in CI on Ubuntu: a bot's link logs `Received a ping that is not present` from `lightyear_sync::ping::manager`, the check counts it a failure, and a rerun of the same commit passes (run 38538783614, commit `0bc73b7c`).
- **Quic.** `EndpointState::admit` (`quic/src/endpoint_state.rs`) counts an address's live connections by a walk of every peer, for each new connection past its Retry: a flood of handshakes from many addresses walks every peer for each, where `Admission` keeps the address's bucket by its key.
- **Quic.** The send queue keeps `quinn-proto`'s 1 MiB: the delay a full queue adds is its size over the connection's sending rate, tens of seconds at the minimum window, and Lightyear queues a reliable message again while its last copy still waits there. No scenario measures the largest burst one client receives, as at its join, or the delay the queue adds: the harness's link models delay and loss, not a rate.
- **Quic.** No bench measures the frame's two systems, the thread's batch, or the frame's waits for the mutex, nor a flood of handshakes from many addresses: what a handshake costs the thread under the mutex, and how long a batch of them holds the frame, which sets how many new connections a pass takes ([QUIC transport](../docs/design/15-quic-transport.md), D14). No pass bounds its new connections.
- **Quic.** The thread allocates under the mutex at times `quinn-proto`'s random numbers choose: 8 times at a connection's first key update, within its first 1,000 packets, and once when the packet numbers a connection received first hold three ranges, as each packet number a sender skips leaves a gap until the ranges before it are acknowledged. A read also allocates a new arena when `RecvBatch`'s ring comes back to an arena that a message slice Lightyear keeps still holds.
- **Runner.** `resume::the_3v3_match_resumes_from_each_checkpoint_to_the_same_hashes` runs past the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, with `--test-threads 1`, 1.41 to 1.45 s at commit aa1e8aac, where `source/.config/nextest.toml` gives it 0.79 s on an M5. On the 1.100 beta, alone, 1.86 s at commit 3e7a72a0; its profile is flat, no function above 3 % at 20 kHz, and the cost is a resume from each checkpoint, each a replay of the log to it.
- **Runner.** `proving_match::a_snapshot_of_drawn_values_restores_or_is_refused_and_never_panics` runs past the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, with `--test-threads 1`, 1.00 to 1.03 s at commit aa1e8aac, and 1.95 s through `./drive.py test` at commit 086b4cf1. The five ticks after each restored draw take most of it, in the navigation's broadphase and collider. On the 1.100 beta, alone, 2.04 s at commit 3e7a72a0; its profile is flat, no function above 7 %, spread over restores, archetype moves, Rhai calls and movement, and the cost is the sweep's size: each state type 16 draws, each restored draw 5 ticks.
- **Runner.** `proving_match::a_snapshot_restores_and_a_flawed_one_never_panics` runs past the bound of 1 s for a test alone: on an i9-13980HX, with `--test-threads 1`, 1.11 s at commit e43f95af, and 0.87 s in a later run. On the 1.100 beta, alone, 1.10 s at commit 3e7a72a0: a restore for each byte of the snapshot, and 5 ticks after each one that restores.
- **Server.** `stop::a_stop_closes_each_link_and_exits_with_its_code` fails at times in CI on macOS: the server's start takes longer than its 800 ms to log `Listening`, in runs 38742678456 (commit `10e52519`), 38754786637 (`a40a6e4b`) and on master 38752703785 (`e0f2243d`), and a run of another commit passes.
- **Stats.** A unit a predicting client predicts again, as its owner changes and `Unpredicted` goes (`net/src/sim_client/mod.rs`, `mark_unpredicted`), keeps the change ticks its parts had while hidden: a pass that visits what changed by those ticks, as `Holds::want_passives` and the stats refresh do, may pass over it until a part of it changes, where a pass over every unit takes it at once. Whether Lightyear's prediction writes the parts again as it starts is not known.

## Ready

- **Combat.** An attack lands its damage in the tick it fires, where Zero Hour deals a weapon of no projectile `distance / WeaponSpeed` frames after the shot, rounded up, when that is a frame or more (`WeaponStore::setDelayedDamage`): the import drops each non-laser weapon whose `WeaponSpeed` is below the default, 7 of the install's starting weapons, `HumveeGun` and `SentryDroneGun` among them (design 19, D6).
- **Harness.** `source/crates/test/harness/Cargo.toml` declares `campfire-verifier` as a dependency that the crate's library does not use: cargo's `unused_dependencies` warns of it in each build on the 1.100 beta.
- **Runner.** `source/crates/session/runner/Cargo.toml` declares the optional dependency `campfire-package-disk`, which no code of the crate uses: cargo's `unused_dependencies` warns of it in each build on the 1.100 beta.
