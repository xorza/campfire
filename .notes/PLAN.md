# Plan

The open steps, in the order they are done, and the foundations. The rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#workflow).

## Veterancy and generals

The slice of [Veterancy and generals](../docs/design/generals.md): a player of a Zero Hour skirmish sees their units rise to veteran, elite and heroic by their kills, with chevrons and bonuses; sees their general's rank rise by their side's kills; buys sciences with the rank's points, which unlock their buttons; and fires Emergency Repair from the command center. Its facts are [Zero Hour's veterancy, general's ranks, sciences and powers](../docs/research/zero-hour-generals.md); its checks are that document's.

### VG1. Veterancy in the engine

Touches progression, stats, combat and the test packages; it stands first, as the import of VG2 writes what it adds.

- [ ] Every type of a track shares the track's thresholds (generals.md, A type's own thresholds): `TypeTracksData` lists names alone (`type_tracks_data.rs:7`), and `TrackBook` holds one `Thresholds` for each track (`track_data/mod.rs:9`). An entry of a type's `tracks` is a name or `{ track, levels }`, of the track's count, and `Experience::add` (`experience.rs:80`) reads its unit's type's thresholds. Checks: a type's own thresholds raise its unit at its own experience, and the load refuses a count unlike the track's. Builds: A name is resolved once.
- [ ] A level gives no modifier, and every level a point (generals.md, A level holds a modifier; generals.md, A track gives points by level): `TrackData` holds `levels` and `level` alone (`track_data/mod.rs:9`), and `Points::gain` adds one for each level (`points.rs:35`). A track gains `modifiers`, one for each level from 2, which its unit holds while at that level as a passive is held (`type_passive.rs:18`), dropped at the next; and `points`, for each level from 1, one each by default. Checks: a unit holds the modifier of its level alone, and its stats follow at once; a track of `points = [0, 0, 0]` gives no point; the 3v3's levels give the points they gave. Builds: A pass visits what changed.
- [ ] A mode has one share of a death's experience, and its killer is any but a friend (generals.md, A death's experience goes by shares; generals.md, A share's killer passes a filter): `ModeExperienceData` names one track (`experience_data.rs:15`), `ExperienceBook` holds one share (`experience_book.rs:26`), a list by level reads the victim's level on the share's track (`experience_book.rs:194`), and the killer passes when not friendly (`experience_book.rs:218`). The mode's `[[experience]]` lists shares, each with a `killer` filter from the victim's side, all but friends by default; a list by level reads the victim's `Level`; the test packages' modes write their one share as a list. Checks: two shares of one death each give their track; a killer the filter refuses gains nothing; the 3v3's scripted match keeps its levels. Builds: What a module adds is declared once.
- [ ] A unit starts at level 1 whatever its player holds (generals.md, A start level by a modifier): `Experience::new` starts each track at its first level (`experience.rs:46`), from the spawn's part (`lib.rs:110`). A type's track entry takes `start = { level, requires }`, a player modifier: a unit spawns at that level, its experience at the level's threshold, while its player holds the modifier. Checks: a unit of a player of the modifier spawns at its level with its modifier and its stats; one of a player without it at level 1. Builds: A kind is a marker, and an entity spawns whole.
- [ ] A weapon's bonus is one stat (generals.md, A weapon's bonus is a sum of stats): `AttackData` holds `rate_bonus` and `damage_bonus` as one `Stat` each (`attack_data.rs:29`, `attack_data.rs:34`), resolved to one `StatId` (`combat/src/lib.rs:416`). Each takes a stat or a list of stats, and the bonus is their sum. Checks: a weapon of two bonus stats fires and hits by their sum, exact to the tick and the point; one stat keeps today's goldens. Builds: A capability serves every game.

### VG2. Zero Hour's veterancy

Touches the Zero Hour import and its check; it stands after VG1, whose data it writes.

- [ ] No unit gains a level (generals.md, Veterancy is progression's data): the imported mode holds no `progression` (`rules_package/mod.rs:135`), the object INI passes over `ExperienceValue`, `ExperienceRequired` and `IsTrainable` (`object_ini/mod.rs:68`), and `GameDataIni` over `WeaponBonus` and `HealthBonus_*` (`game_data_ini/mod.rs:133`). The mode gains `progression`, a `level` track `veterancy` of the commonest `ExperienceRequired`, `0 100 200 400`, a `modifiers` of `veteran`, `elite` and `heroic` of `GameData.ini`'s damage, rate of fire and life bonuses less 1, `points` of none, and a share to the killer of `enemies`, victims `!constructing`; each trainable type its entry of its own thresholds where they differ, and each type of an `ExperienceValue` its `gives` by level; each weapon its bonus stats, the veterancy's beside its stage's and its upgrade's (`combat_import/mod.rs:470`, `combat_import/mod.rs:616`); a trainable type of thresholds not ascending is a counted loss. ### VG3. Generals in the engine

Touches progression, the players, the script API, the bots and the visibility kinds; it stands after VG2, so its tracks join a mode that has them.

- [ ] A track belongs to units alone (generals.md, A track of players): `Experience` and `Points` are hosted by a unit (`experience.rs:99`, `points.rs:50`), and a share gives to its killer or the units near (`experience_data.rs:26`). A track takes `holder = "player"`, whose experience and points each player's entity holds from the match's start, of the kind `progression` sent to its owner; a share takes `to = "player"`, the killer's player. Checks: a kill raises its killer's player's track, by the victim's level, and its points; the leak test holds them to their owner; a save and a restore keep them. Builds: What a module adds is declared once.
- [ ] No player learns a perk (generals.md, A science is a perk of a player; generals.md, A perk's modifier is a player modifier): `grant_perk` and `has_perk` are planned (`progression_api.rs:22`, `progression_api.rs:63`), and `ProgressionDirective` holds `Learn` of a slot alone (`progression_directive.rs:6`). The mode's `[perks.<name>]` give `cost`, `requires`, `track` and `level`, and `modifier`, a player modifier of the package, resolved at the load to a `PerkId`; `ProgressionDirective::LearnPerk` learns one for its player in Inputs, in input order, when the player holds its points, its level and its requires, and none of cost none; `grant_perk(player, id)` grants one to the mode's script; a player's perks are state, and its modifier holds while it has it. Checks: a perk learned spends its cost and gives its modifier; one of a missing level, requires or points, or of no cost, is dropped; a second learn of one tick sees the point the first spent; a granted perk needs nothing; the reference lists the calls and the section. Builds: A name is resolved once.
- [ ] A bot learns nothing (generals.md, A bot buys its skill set): a bot's `order` gives units a directive (`06-script-api-reference.md`, `order`). A bot's `order` of no units carries a perk's learn, and a bot reads its player's points and perks. Checks: a bot's learn applies as a player's does, and a replay of its log learns the same. Builds: A capability serves every game.

## Combat response

The slice of [Combat response](../docs/design/combat-response.md): a player of a Zero Hour skirmish sees a unit under fire from beyond its reach bring its idle, armed allies after the attacker and back, a tank fire from its turret as it drives, and a unit of two weapons fire the better one at each target. Its facts are [Zero Hour's retaliation, turret scan and weapon choice](../docs/research/zero-hour-combat-response.md); its checks are that document's. It stands after Veterancy and generals, whose `vehicle` tag the preferences read.

### CR1. Weapon choice in the engine

Touches actions, combat and orders; it stands first, as the turret and the chase of the later batches read the weapon it chooses.

- [ ] A unit attacks with its first weapon that selects its target (combat-response.md, Combat chooses the weapon; combat-response.md, An estimate is the weapon's damage as the armor takes it; combat-response.md, An attack may prefer its targets): `ActionSlots::weapon_in` takes the first slot whose action arms (`action_slots/mod.rs:125`), which `Armed::weapon_against` returns (`armed.rs:18`). An attack takes `prefers`, a tag filter; combat chooses, each tick, for each unit the index of attack targets holds, the weapon of its target by readiness, preference, estimated damage and slot, its estimate the attack's amount times its bonus as the target's armor takes its kind. Checks: a ready weapon over a cooling one, a preferred over a stronger one, a preferred one cooling over a ready one it does not prefer, the stronger of two by armor, the lower slot of two equal, none of an estimate of 0; the 3v3's goldens do not move. Builds: A pass visits what changed.
- [ ] Each reader of a unit's weapon chooses it again (combat-response.md, The chosen weapon is the attack's; combat-response.md, The scan reaches by any weapon): the attack's start (`attacks.rs:194`), the turret (`turret/aiming.rs:79`), the chase (`destinations.rs:60`), combat's effect (`combat_effect.rs:116`) and the scan (`acquiring.rs:118`) each call `weapon_in`. The unit's `ActionProgress` keeps the chosen slot beside its attack target (`action_progress/mod.rs:431`), which the start, the turret and the chase read; the scan takes a target a weapon that selects it reaches. Checks: a chase walks to the chosen weapon's range, and its turret aims for it; a scan takes a target its second weapon alone reaches. Builds: Each crate depends only on lower layers.
