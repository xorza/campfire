# Plan — next steps

Open items only, in order. Remove an item when it is done. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. Stage context: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md); design: [design/](design/).

1. **Mode from its package**: the runner loads a mode package and its dependencies with the load checks of design 08, and builds the match from it in place of `StandInMode`: its capabilities, its map (lanes and structures, in a `map/` the 3v3 package does not have yet), its unit types from `units.toml`, rewritten in capability sections, with times rounded up to whole ticks, and `mode.rhai` with its timers and `spawn_wave`. The manifest sets the tick rate range, the script pools and `max_move_speed` (6.0 m/s for the 3v3); a pool smaller than one call fails the load, in place of the assert `Units::install` makes today; every move speed is clamped to the cap, and a homing projectile not faster than it fails the load. The session terms name the engine release tag, the mode's fingerprint and its dependencies' fingerprints, so the session id binds them; the verifier replays only a log whose release and packages it holds, checked by fingerprint. Test: a 3v3 match from the reference package replays to the same hashes; a log that names another release, another mode fingerprint or a missing dependency, or a tick rate outside the manifest's range, is refused with its own error; and a caster creep projectile slower than the cap fails the load.
2. **Fog of war**: grid visibility per team, and each client receives only the units its team sees, through Lightyear's replication visibility. Test: an enemy outside sight is not in the client's `World`, and appears on the tick it comes into sight.
3. **LAN play**: `server` and `client` binaries over WebTransport, with the certificate hash pinned and the connect challenge of design 05, which checks the delegation's expiry against the server clock; the client signs with auxiliary randomness from the OS, which `net` lacks today. The client draws capsules, predicts its own hero and interpolates the other units. Exit: two machines play a 1v1 lane, and the log verifies on another OS.
