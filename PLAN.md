# Plan — next steps

The concrete steps of the current stages of the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), in order, open items only: remove a step when it is done, and a section when it is empty. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. A later stage's steps come here when it starts, after its design step ([Workflow](AGENTS.md#workflow)).

## Stage 6: Sessions

The design is [Sessions](docs/design/10-sessions.md): a journal that makes the log durable, crash restore, slots and their controllers, reconnects and late joins, server bots, receipts, checkpoints by a copy of the changed state, saves and loads, and the local server. Each step updates the design documents it changes: design 05's log format and inputs, design 02's lifecycle, design 08 and its generated reference for each name it makes run.

2. **K2. Keep the TLS identity** (server; [Crash restore](docs/design/10-sessions.md#crash-restore)): the server keeps its TLS certificate and key in its data directory, made when missing and made again at a start when the certificate would expire within two days. Tests: two server starts in turn on one data directory give one certificate hash. Waits on a question: [K2](PLAN_QUESTIONS.md#k2-keep-the-tls-identity).
9. **R1. Crash restore** (runner, net, server; [Crash restore](docs/design/10-sessions.md#crash-restore)): a server whose data directory holds a session with no result restores it, and refuses, naming the release, a journal whose terms name another engine release: within the restore window, `--restore-window`, 120 s by default, by the journal's modification time, it rebuilds the log, replays the match from tick 0, logs `Disconnected` for each player's slot and runs on with a new start, its certificate kept; past it, it logs the `aborted` result and publishes the log. Design 10's systemd unit is the hosting example. Tests: a `LocalMatch` whose server is dropped mid-match and made again from its data directory plays on, its per-tick hashes up to the cut equal to the first run's; a restore past the window publishes an aborted log that verifies; a journal of another release is refused.
10. **P1. Reconnect, late join and leave** (net, client, server; [Reconnect and late join](docs/design/10-sessions.md#reconnect-and-late-join)): the server's door after the start, whose offer carries the grace period and the restore window; the newest login of a main key wins, its slot's old link ended, in the lobby too, which design 05's connection rule then says; a leaver takes back a slot still reserved, played by a bot or open; each join's delegation checked for expiry, an expired one answered by a new session key, logged as a `Renew`; the new link shown every unit its team sees at once; `Join`, `Renew`, `Connected`, `Disconnected` and `Leave` logged as their events come; the grace period, `--grace`, 60 s by default; the resume message; the client's rejoin with its chain history, which it cuts back to the server's head or takes when it has none, and its refusal of a rewritten head; the client, whose replicated units Lightyear despawns when its link fails, clears its predicted ticks and its sent inputs, and tries again after waits from 1 s doubling to 8 s, each drawn at random below its bound, until the restore window and the grace period pass; the grace period runs by the server's monotonic clock; a leave message, which a bot script's `end` sends; the session ends when no player slot is held and no link stays. Tests, net scenarios: a client whose link is cut and comes back within the grace period, no unit of the old link left in its world, its discarded inputs logged; a second login of a seated main key while the first link lives, which takes the slot and ends the first link; a delegation that expired, renewed; one past it, under each `leaver` rule, with a late joiner where the rule lets one in; a late join to an open slot and a bot takeover; the restored server of R1, which every client rejoins.
11. **B1. Server bots** (net, server, runner; [Server bots](docs/design/10-sessions.md#server-bots)): the plan's bot slots, `--bot <slot>=<orders file>`, and the leavers' bot, `--takeover <orders file>`; the driver, whose payloads the server signs as `Bot` inputs before each tick; order scripts' `[[input]]` entries for mode inputs. Tests: an order script with inputs parses, and each flaw is refused; a `LocalMatch` with a server bot applies its orders and its pick in their ticks, and its log verifies; a leaver under `bot` gets the takeover bot's orders.
12. **Q1. Receipts** (protocol, net, client; [Receipts](docs/design/10-sessions.md#receipts)): the signed receipt, over durable inputs only, with the delegation's id, which design 05's receipts then name; the server sends one about once a second to each player whose durable seq advanced; the client checks it against its own chain, keeps the newest, and writes it to its data directory. Tests: a receipt round-trips and is refused under another key or head; a net scenario's client holds a receipt whose seq the journal had synced.
13. **H1. Every state write marks its change** (sim, capabilities; [D3](docs/design/10-sessions.md#decisions), [Checkpoints](docs/design/10-sessions.md#checkpoints)): the registry's copy of each state type's changed values, by the world's change tick at the last checkpoint, which the checkpoint then advances, and its apply; the entity index's gained and lost ids; the drain of removed components; `StateCopy`, a world that follows the match by those copies; every state write that skips Bevy's change detection now marks it, the modifiers' share values in `stats/refresh.rs` among them, with the derived refresh no longer started by them; no state type holds interior mutability. Tests: the proving match, the lane match, the 3v3 and the archetype-shuffle run keep a `StateCopy` through each tick's copy, and its snapshot equals a full one after every tick.
14. **H2. Restore equivalence** (runner, sim, capabilities; [Checkpoints](docs/design/10-sessions.md#checkpoints)): `Runner::resume`, a match built from packages, a snapshot and a log carry; every gap the test finds closed as state, or as derived data rebuilt after a restore. Tests: the proving match, the lane match and the 3v3, each snapshot at three ticks, a death and a respawn between them, resume and play to the same per-tick hashes as the run that made them.
15. **H3. The checkpoint thread** (runner, server; [Checkpoints](docs/design/10-sessions.md#checkpoints)): a checkpoint at a tick boundary builds the delta, which the checkpoint thread applies, snapshots, hashes and writes, syncs, then signs its record; the journal's `CheckpointBegun` and `CheckpointDone`; the next segment's seed from its boundary; a checkpoint past the seed chain's last segment ends the session with an `aborted` result; a restore from the latest done checkpoint, a checkpoint begun but not done taken again as the replay passes its boundary. Tests: a session checkpointed at ticks 30 and 60 restores from the second to the same hashes, and one cut between the second's begin and done restores from the first and takes the second again, and its log verifies with its snapshots; the work record's worst 3v3 tick with a checkpoint every 100 ticks against none, in the tick bench.
16. **O1. The local server** (client, net; [Local server](docs/design/10-sessions.md#local-server)): `campfire-client --local`, with the server's bot and open slots as its options, starts the server as a thread linked in process, its data in the client's data directory; the pause key and the speed keys. Tests: an in-process local match runs no sim tick while paused and resumes at the same tick; at speed 2 it runs twice the ticks a second of its clock.
17. **V1. Saves and loads** (capabilities, runner, net, client; [Saves and loads](docs/design/10-sessions.md#saves-and-loads)): `[saves]` with `by`, an enum `SaveBy`, and `autosave_ms`; `ctx.save()` runs; a checkpoint at the end of the tick the mode asks in, at each autosave, and at a player's save on the local server unless `by` is `mode`; a load of a checkpoint as a new segment, logged `Loaded`, that every client resumes from; the save and quick-load keys. Tests: the proving match saves by `ctx.save()` and by autosave in their ticks; a load drops the log after the save, and the published log's segments verify; a player's save is refused under `by = "mode"`.
18. **X1. The LAN check** (lan-check; [Tests](docs/design/10-sessions.md#tests)): the check kills the server mid-match, starts it again on its data directory, stops one bot client and starts it again with its key file, and passes when every order either took effect as the rules say or was logged as discarded at a resume, and the published log verifies to the server's final hash; and it plays a match of a client bot against server bots on a local server with no network, whose log verifies. CI runs both on every OS.
