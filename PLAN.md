The open steps, in the order they are done, and the foundations. The rules are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md#workflow).

## Slice: Missiles

A player who trains Tank Hunters, Missile Defenders, tunnel defenders or rocket buggies, or builds a Patriot battery, sees them fire missiles that leave on a straight run, turn and speed up toward their targets, and strike them; a unit too near its target backs away before it fires, and a blast's outer ring wounds less than its center. The Stinger soldiers', the Tomahawks' and the Scud launchers' weapons still drop, by causes in the issue log. Its decisions are in [Actions](docs/design/04-capabilities/actions.md), [Combat](docs/design/04-capabilities/combat.md) and [Zero Hour](docs/design/09-zero-hour.md).

### 1. The play (S)

The user. Last: it ends the slice.

- **Check:** the user plays Tournament Desert in the client and sees a Tank Hunter's and a Missile Defender's missiles fly and strike, a rocket buggy back away from a near target, and a Patriot battery fire.
