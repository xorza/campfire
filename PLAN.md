# Plan — next steps

Open items only, in order. Remove an item when it is done. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. Stage context: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md); design: [design/](design/).

1. **Unsteerable seeds**: every segment seed is fixed at the session's start, from inputs no one can change or choose with the outcome in view, as design 05's seed chain and seed rules now say.
   - Each player's seed contribution moves into their delegation as a `seed_contribution` tag (`DelegationTag::SeedContribution`, a term of `DelegationTerms`), so the main key signs it after the seed commitment is fixed in the session id. `SessionPlayer` becomes the delegation alone, and the log file no longer writes the contribution beside it.
   - The server seed becomes a `SeedChain` of a root and a length: `seed(k)` hashes the root `N − 1 − k` times, and the terms' commitment is the hash of `seed(0)`. `ServerSeed::check(k, commitment)` hashes `k + 1` times. `segment_seed(k)` mixes in the segment's index and the contributions from the delegations. The log keeps one segment until checkpoints come, and reveals `seed(0)`.
   - Test: a delegation without the tag, or with it malformed, is refused with its tag; a contribution changed in the delegation's JSON breaks the signature; no one-bit flip of any byte of a log file replays any more, where 96 flips of the contributions replay today; `seed(1)` gives `seed(0)` by one hash and passes the check for segment 1 but not for segment 0; `seed(0)` passes for segment 0; segments 0 and 1 have different seeds; and a changed contribution changes every segment's seed.
2. **Mode from its package**: the runner loads a mode package and its dependencies with the load checks of design 08, and builds the match from it in place of `StandInMode`: its capabilities, its map (lanes and structures, in a `map/` the 3v3 package does not have yet), its unit types from `units.toml`, rewritten in capability sections, with times rounded up to whole ticks, and `mode.rhai` with its timers and `spawn_wave`. The manifest sets the tick rate range, the script pools and `max_move_speed` (6.0 m/s for the 3v3); a pool smaller than one call fails the load, in place of the assert `Units::install` makes today; every move speed is clamped to the cap, and a homing projectile not faster than it fails the load. The session terms name the engine release tag, the mode's fingerprint and its dependencies' fingerprints, so the session id binds them; the verifier replays only a log whose release and packages it holds, checked by fingerprint. Test: a 3v3 match from the reference package replays to the same hashes; a log that names another release, another mode fingerprint or a missing dependency, or a tick rate outside the manifest's range, is refused with its own error; and a caster creep projectile slower than the cap fails the load.
3. **Fog of war**: grid visibility per team, and each client receives only the units its team sees, through Lightyear's replication visibility. Test: an enemy outside sight is not in the client's `World`, and appears on the tick it comes into sight.
4. **LAN play**: `server` and `client` binaries over WebTransport, with the certificate hash pinned and the connect challenge of design 05, which checks the delegation's expiry against the server clock; the client signs with auxiliary randomness from the OS, which `net` lacks today. The client draws capsules, predicts its own hero and interpolates the other units. Exit: two machines play a 1v1 lane, and the log verifies on another OS.
