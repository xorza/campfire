1. **Circle collision**: the second playtest worked, and the log verified on Linux and macOS, but units walk through each other; the design has circles on a plane, and the Collide stage has no system yet. Each unit type that collides declares a radius in its data, and the client draws each unit at that radius. In Collide, after Move, the core pushes each pair of overlapping living units apart along the line between them, by exact fixed-point steps, in stable-id order, so every machine gets the same result. A unit that does not walk, such as a tower, does not move, and the other unit takes the whole push. Dead units and projectiles do not collide. The client predicts its own avatar against the units it holds, so a push by a unit it does not predict comes as a correction. Test: two units walking into each other stop at the sum of their radii; a unit walking into a tower stops at its edge; the same match gives the same hash on a replay. This closes stage 3.
