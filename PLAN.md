# Plan — next steps

Open items only, in order. Remove an item when it is done. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. Stage context: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md); design: [design/](design/).

1. **CI on three platforms**: a GitHub Actions workflow runs the check chain (`fmt`, `clippy`, `test`) on Linux x86_64, Windows x86_64 and macOS aarch64, with the Linux packages Bevy's window backends need. The Linux job also runs `campfire-lan-check` and keeps the session log and its hash as an artifact; the Windows and macOS jobs verify that log with their own `campfire-verifier` and compare the hash. This closes stage 0, and the golden and prototype tests then show equal hashes on every platform, as stage 2 asks.
2. **Vertical slice playtest**: a 1v1 on LAN between two machines, with the test heroes' abilities, the players' attack and cast orders and the readable match, and the log verified on a third OS. Write down what makes the match fun or not, and which items stage 3 still needs. This closes stage 3, or names what is missing.
3. **Neutral core vocabulary**: a mode declares its damage kinds, stats and resources in data, and the reference MOBA and the test lane declare theirs; the core's mode calls and fields take the neutral names avatar, loadout, spawn group and path. Test: a test mode with other damage kinds, stats and resources runs, and the fixed lists are gone from the core.
4. **Map bounds**: every map has bounds, a closed rectangle on the ground plane, and no unit is ever outside them. The vision grid is derived from the bounds and a cell size, so the two cannot disagree, and a point on the max edge lies in the last cell. A move order's point clamps to the bounds; after Move and Collide the core clamps every unit that moved; a spawn outside the bounds fails its call, and a map whose structures, spawns or lanes lie outside fails its load. So every unit stands in a cell, and a unit next to an enemy is always in its sight. Test: an order past the edge stops the unit on the edge, where an enemy beside it sees it; a spawn outside fails; a map with a structure outside fails its load; the cast scenario's heroes can stand on the edge again.
