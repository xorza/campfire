# Plan — current slice

The steps of the current slice ([Workflow](AGENTS.md#workflow)), in order, open items only: remove a step when it is done. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. The slice ends when the user plays it in the client. Slice 3 runs first; slice 2 goes on from S1 after it. The QUIC transport's slice has its own plan, [PLAN-QUIC.md](PLAN-QUIC.md).

## Slice 3 — Replication audiences

The user sees their own gold, experience and cooldowns in the HUD, and another team's pools and cooldowns are on no client of theirs; a spectator watches the match, through all fog or one team's; a mode's `[visibility]` that shows every hero's items to all shows them; and a host that lets dead players watch the other team gives a dead hero's player that view until it respawns. The design is [Replication audiences](docs/design/14-replication-audiences.md); each step folds its rules into the design 04 file of each capability it changes, and into design 03's script state.

1. **A3. The mode's and the host's visibility, and the spectator** (`package`, `protocol`, `net`, `server`, `client`; D1, D4, D14, D15): `[visibility]` with `spectators`, `out` and `host`, and its load checks; the host's `--visibility` and `--spectators`, which the server checks against the mode's `host` list as it starts; the settings in the session's terms, the session id's `v3` and the log's tag `v4`, design 05; a spectator's link, which holds no slot, and its rooms: every room with `all`, the teams its message chooses with `teams`; the client's spectator, which predicts nothing, and its choice of team. Check: the load refuses each flaw of `[visibility]`, and the server each flaw of the host's settings; two sessions of other visibility settings have other ids; the leak test on the 3v3, with both settings, through the host's settings where the mode leaves them to it; a player's allies hold its gold when the mode adds allies to `resources`, and a spectator as the mode sets; a spectator holds the relations and the match's end.
2. **A5. Players out of play** (`capabilities`, `net`, `client`; D16): out of play while every avatar a player holds is dead, or while `ctx.set_out` holds it out, a field of its player entity; its link, which carries it, and the filters and rooms that follow it; design 08's script API, and its reference generated again. Check: in the leak test, a dead hero's player holds, with `out = "spectators"`, the other team's units through the spectators' fog and the kinds whose audiences hold spectators until its respawn, and none of them after it; with `team`, nothing more than its team's; a living player that a script holds out holds the same as a dead one.
3. **A6. Prediction** (`capabilities`, `net`; D5, D11, D12): `UnitStats` for every unit whose inputs a client holds; the prediction test. Check: a hero walks into an ally's aura, whose numbers come from its source's stats, with no correction; with `modifiers` hidden from enemies, an enemy aura corrects once.

## Slice 2 — Wide bodies, uses, the shop and gravity

The user plays a 3v3 match of the MOBA test content: their hero holds a use on the jungle's shrine, buys items from the shop panel, and casts their actives with 1 to 6, a ward at the ground point; a test map holds a 1,117 m box that walkers go around. The design is [Wide bodies, uses, the shop and gravity](docs/design/13-bodies-uses-shop-gravity.md); each step folds its rules into the design 04 file of its capability.

1. **S1. Names and readers** (`capabilities`, `package`, `packages/test/moba`; D8): `name` on item types and the MOBA's names; a public reader of the shop, an item's price for a unit, whether a unit stands where it can buy, and an action's targeting. A player's resources reach it on its player entity, `PlayerResources::of_player`. Check: a price less held components as computed by hand; an item type with no `name`, or one a locale lacks, fails its load.
2. **S2. The shop panel and item keys** (`client`; D9, D10, D11): Bevy's UI features; the shop panel, the inventory bar, keys 1 to 6, and every cast key aimed as its action takes. Check: the key's target for an action of each targeting; the panel's dimmed items for an avatar alive out of a shop, too poor and with no room; the user buys a ward, places it at a point with its key, sells an item and swaps two slots.
3. **U1. The `use` kind** (`capabilities`, `package`, `client`; D4, D5): the `use` and `uses` sections and their load checks; the `use` order; `damage_interrupts`; `on_channel_tick`'s target; the script API reference generated again. Check: a use resolves on its object; a unit without `uses`, one that `users` refuses and one out of range that cannot walk drop the order; a held use is cut by a new order, a block of `use`, a death and, with `damage_interrupts`, by damage, not by a heal; a second use of the same object keeps its slot, and one of another object fills it afresh.
4. **U2. The shrine** (`packages/test/moba`, `client`; D6): the shrine, its action, `shrine_blessing` and `shrine_spent` in design 07 and the 3v3 package; a right click on a unit the avatar may use orders the use; the channel bar. Check: the scripted match has a hero use the shrine and the team's heroes take the blessing, and a second hero's use cut by a hit; the user holds a use on the shrine in the client.
5. **G1. Gravity** (`capabilities`, `package`, `packages/test/moba`; D12): the falling flight; `gravity` past planned, refused with `homing` or not positive; a grenade of the MOBA's items, which arcs to its point. Check: the points of a flight on each metric at its launch, its peak and its end as computed by hand, the planar one back at its launch height at its end; a spatial one hits a unit its chord meets and not one its straight line would; the client draws the grenade's arc.
