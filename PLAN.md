# Plan — next steps

The concrete steps of the current stages of the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), in order, open items only: remove a step when it is done, and a section when it is empty. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. A later stage's steps come here when it starts, after its design step ([Workflow](AGENTS.md#workflow)).

## Stage 5: MOBA mechanics

Every mechanic design 07 lists, run from the reference packages, in the order each needs the one before: the core's draws and vectors, the actions over time, reveals, forced movement, terrain and brush, items, the 3v3 scenario that casts them all, and last the load that refuses a planned name, once no reference package uses one. Each step runs a name or field the reference lists as planned, and marks it as running.

11. **M2. A dash as a delivery** (navigation, actions): a dash from an instant action's `on_resolve` runs its `on_end` as it ends. Tests: Night Step deals its damage as the dash ends beside its target; Grasping Wraps' pull from `on_hit` runs no `on_end`.
12. **M3. Walk in range** (actions, orders, [The pipeline](docs/design/04-capabilities/actions.md#the-pipeline)): a cast whose target, a unit or a point, is beyond its range walks in range first and starts there, as an attack's chase does, where `start_casts` now drops the order; `clamp_to_range` still casts where the unit stands. Tests: a cast at a unit and at a point beyond range each starts at the hand-computed tick the unit comes in range; a target that moves is followed.
13. **T1. Walls** (navigation, [Data](docs/design/04-capabilities/navigation.md#data)): `[[navigation.walls]]`, read as checked polygons; their cells in each layer's pathing grid by the exact center rule; smoothing and the straight goal test the cells a line crosses. Load errors for a polygon of fewer than three points, edges that cross, or an unknown layer. Tests: the cells of a triangle and of a box, at two cell sizes; a route round a wall, smoothed past its corner and never through it; a map check that refuses a path through a wall.
14. **T2. Brush** (vision): `[[grid.brush]]`, each cell's brush, and the rule that only a unit in a brush reveals its cells; a reveal sees into it. Tests: a hero in brush unseen from outside, seen from inside it and from another unit in it, and by a reveal.
15. **T3. The 3v3's terrain** (reference MOBA): walls between the lanes and the jungle, and brush beside each lane, in the 3v3 map, with its paths and camps still reachable. Tests: the map checks pass; the scripted 3v3's hashes change only where a route or a sight does, which the review checks.
16. **I1. Item types and inventories** (items, [Items](docs/design/04-capabilities/items.md)): `[items.<id>]` with its checks (costs in declared resources, components of the same package and no cycle, a stack of at least 1, its modifiers and action declared), `inventory = { slots, kind }`, and `[shop]`. Load errors for each. Tests: a table of item data, read and refused.
17. **I2. Buying and selling** (items, orders): the orders `buy`, `sell` and `swap`, the shop's regions and the dead, recipes that give up their components, stacks, and the sell share. Tests: each case, hand-computed gold, and each refusal.
18. **I3. Actives and passives** (items, actions, stats): each item's action in its slot's action slot, a consumable's uses, and its modifiers held while carried, once for two of one item. The tag effect `blocks = ["use"]` runs: it stops an item's action, and `blocks = ["cast"]` does not ([Items](docs/design/04-capabilities/items.md#rules)). Tests: a potion's heal and its last use, a stun that stops it and a silence that does not, an active's cooldown kept across a swap, and a passive's stats on and off as it is bought and sold.
19. **I4. Wards** (actions): the `spawn` effect of a mode package's action, with `duration_ms`. Tests: a sight ward spawns at its aim, hidden, sees, and despawns at its time; an avatar package's `spawn` still fails the load.
20. **I5. The 3v3's items** (reference MOBA): potions, the two wards, the elixir, and a few items with components and actives, in the mode's package; the heroes' inventories and the shop at each base. Tests: the package loads, and the item checks hold.
21. **S1. The 3v3 casts** (runner): the scripted 3v3's players learn and cast each hero's abilities and use the spells, and buy, use and sell items, beside stage 4's scenario; the match test checks each mechanic by its computed numbers. The 3v3 golden is blessed.
22. **N1. Planned names refused** (package, [One source](docs/design/08-script-api.md#one-source)): the load refuses a planned name in a script, a planned data field and a planned hook, with the name and where it stands. Tests: one flaw each; every reference package loads.
