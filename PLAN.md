# Plan — next steps

The concrete steps of the current stages of the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), in order, open items only: remove a step when it is done, and a section when it is empty. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. A later stage's steps come here when it starts, after its design step ([Workflow](AGENTS.md#workflow)).

## Stage 5: MOBA mechanics

Every mechanic design 07 lists, run from the reference packages, in the order each needs the one before: the core's draws and vectors, the actions over time, reveals, forced movement, terrain and brush, items, the 3v3 scenario that casts them all, and last the load that refuses a planned name, once no reference package uses one. Each step runs a name or field the reference lists as planned, and marks it as running.

1. **C1. Draws** (core, [Randomness](docs/design/08-script-api.md#rules)): `ctx.chance(p)` and `ctx.pick(list)` from the stream `script.draw`, one sequence for each acting unit and tick and one for the mode's calls, kept for the tick so the draws of every call follow on. Tests: hand-computed draws from a fixed seed, two calls of one unit in one tick that draw on, two units that draw apart, a `p` of 0 and 1 and outside, an empty list, and a run that draws alike from the same seed.
2. **C2. Vectors** (core, [Vectors](docs/design/08-script-api.md#rules)): `+`, `-` and `*` on vectors, each rounded once, an overflow a script error. Tests: exact sums, differences and scales, a number on either side of `*`, and an overflow.
3. **A1. Values and cooldowns** (actions, [Values and bookkeeping](docs/design/04-capabilities/actions.md#values-and-bookkeeping)): `ctx.range`, `ctx.origin`, `ctx.reduce_cooldown(unit, id, ms)` and `ctx.reduce_cooldowns(unit, kind, fraction)`, whose registry entry gains the kind; Kensho's Unbound passes `"basic"`. Tests: each value, a cut that stops at the current tick, and each refusal.
4. **A2. Charges** (actions, [Over time](docs/design/04-capabilities/actions.md#over-time)): `charges`, the recharge timer, a `cooldown_ms` beside them as a lockout, and `ctx.add_charge`. Tests: Night Step's three charges spent, recharged one at a time, given back on a takedown, and capped at `max`.
5. **A3. Range clamp** (actions): `clamp_to_range` on a point aim. Tests: Blink aimed past its range lands at its range, along the line, and one within it lands on the point.
6. **A4. Toggles** (actions): on and off by a cast, the `hold` modifier, `cost_per_attack` and `cost_per_second`, and the ends: an unpaid cost, death and a blocking tag. Tests: Chill Arrows' mana per attack and its end at an empty pool; Dread's mana each second, its hold's interval damage, and its end at a stun.
7. **A5. Channels** (actions): the channel after resolve, its `hold`, `on_channel_tick`, and the interrupts with `on_interrupt`. Tests: Still Mind's ticks and its guard, whole and cut by a move order, by a stun and by death; Tempest's heal ticks.
8. **A6. Charged actions** (actions): charging in place of the windup, release by a second order or at `max_ms`, a move order that keeps it, and `ctx.charge` in every hook of the action. Gale's Cyclone reads `ctx.charge`, not a state its projectile never had. Tests: a release at a third of `max_ms` gives a third; the cap; a cancel spends nothing.
9. **V1. Reveals** (vision, [Grid fog of war](docs/design/04-capabilities/vision.md#grid-fog-of-war)): `ctx.reveal`, its reveals as state, seen from the next Vision stage for their time. Tests: Farsight shows its cells to the caster's group alone and for its time, finds no hidden unit, and restores from a snapshot.
10. **M1. Forced movement** (navigation, [Forced movement](docs/design/04-capabilities/navigation.md#forced-movement)): the forced move as state, in Move before walkers; `ctx.dash`, `ctx.knock_back` and `ctx.teleport`, registered by navigation, and the `move` effects; a teleport's disjoint; a client that continues a forced move. Tests: a dash at a point and at a unit that walks, exact per tick; a knock back off a point and off the unit's own place; a stop at a body that cannot walk and at the bounds; a windup interrupted; an order that waits; a homing projectile a teleport ends and a dash does not; a client that predicts the rest of a dash with no correction.
11. **M2. A dash as a delivery** (navigation, actions): a dash from an instant action's `on_resolve` runs its `on_end` as it ends. Tests: Night Step deals its damage as the dash ends beside its target; Grasping Wraps' pull from `on_hit` runs no `on_end`.
12. **T1. Walls** (navigation, [Data](docs/design/04-capabilities/navigation.md#data)): `[[navigation.walls]]`, read as checked polygons; their cells in each layer's pathing grid by the exact center rule; smoothing and the straight goal test the cells a line crosses. Load errors for a polygon of fewer than three points, edges that cross, or an unknown layer. Tests: the cells of a triangle and of a box, at two cell sizes; a route round a wall, smoothed past its corner and never through it; a map check that refuses a path through a wall.
13. **T2. Brush** (vision): `[[grid.brush]]`, each cell's brush, and the rule that only a unit in a brush reveals its cells; a reveal sees into it. Tests: a hero in brush unseen from outside, seen from inside it and from another unit in it, and by a reveal.
14. **T3. The 3v3's terrain** (reference MOBA): walls between the lanes and the jungle, and brush beside each lane, in the 3v3 map, with its paths and camps still reachable. Tests: the map checks pass; the scripted 3v3's hashes change only where a route or a sight does, which the review checks.
15. **I1. Item types and inventories** (items, [Items](docs/design/04-capabilities/items.md)): `[items.<id>]` with its checks (costs in declared resources, components of the same package and no cycle, a stack of at least 1, its modifiers and action declared), `inventory = { slots, kind }`, and `[shop]`. Load errors for each. Tests: a table of item data, read and refused.
16. **I2. Buying and selling** (items, orders): the orders `buy`, `sell` and `swap`, the shop's regions and the dead, recipes that give up their components, stacks, and the sell share. Tests: each case, hand-computed gold, and each refusal.
17. **I3. Actives and passives** (items, actions, stats): each item's action in its slot's action slot, a consumable's uses, and its modifiers held while carried, once for two of one item. Tests: a potion's heal and its last use, an active's cooldown kept across a swap, and a passive's stats on and off as it is bought and sold.
18. **I4. Wards** (actions): the `spawn` effect of a mode package's action, with `duration_ms`. Tests: a sight ward spawns at its aim, hidden, sees, and despawns at its time; an avatar package's `spawn` still fails the load.
19. **I5. The 3v3's items** (reference MOBA): potions, the two wards, the elixir, and a few items with components and actives, in the mode's package; the heroes' inventories and the shop at each base. Tests: the package loads, and the item checks hold.
20. **S1. The 3v3 casts** (runner): the scripted 3v3's players learn and cast each hero's abilities and use the spells, and buy, use and sell items, beside stage 4's scenario; the match test checks each mechanic by its computed numbers. The 3v3 golden is blessed.
21. **N1. Planned names refused** (package, [One source](docs/design/08-script-api.md#one-source)): the load refuses a planned name in a script, a planned data field and a planned hook, with the name and where it stands. Tests: one flaw each; every reference package loads.
