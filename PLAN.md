# Plan — current slice

The steps of the current slice ([Workflow](AGENTS.md#workflow)), in order, open items only: remove a step when it is done. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. The slice ends when the user plays it in the client. Slice 3 runs first; slice 2 goes on from S1 after it. Slice 4 is a proposal for review, with no place in that order yet.

## Slice 3 — Replication audiences

The user sees their own gold, experience and cooldowns in the HUD, and another team's pools and cooldowns are on no client of theirs; a spectator watches the match; and a mode's `[visibility]` that shows every hero's items to all shows them. The design is [Replication audiences](docs/design/14-replication-audiences.md); each step folds its rules into the design 04 file of each capability it changes, and into design 03's script state.

1. **A2. One kind for each type** (`capabilities`, `net`; D3): `Life`, `SlotTimes`, `UnitState`'s components by `sync`. Check: the state golden changes, the behaviour golden does not.
2. **A3. The mode's visibility and the spectator** (`package`, `net`, `client`; D1, D4): `[visibility]` and its load checks; a spectator's link, which holds no slot, and its rooms; the client's spectator, which predicts nothing. Check: the load refuses each flaw of `[visibility]`; the leak test on the 3v3, with both settings.
3. **A4. Player, team and match entities** (`capabilities`, `net`, `client`; D6, D7, D8): the entities; the player's resources and modifiers, the mode's fields, the relations and the match's end on them; the `Relations` and `MatchEnd` messages gone. Takes the resources that slice 2's S1 planned to send by a message. Check: a player holds its own gold after a kill and a buy, another player's never, its allies' when the mode adds allies to `resources`, and a spectator as the mode sets.
4. **A5. Prediction** (`capabilities`, `net`; D5, D11, D12): `UnitStats` for every unit whose inputs a client holds; the prediction test. Check: a hero walks into an ally's aura, whose numbers come from its source's stats, with no correction; with `modifiers` hidden from enemies, an enemy aura corrects once.

## Slice 2 — Wide bodies, uses, the shop and gravity

The user plays a 3v3 match of the MOBA test content: their hero holds a use on the jungle's shrine, buys items from the shop panel, and casts their actives with 1 to 6, a ward at the ground point; a test map holds a 1,117 m box that walkers go around. The design is [Wide bodies, uses, the shop and gravity](docs/design/13-bodies-uses-shop-gravity.md); each step folds its rules into the design 04 file of its capability.

1. **S1. Names and readers** (`capabilities`, `package`, `packages/test/moba`; D8): `name` on item types and the MOBA's names; a public reader of the shop, an item's price for a unit, whether a unit stands where it can buy, and an action's targeting. A player's resources reach it through slice 3's player entity. Check: a price less held components as computed by hand; an item type with no `name`, or one a locale lacks, fails its load.
2. **S2. The shop panel and item keys** (`client`; D9, D10, D11): Bevy's UI features; the shop panel, the inventory bar, keys 1 to 6, and every cast key aimed as its action takes. Check: the key's target for an action of each targeting; the panel's dimmed items for an avatar alive out of a shop, too poor and with no room; the user buys a ward, places it at a point with its key, sells an item and swaps two slots.
3. **U1. The `use` kind** (`capabilities`, `package`, `client`; D4, D5): the `use` and `uses` sections and their load checks; the `use` order; `damage_interrupts`; `on_channel_tick`'s target; the script API reference generated again. Check: a use resolves on its object; a unit without `uses`, one that `users` refuses and one out of range that cannot walk drop the order; a held use is cut by a new order, a block of `use`, a death and, with `damage_interrupts`, by damage, not by a heal; a second use of the same object keeps its slot, and one of another object fills it afresh.
4. **U2. The shrine** (`packages/test/moba`, `client`; D6): the shrine, its action, `shrine_blessing` and `shrine_spent` in design 07 and the 3v3 package; a right click on a unit the avatar may use orders the use; the channel bar. Check: the scripted match has a hero use the shrine and the team's heroes take the blessing, and a second hero's use cut by a hit; the user holds a use on the shrine in the client.
5. **G1. Gravity** (`capabilities`, `package`, `packages/test/moba`; D12): the falling flight; `gravity` past planned, refused with `homing` or not positive; a grenade of the MOBA's items, which arcs to its point. Check: the points of a flight on each metric at its launch, its peak and its end as computed by hand, the planar one back at its launch height at its end; a spatial one hits a unit its chord meets and not one its straight line would; the client draws the grenade's arc.

## Slice 4 — QUIC transport

The user plays a match on a server over QUIC: the client reaches the server by its address and the TLS key its listing names, and a client that pins another key ends at once, saying so. The design is [QUIC transport](docs/design/15-quic-transport.md); each step folds its rules into designs 02, 05 and 10 where they change. Step Q1 adds `quinn-proto`, `quinn-udp`, `rustls` and `bytes` as direct dependencies of `net`, pinned to the versions the lock holds, once the user approves them.

1. **Q1. The QUIC IO** (`net`; D1 to D5, D7, D8): `net`'s `quic` module, which no binary uses yet: the socket over `quinn-udp`, with its receive buffers from a pool; the server's IO, its endpoint on the server's entity and a `LinkOf` with its connection for each peer; the client's IO; the frame's two systems; the datagram queue that drops its oldest; `LINK_MIN_MTU` and the MTU that follows `max_size`; Retry; the keep-alive and the idle timeout; the TLS configs of a raw Ed25519 key and a verifier of one pinned key; the close that drains. Check, on loopback, each test below 1 s: a handshake, and datagrams both ways; `LINK_MIN_MTU` equals `max_size` on a connection before discovery, and a datagram of that size arrives whole; each accepted connection follows a Retry; a pinned key that is not the server's fails the handshake by `access_denied`; an `Unlink` on either end ends the other's link in the same frames, not by the idle timeout; with a short idle timeout, a peer that stops is lost by it; the receive pool misses nothing after the first frames; a key's encodings equal RFC 8410's vectors.
2. **Q2. The binaries on QUIC** (`protocol`, `net`, `server`, `client`, `lan-check`; D6): `TlsKey` in place of `CertificateHash` in the pin, the client's arguments, `Listening` and the LAN check; the `tls` file as the key's seed, made once; the server's and the client's QUIC IO; WebTransport's plugins, their asserts and the rule of one plugin for each process gone; `wtransport` and Lightyear's `webtransport` feature gone; designs 02, 05 and 10. Check: the `tls` file's test, a key kept over starts and a file cut short or open to others refused; the LAN check passes; no crate of the tree is tokio; the user plays a match on the server over QUIC.
3. **Q3. The link's end** (`net`, `lan-check`; D9): `LinkEnd` from the IO, on the link's entity; `LinkLost` with it, `ChainRewritten` among its cases. Check: each loopback test of Q1 that ends a link asserts its `LinkEnd`; the LAN check's verdict refuses an impostor that ends by another cause; the client logs `WrongServerKey` against a wrong pin.
4. **Q4. The answer bound to the TLS session** (`protocol`, `net`; D10): `ChannelBinding` from each end's exporter, and the fixed one of in-process links; the answer over `"campfire/connect/v2" ‖ challenge ‖ binding`; design 05's step 3. Check: the two ends of a loopback link read the same binding, and two links read different ones; an answer signed over another link's binding is refused; the in-process scripted matches pass as before.
