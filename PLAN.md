# Plan — current slice

The steps of the current slice ([Workflow](AGENTS.md#workflow)), in order, open items only: remove a step when it is done. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. The slice ends when the user plays it in the client.

## Slice 1 — A Zero Hour map on screen

The user imports their install with `campfire-import`, starts `campfire-client --local` on Tournament Desert, and sees its terrain, its tiles, blends and cliffs, and its placed objects drawn with Zero Hour's models at their places and angles, from a camera that pans, zooms and turns as the original's does. Nothing moves, and no rule runs. The design is [Zero Hour](docs/design/12-zero-hour.md), the package rules of [Packages](docs/design/05-protocol-spec.md#packages) and [Game package](docs/design/03-game-scripting.md#game-package), and what the import reads is [Zero Hour research](docs/research/zero-hour.md). A step that changes a golden says why in its commit, with the number that moved.

1. **Z11. The rules package and the install check** (`package`, `capabilities`, `campfire-import`, `packages/zero-hour`, new check `campfire-zero-hour-check`; D7): a mode's `script` as `{ package, path }`, a dependency's script, checked at load as its own are; `source/packages/zero-hour/`, which the imported package depends on, with the mode script of a map that only stands; the imported package as the mode, of one slot, with the fewest capabilities a map that only stands needs; the check in `source/checks/`, which imports the install `GENERALSZH_DATA` names, fails when it names none, prints the fingerprint, checks that Tournament Desert imports to 270 × 340 samples, 580 units of 63 types and 28 markers past its 176 road ends, loads it in a match and plays a tick. Check: a mode whose script is a dependency's loads and runs it, and one that names a dependency it lacks, or a path that dependency lacks, fails its load with what it named; the check passes on the Mac and on the Ubuntu host with the same fingerprint; the user runs the client on Tournament Desert and sees the slice's end.

## Slice 2 — Wide bodies, uses, the shop and gravity

It starts once slice 1 ends. The user plays a 3v3 match of the MOBA test content: their hero holds a use on the jungle's shrine, buys items from the shop panel, and casts their actives with 1 to 6, a ward at the ground point; a test map holds a 1,117 m box that walkers go around. The design is [Wide bodies, uses, the shop and gravity](docs/design/13-bodies-uses-shop-gravity.md); each step folds its rules into the design 04 file of its capability.

1. **B1. The 2,048 m bound** (`math`, `capabilities`; D1, D2): `U256` division rounded up and with remainder by a `u128`; `Shape::MAX_BOUND` at 2,048 m and `BodyBox::MAX_DIAGONAL` at 4,094 m; `Collider::reaching`, `outward` and `SquaredDistance`'s order as D2 gives them; every stated bound derived again. Check: each `U256` operation against hand-computed values at 2²⁵⁵ and at 1; `reaching` and `outward` against a scan of every `k` at small sizes, and at the bound for a 2,048 m walker on a 4,094 m box's corner and edge and two 2,048 m circles on one spot and a bit apart, with the count of `k` tried at most 2; two distances of each kind compared both ways at their largest; the navigation benches within their noise of before.
2. **B2. Levels by size** (`capabilities`; D3): `BodyGrid`, `BodyIndex` and the broadphase as hierarchical grids. Check: each finds the same bodies and pairs as a scan of every body, for bodies of 0.35 m to 2,048 m mixed; a 1,117 × 100 m box covers at most four cells of its level; a bench case of the 3v3 with that box, its worst tick beside the case without it; a test map with the box, where a walker routes around it and collides with it.
3. **S1. Resources, names and readers** (`capabilities`, `net`, `package`, `packages/test/moba`; D7, D8): the player's row of resources to its player after each tick that changes it; `name` on item types and the MOBA's names; a public reader of the shop, an item's price for a unit, whether a unit stands where it can buy, and an action's targeting. Check: a player receives its own gold after a kill and a buy, and another player's never; a price less held components as computed by hand; an item type with no `name`, or one a locale lacks, fails its load.
4. **S2. The shop panel and item keys** (`client`; D9, D10, D11): Bevy's UI features; the shop panel, the inventory bar, keys 1 to 6, and every cast key aimed as its action takes. Check: the key's target for an action of each targeting; the panel's dimmed items for an avatar alive out of a shop, too poor and with no room; the user buys a ward, places it at a point with its key, sells an item and swaps two slots.
5. **U1. The `use` kind** (`capabilities`, `package`, `client`; D4, D5): the `use` and `uses` sections and their load checks; the `use` order; `damage_interrupts`; `on_channel_tick`'s target; the script API reference generated again. Check: a use resolves on its object; a unit without `uses`, one that `users` refuses and one out of range that cannot walk drop the order; a held use is cut by a new order, a block of `use`, a death and, with `damage_interrupts`, by damage, not by a heal; a second use of the same object keeps its slot, and one of another object fills it afresh.
6. **U2. The shrine** (`packages/test/moba`, `client`; D6): the shrine, its action, `shrine_blessing` and `shrine_spent` in design 07 and the 3v3 package; a right click on a unit the avatar may use orders the use; the channel bar. Check: the scripted match has a hero use the shrine and the team's heroes take the blessing, and a second hero's use cut by a hit; the user holds a use on the shrine in the client.
7. **G1. Gravity** (`capabilities`, `package`, `packages/test/moba`; D12): the falling flight; `gravity` past planned, refused with `homing` or not positive; a grenade of the MOBA's items, which arcs to its point. Check: the points of a flight on each metric at its launch, its peak and its end as computed by hand, the planar one back at its launch height at its end; a spatial one hits a unit its chord meets and not one its straight line would; the client draws the grenade's arc.
