# Plan — next steps

Open items only, in order. Remove an item when it is done. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. Stage context: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md); design: [design/](design/).

1. **CI on three platforms**: a GitHub Actions workflow runs the check chain (`fmt`, `clippy`, `test`) on Linux x86_64, Windows x86_64 and macOS aarch64, with the Linux packages Bevy's window backends need. The Linux job also runs `campfire-lan-check` and keeps the session log and its hash as an artifact; the Windows and macOS jobs verify that log with their own `campfire-verifier` and compare the hash. This closes stage 0, and the golden and prototype tests then show equal hashes on every platform, as stage 2 asks.
2. **Prototype gate numbers**: run the `chain_head_signature` and `rollback` benches in the `bench` profile, and write into design 02 the cost of the Schnorr checks for one packet, one frame with a rollback and one without, each against the 33 ms tick at 30 Hz, and the worst case over the scenario match. State whether decision 1 holds. This closes stage 2.
3. **An ability in the test lane**: the walker's first ability becomes a Rhai script, a short-range damage hit with a cooldown and a mana cost. A match scenario casts it through the delayed links: the cast takes effect in its stamp tick, the target's health and the caster's mana and cooldown agree on the server and both clients, the replayed log gives the same hashes, and each client makes the rollbacks the scenario states.
4. **Attack and cast orders from the player**: a right click on an enemy unit orders an attack, and the keys Q, W, E and R cast the abilities of those slots at the cursor. `OrderScript` reads `attack` and `cast` orders, so bots, match scenarios and the LAN check play them too. Test: scripted attack and cast orders land in their stamp ticks in a scenario and in the LAN check.
5. **A readable match**: each unit shows its health, the own hero shows its mana and its ability cooldowns, and an attack or a cast shows where it lands; plain shapes, no art. Nothing in the view changes the sim.
6. **Vertical slice playtest**: a 1v1 on LAN between two machines with items 3 to 5, and the log verified on a third OS. Write down what makes the match fun or not, and which items stage 3 still needs. This closes stage 3, or names what is missing.
