## Stage 6: Sessions

The design is [Sessions](docs/design/10-sessions.md): a journal that makes the log durable, crash restore, slots and their controllers, reconnects and late joins, server bots, receipts, checkpoints by a copy of the changed state, saves and loads, and the local server. Each step updates the design documents it changes: design 05's log format and inputs, design 02's lifecycle, design 08 and its generated reference for each name it makes run.

2. **K2. Keep the TLS identity** (server; [Crash restore](docs/design/10-sessions.md#crash-restore)): the server keeps its TLS certificate and key in its data directory, made when missing and made again at a start when the certificate would expire within two days. Tests: two server starts in turn on one data directory give one certificate hash. Waits on a question: [K2](PLAN_QUESTIONS.md#k2-keep-the-tls-identity).
14. **H2. Restore equivalence** (runner, sim, capabilities; [Checkpoints](docs/design/10-sessions.md#checkpoints)): `Runner::resume`, a match built from packages, a snapshot and a log carry; every gap the test finds closed as state, or as derived data rebuilt after a restore. Tests: the proving match, the lane match and the 3v3, each snapshot at three ticks, a death and a respawn between them, resume and play to the same per-tick hashes as the run that made them.
15. **H3. The checkpoint thread** (runner, server; [Checkpoints](docs/design/10-sessions.md#checkpoints)): a checkpoint at a tick boundary builds the delta, which the checkpoint thread applies, snapshots, hashes and writes, syncs, then signs its record; the journal's `CheckpointBegun` and `CheckpointDone`; the next segment's seed from its boundary; a checkpoint past the seed chain's last segment ends the session with an `aborted` result; a restore from the latest done checkpoint, a checkpoint begun but not done taken again as the replay passes its boundary. Tests: a session checkpointed at ticks 30 and 60 restores from the second to the same hashes, and one cut between the second's begin and done restores from the first and takes the second again, and its log verifies with its snapshots; the work record's worst 3v3 tick with a checkpoint every 100 ticks against none, in the tick bench.
16. **O1. The local server** (client, net; [Local server](docs/design/10-sessions.md#local-server)): `campfire-client --local`, with the server's bot and open slots as its options, starts the server as a thread linked in process, its data in the client's data directory; the pause key and the speed keys. Tests: an in-process local match runs no sim tick while paused and resumes at the same tick; at speed 2 it runs twice the ticks a second of its clock.
17. **V1. Saves and loads** (capabilities, runner, net, client; [Saves and loads](docs/design/10-sessions.md#saves-and-loads)): `[saves]` with `by`, an enum `SaveBy`, and `autosave_ms`; `ctx.save()` runs; a checkpoint at the end of the tick the mode asks in, at each autosave, and at a player's save on the local server unless `by` is `mode`; a load of a checkpoint as a new segment, logged `Loaded`, that every client resumes from; the save and quick-load keys. Tests: the proving match saves by `ctx.save()` and by autosave in their ticks; a load drops the log after the save, and the published log's segments verify; a player's save is refused under `by = "mode"`.
18. **X1. The LAN check** (lan-check; [Tests](docs/design/10-sessions.md#tests)): the check kills the server mid-match, starts it again on its data directory, stops one bot client and starts it again with its key file, and passes when every order either took effect as the rules say or was logged as discarded at a resume, and the published log verifies to the server's final hash; and it plays a match of a client bot against server bots on a local server with no network, whose log verifies. CI runs both on every OS.
