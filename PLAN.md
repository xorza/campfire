# Plan — next steps

The concrete steps of the current stages of the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), in order, open items only: remove a step when it is done, and a section when it is empty. Each step ends with the check chain passing for the crates it touches, and a stop for review. A later stage's steps come here when it starts, after its design step ([Workflow](AGENTS.md#workflow)).

## Fixes

The open issues that have no stage and that should not wait: defects that are quietly wrong, that weaken a secret or a rule of the game, or that break a rule of the code. They come before R8: F2 and F3 because they move matches, and R9 records the skirmish's golden. Each fix that moves a golden says in its commit which golden moved and by what.

1. **F2. Exact parts** (navigation): `Collider::part` moves two overlapping circles apart along the line of their centres by their whole overlap and no more; the floor root's shortfall no longer scales the move. Check: the issue's case, two centres 1 bit apart along each axis, parts to the sum of the radii, where today it parts by √2 times the overlap; a sweep of overlaps and directions, with squared distances that are and are not perfect squares, parts each pair to the sum of the radii within the rounding of the two shifts, computed by hand in a comment.
2. **F3. Orders at hidden units** (control): an order whose unit target its player's vision group does not see as the order applies is ignored, as [Orders](docs/design/04-capabilities/control.md#orders) says; in a match with no `vision`, every unit counts as seen. Check: the proving match's script gains an attack order at a unit its player's group does not see, and its assertion reads in that tick that the order did nothing; a later order at the same unit, once seen, takes effect.
3. **F4. Change ticks past the wrap** (sim): `StateChanges` keeps the change tick of its last copy within Bevy's maximum change age, as Bevy's check of the world's change ticks keeps every component's. Check: a copy whose last tick is older than the maximum age when Bevy checks the ticks still takes a value changed after it, which today it misses; the test sets the ticks, so it runs under 1 s.
4. **F5. A restored server's waiting bot payloads** (net): `BotDriver::new` rebuilds, for each bot, the payloads its script made before the tick the server runs on from that the log does not hold, and logs them in the next ticks, as [Server bots](docs/design/10-sessions.md#server-bots) logs the payloads past a tick's max inputs. Check: a restore test with a bot whose payload waits past a tick's max inputs at the stop logs the same inputs from the stop on as the same match with no stop, and both logs end at the same hash.
5. **F6. Secret files by type** (store, net, server): each secret file's path, `server.nsec` and `tls` from the server's data directory and a key file from a command line, is a secret path that `SecretFile` alone reads and writes, and that gives no `&Path`, so no `fs::read` of a secret compiles; `ServerTls::open` reads `tls` through it, as [Storage](docs/design/02-engine-core.md#storage) says. Clippy's `disallowed-methods` cannot hold this rule: it bans writes, and reads of packages and logs are allowed everywhere, so only a type tells a secret from them. Check: on Unix, a `tls` file that others may read is refused with its own error case, and one of mode 0o600 loads.
6. **F7. The verifier's errors** (verifier): the binary's `verify` returns an error enum, one variant for each failure a caller can tell apart: a package store that does not scan, a log that does not read, a log that does not decode, a replay that does not start, a snapshot that does not check. Check: a test reaches each variant and asserts it.
7. **F8. The 3v3 test's time** (runner): measure where the 8 s of `a_3v3_match_replays_to_the_same_hashes` go: the match, its replay and its copy check. Check: the runner's issue log holds a Decide item with each part's share, and the options that bring the test under 1 s, each with its cost.
8. **F10. Frame and input times in the LAN check's logs** (net, server, client): the server and the client log at Debug each frame's start and the tick it runs, each input's send with its stamp, and each input's receipt; the LAN check keeps them, so the next failure of an order that misses its stamp tick shows which process waited, as the Ubuntu failure of run 37523961750 could not. Check: a local run's logs hold, for each order, its send, its receipt and the tick it took effect in, and the frames between them.
9. **F11. A receipt after a leave** (net): a client that leaves keeps its chain until its link closes, and takes a receipt that comes after its leave, as [Receipts](docs/design/10-sessions.md#receipts) says. Check: a net scenario whose server sends a receipt of the client's last inputs after the client's leave reaches it before the link closes; the client keeps that receipt, as the newest, and logs no warning; a receipt after the link closed reaches no client.

## Stage 7 — RTS skirmish

The design is [RTS skirmish](docs/design/11-rts-skirmish.md), with the rules in [Space and map](docs/design/04-capabilities/00-overview.md#space-and-map), [Navigation](docs/design/04-capabilities/navigation.md), [Production](docs/design/04-capabilities/production.md) and [Control](docs/design/04-capabilities/control.md#orders). A step that adds a script name writes the [reference](docs/design/08-script-api-reference.md) again from the registry. A step that changes a golden says why in its commit, with the number that moved.

1. **R8. The skirmish mode** (test packages; D9, D13): `packages/test/modes/skirmish`, its manifest of three teams, the neutral one of no slots, and two layers; its faction's eight unit types of design 11's table, their actions, a worker's gather and its three builds in each style, the trains, the weapons, each time a multiple of 50 ms and each speed exact over 20 ticks a second; `[supply]`; its map of the wall with its gap, the start markers, the west's three nodes, one at 30°, the east's lone node and the brush, each scene's part of the map open; its script, which spawns each player's town hall, four workers and first minerals, and ends the match when a player owns no living `building`. Check: the package loads and passes the map's checks; after tick 0, each player owns its town hall and four workers at its marker, holds its minerals, and uses four of four supply, and each node stands at its place and angle; the reference does not change, as the mode adds no script name.
2. **R9. The skirmish match** (runner; D12, D13): `SkirmishMatch` in the runner's harness, whose script names units by role, as `ProvingMatch`'s does, and sends each scene's orders on a tick or after the event that ends the scene before; the observer, which reads each tick into typed events; the test, which plays the 13 scenes of [The skirmish match](docs/design/11-rts-skirmish.md#the-skirmish-match) and asserts the whole timeline against the expected one, each tick computed by hand in a comment; its golden `skirmish`; its log replayed to the same hashes; its copy check; no failed script call. Check: the timeline equals the expected one; the match ends in its last scene, west the winner; an expected event moved by one tick fails with that event named; the test runs under 1 s.
3. **R10. The sweeps** (runner; D14): the skirmish's four checkpoints; the archetype-shuffle test, the two snapshot tests and the resume from each checkpoint as sweeps over the proving match and the skirmish. Check: each sweep passes for both matches; the scene 8 snapshot holds a queue of five, an `alone` site and two rally points; the suite stays under 30 s.
4. **R11. The logs on every platform** (verifier, CI; D10): the verifier's headless test plays the skirmish and replays its log's file in process and through the binary, with its checkpoints' snapshots, and keeps the log and the snapshots under `CAMPFIRE_MATCH_LOGS` when it is set; CI's test step sets it and keeps the directory as `match-logs-<os>`, and cross-verify runs `campfire-verifier` on each platform's log with its snapshots. Check: the test verifies locally on every run; a CI run on Linux, Windows and macOS where each platform verifies all three logs and the match plays to its golden.
5. **R12. The skirmish's cost** (runner's bench, docs; D11): `server_tick/first_skirmish`, `worst_skirmish` and `mean_skirmish`, and `server_stage/worst_skirmish_<stage>` and `mean_skirmish_<stage>`, which play `SkirmishMatch`. Check: the cases run once in CI's bench step; design 11's Cost holds the mean and the worst tick, production's Cost the Act and Mode stages and navigation's the Move and Collide stages, measured on one core of the Ryzen 7 6800U; the roadmap's Stage 7 loses the summons, production's first cut and the skirmish.
