## Slice 2 — Wide bodies, uses, the shop and gravity

The user plays a 3v3 match of the MOBA test content: their hero holds a use on the jungle's shrine, buys items from the shop panel, and casts their actives with 1 to 6, a ward at the ground point; a test map holds a 1,117 m box that walkers go around. The design is [Wide bodies, uses, the shop and gravity](docs/design/13-bodies-uses-shop-gravity.md); each step folds its rules into the design 04 file of its capability.

1. **S1. Names and readers** (`capabilities`, `package`, `packages/test/moba`; D8): `name` on item types and the MOBA's names; a public reader of the shop, an item's price for a unit, whether a unit stands where it can buy, and an action's targeting. A player's resources reach it on its player entity, `PlayerResources::of_player`. Check: a price less held components as computed by hand; an item type with no `name`, or one a locale lacks, fails its load.
2. **S2. The shop panel and item keys** (`client`; D9, D10, D11): Bevy's UI features; the shop panel, the inventory bar, keys 1 to 6, and every cast key aimed as its action takes. Check: the key's target for an action of each targeting; the panel's dimmed items for an avatar alive out of a shop, too poor and with no room; the user buys a ward, places it at a point with its key, sells an item and swaps two slots.
3. **U1. The `use` kind** (`capabilities`, `package`, `client`; D4, D5): the `use` and `uses` sections and their load checks; the `use` order; `damage_interrupts`; `on_channel_tick`'s target; the script API reference generated again. Check: a use resolves on its object; a unit without `uses`, one that `users` refuses and one out of range that cannot walk drop the order; a held use is cut by a new order, a block of `use`, a death and, with `damage_interrupts`, by damage, not by a heal; a second use of the same object keeps its slot, and one of another object fills it afresh.
4. **U2. The shrine** (`packages/test/moba`, `client`; D6): the shrine, its action, `shrine_blessing` and `shrine_spent` in design 07 and the 3v3 package; a right click on a unit the avatar may use orders the use; the channel bar. Check: the scripted match has a hero use the shrine and the team's heroes take the blessing, and a second hero's use cut by a hit; the user holds a use on the shrine in the client.
5. **G1. Gravity** (`capabilities`, `package`, `packages/test/moba`; D12): the falling flight; `gravity` past planned, refused with `homing` or not positive; a grenade of the MOBA's items, which arcs to its point. Check: the points of a flight on each metric at its launch, its peak and its end as computed by hand, the planar one back at its launch height at its end; a spatial one hits a unit its chord meets and not one its straight line would; the client draws the grenade's arc.
